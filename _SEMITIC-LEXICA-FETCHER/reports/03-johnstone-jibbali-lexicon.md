## Access class and what the tool does

`johnstone-jibbali-1981` → **offline**. `cli status` runs an archive.org title search, the HathiTrust record probe and the Google Books probe, so that a newly digitised lending copy would show up. Nothing is downloaded. A scan you make yourself from a purchased/borrowed copy can be split with the `johnstone-jibbali` profile.

## How to obtain it

1. Second-hand: search ISBN 0197136028 on AbeBooks, viaLibri, Amazon marketplaces (US/UK/AE/SG listings exist; typical price US$150–400).
2. Inter-library loan (WorldCat shows dozens of holdings, e.g. Oman Library at MEI, Princeton, SOAS). Most libraries will scan a research portion (a chapter-equivalent) on request.
3. Reverse access without the book: buy the Mehri Lexicon e-book (report 02) — its *Index of the English definitions in the Jibbāli Lexicon* maps every English gloss to the JL page and headword.

## Structure of the work (for parsing)

Same family as the Mehri Lexicon: introduction (phonology, morphology), then root-ordered lexicon with capital root headings and transliterated lemmas with glosses, verb forms, plurals, Mehri/Arabic comparisons. Profile `johnstone-jibbali` mirrors `johnstone-mehri`.

## Value for the perush project

Jibbāli is the most conservative continental MSA language in some respects (lateral fricatives, archaic vocabulary); its forms appear as *Jib.* in comparative entries.
