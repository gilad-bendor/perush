# 05 · Leslau, *Lexique soqoṭri (sudarabique moderne)* (1938)

## Bibliographic identity

- Wolf Leslau (1906–2006), *Lexique soqoṭri (sudarabique moderne) avec comparaisons et explications étymologiques*. Collection linguistique publiée par la Société de Linguistique de Paris, XLI. Paris: Librairie C. Klincksieck, 1938. viii + 501 pp. (Original price 130 F.)
- Standard abbreviation: LS (StarLing, SED, SLOnline: *LS* + page).
- Nature of the work: a Soqoṭri–French dictionary compiled from the texts of the Vienna Südarabische Expedition (Müller 1902, 1905, 1907; Bittner 1913–1918), with comparisons to Mehri, Šḫauri (Jibbāli), Arabic, Ethiopic, Hebrew and other Semitic languages. Called "an outstanding monument of Semitic lexicography" by the SLOnline editors.

## Editions and identifiers

- Single edition, 1938. No reprint, facsimile, e-book or translation was found (Klincksieck still exists but lists no reprint; no Forgotten-Books/Inktank reprint either — those exist only for the public-domain Vienna volumes).
- Library identifiers: held widely (WorldCat); reviews: *Syria* 19/4 (1938) on Persée (`syria_0039-7946_1938_num_19_4_8373`), a 1939 review on Persée (`mefao_0253-164x_1939_num_22_1_1347`), BSOAS 1939 (Cambridge Core `S0041977X00135438`).

## Copyright status

- EU/Israel: life + 70 → protected until 31 Dec 2076.
- USA: published abroad in 1938, restored by the URAA (in copyright in France on 1 Jan 1996) → 95 years from publication → public domain on 1 Jan 2034. HathiTrust and Google can therefore only offer search-only/snippet access in the US until then.

## Access class and what the tool does

`leslau-soqotri-1938` → **offline**. `cli status` runs the archive.org, Google Books and HathiTrust probes so a future digitisation is noticed, and prints the HathiTrust full-text-search URL. If you obtain a scan, OCR it (the transliteration needs a Unicode-capable OCR such as Tesseract with `fra` + `script/Latin`) and run `cli pdf-split leslau scan.pdf`.

## How to obtain it

1. **Rare-book market**: set alerts for "Lexique soqotri" on viaLibri, AbeBooks, ZVAB, Livre Rare Book, edition-originale. Copies are rare; expect several hundred € when one appears.
2. **Library reproduction / document delivery** (lawful for a research portion): National Library of Israel (scan-on-demand service), BnF reproduction service, Subito (German libraries, fast), British Library on demand. Combine with HathiTrust full-text search (`babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/ls?q1=<word>;anyall1=phrase`) which reports page numbers where a term occurs, so you can order exactly the pages you need.
3. **Inter-library loan** of the volume through any academic library.
4. **Reconstruct instead of obtain**: much of Leslau's content is now superseded or re-edited — see Substitutes.

## Structure of the work (for parsing a scan)

Headword in Leslau's transliteration, then French gloss(es), then the comparative block introduced by language abbreviations (mh., šḫ., ar., hb., éth., aram. …), then references to the Vienna texts by volume and page. Profile `leslau`: headword + separator + French gloss.

## Value for the perush project

Soqoṭri is the most archaic MSA language; Leslau's comparisons connect Soqoṭri lexemes directly to Hebrew and other Semitic roots — exactly the etymological layer `ניתוחים-לשוניים/` needs.

## Substitutes (in order of usefulness)

1. **Soqotri Lexicon Online** (soqotri-lexicon.ru; Naumkin, Kogan, Bulakh et al.): ≈220 finished entries, each citing LS by page — report 07.
2. **Corpus of Soqotri Oral Literature** I (Brill 2014, ISBN 978-90-04-27841-7) and II (Brill 2018, ISBN 978-90-04-37619-9): every volume ends with a complete glossary of all attested words; paid (Brill DRM-free PDF; Google Books `PF1hBQAAQBAJ`, `IMuDDwAAQBAJ`); the audio/data set is on Brill Figshare (1379832).
3. **Webonary Soqotri Dictionary** (SIL 2022) — report 08.
4. Kogan & Bulakh, "Soqotri", in *The Semitic Languages*, 2nd ed. (Routledge 2019); Bulakh, *The T-Stems in Soqotri* (Brill 2024); Bulakh & Kogan (eds), *Soqotra, Mahra, and Dhofar* (Brill 2026).
5. **Müller's Soqoṭri-Texte** (1905, 1907; public domain) — Leslau's own source material — report 11.
6. Wiktionary Soqotri (35 lemmas) — report 10; StarLing/SED *LS* citations — report 06.
