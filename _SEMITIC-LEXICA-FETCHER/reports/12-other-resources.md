# 12 · Other resources (paid, archival, or minor)

## Comparative MSA lexicography (paid)

- **Morris, Watson & Eades, *A Comparative Cultural Glossary across the Modern South Arabian Language Family*** (Oxford University Press for the British Academy, JSS Supplement, April 2019; ISBN 978-0-19-884738-0). 345 head terms in Mehri, Ḥarsūsi, Baṭḥari, Śḥerɛ̄t (Jibbāli), Soqoṭri, Hobyōt. Paperback ≈ £50; ResearchGate 328289978 (author page). White Rose Research Online lists Watson's outputs (a 2023 chapter "Developing resources for Modern South Arabian languages", Routledge, has an open PDF at eprints.whiterose.ac.uk 198550).
- **Naumkin & Kogan (eds), *Corpus of Soqotri Oral Literature*** I (Brill 2014) and II (Brill 2018), each with a full glossary. Brill DRM-free PDF; dataset on Brill Figshare 1379832.
- **Militarev & Kogan, *Semitic Etymological Dictionary*** I–II (AOAT 278/1–2, Ugarit-Verlag 2000, 2005) — the print form of the StarLing database (report 06).
- **Stroomer**, *Mehri Texts from Oman* (1999) and *Ḥarsūsi Texts from Oman* (2004), both "based on the field materials of T. M. Johnstone" (Harrassowitz, Semitica Viva).
- **Watson**, *The Structure of Mehri* (2012); **Sima**, *Mehri-Texte aus der jemenitischen Šarqīyah* (2009).

## Ugaritic (paid or partial)

- **Tropper**, *Kleines Wörterbuch des Ugaritischen* (Harrassowitz 2008) + Halayqa's open supplement (UF 41; Birzeit FADA PDF — fetched by `halayqa-supplement-tropper`).
- **Huehnergard**, *Ugaritic Vocabulary in Syllabic Transcription* (HSS 32, 1987; rev. 2008 with 32-page appendix; out of print) — 2008 *Additions and Corrections* on Academia.edu 234596.
- **Ugaritic Data Bank / *The Texts of the Ugaritic Data Bank*** (Cunchillos, Vita, Zamora; Laboratorio de Hermeneumática, CSIC, 2003; Gorgias Press 4-vol. set; Accordance and Logos modules). Texts, not a lexicon; no free online version found.
- **Bordreuil & Pardee, *A Manual of Ugaritic*** (Eisenbrauns 2009) — glossary; Logos module.
- Lending scans on archive.org: Gordon, *Ugaritic Textbook* (1965); Aistleitner, *WUS* (1963) — browse-only.

## Archives

- **T. M. Johnstone Papers**, Durham University Library, Archives and Special Collections, GB 33 JOH (c. 1960–1983): lexicon slips, grammars, vocabularies and texts for Mehri, Socotri, Ḥarsūsi, Jibbāli, Baṭḥari, Hobyōt; open-reel and cassette recordings (access copies on CD at Durham and the British Library National Sound Archive). Catalogue on Archives Hub (`cd6b9f14-7038-3f62-9572-9906b91cd6a4`). Reading-room access; reproduction on request.
- **ELAR (Endangered Languages Archive)**: Watson et al. deposits `mehri-watson-0307`, `shahri-watson-0308`, Harsusi and Hobyot collections (Leverhulme project 2013–2016, Leeds/St Andrews/Durham). Free registration; some items open, some restricted. Transcribed, translated texts.
- **Leeds MSAL project site** (ahc.leeds.ac.uk/modern-south-arabian-languages): bibliographies 2019 and 2022 (open PDFs), theses (White Rose eTheses: Castagna 2018 Kuria Muria; Boom 2024 *Language and Nature in South Arabia*; al-Fadly Mehri of Qishn), Alfadly's PhD on Mehri morphology.

## Minor online dictionaries

- **Living Dictionaries — Mehri** (livingdictionaries.app/mehri): ≈18 entries; SPA on Firebase; managers can export CSV/PDF/SQLite. Not worth crawling.
- **MCSR — Mehri Language Center for Studies and Research** (Al-Mahrah University, Yemen; mcsr.mhru.edu.ye/en/dictionary/): Mehri vocabulary lists by category; structure not probed; `terms-check`.
- **Google Play "Soqotri Dictionary"** app (org.ipsapps.yemen.sqt…): closed app, not a data source.
- **endangeredlanguages.com "Mehri Lexicon"** file (2025): community word list Mehri–Arabic–English, growing.

## Bibliographic backbones

- Watson & Morris, *Bibliography of the Modern South Arabian languages* (Leeds, 2019; updated 2022; open PDFs at ahc.leeds.ac.uk/download/downloads/id/339 and /id/743) — the complete list of MSA sources with digitisation notes.
- Glottolog (soqo1240 etc.), WALS (Harsusi `hrs`, Soqotri `soq`), Ethnologue (`gdq`, `sqt`), OLAC (language-archives.org/language/sqt) — for further digital deposits.
