# 11 · The Vienna *Südarabische Expedition* corpus (1902–1918) — public domain

## Why it is here

Leslau's *Lexique soqoṭri* is a dictionary **of these texts**; Jahn's volume contains the first Mehri dictionary; Bittner's studies are the first grammars. All authors died before 1956, so the volumes are public domain worldwide and can be fetched in full.

## Access class and what the tool does

`vienna-expedition-pd` → **open**. `cli fetch vienna-expedition-pd`: downloads `_djvu.txt` and PDF for the two known archive.org items, runs three archive.org searches (Müller / Jahn+Bittner / Soqotri-Sprache) and downloads every non-restricted hit, and pulls Jahn 1902 from the Halle DSpace (`/rest/handle/1981185920/39161` → bitstreams). Google Books ids `lhYUAAAAYAAJ`, `pxM7AQAAMAAJ` are recorded for the Volumes API (full-view PD PDFs are downloadable there too, by hand).

## Using it

- OCR quality of the Google scans is mediocre for the transliteration; the Halle scan is better. For Soqotri, Müller's transcription differs from Leslau's — Leslau's introduction explains the correspondence.
- The German glosses in Jahn's Wörterbuch and Müller's translations give a usable Mehri/Soqotri lexicon for free, and every Leslau entry points back to these pages.
