2. Buy: second-hand copies appear regularly and are cheaper than the Mehri/Jibbāli volumes (US$60–200).
3. ILL / library scan of a research portion.

## Structure of the work

Short introduction; root-ordered lexicon in the same format as the later Jibbāli and Mehri volumes (capital root headings; transliterated lemmas; glosses; Mehri comparisons); English–Ḥarsūsi word-list. The `johnstone-mehri` profile can be reused for a self-made scan.

## Value for the perush project

Ḥarsūsi is close to Mehri; its forms mainly confirm Mehri data in comparative entries (*Hrs.*).
