# The school canon – renderings (binding for both projects)

> The corpus-level glossary (guidelines §6.6). It binds `1-העשירייה/` (the Decad) and `2-הרביעייה/` (the Tetrad).
> The Sumerian precedents of the other corpora bind it too: the creation corpus (`../סיפורי-הבריאה-המסופוטמיים/renderings.md`
> §6–§8: *me* `הַ־מֶה`, *ĝarza₂* `פֻּלְחָן`, *ĝiš-ḫur* `צוּרָה`, *za-gin₃* `סַפִּיר`, *mušen* `צִפּוֹר` …), the laments
> (`../קינות-על-ערים/renderings.md`), the temple corpus (`../בניין-המקדש/renderings.md`: royal and building words), the
> netherworld corpus and the Bilgameš poems (`../אפוס-גילגמש/renderings.md` §23). **Grep before fixing any new word**
> (guidelines §5.2 ★): `python3 -I -B ../העולם-התחתון/netherworld_tools.py grep all '<Hebrew>'`, and
> `canon_tools.py rel KEY FROM TO` for whole lines already translated.
> Rows marked *(pre-set)* were fixed by the coordinator before the first line, from the precedents counted in the
> folder's annotated files (2026-11-13); the translators may revise one with a reason in their Proposals.

## 3. Formulas (fixed; the verifier checks them)
- *X za₃-mi₂* (closing doxology) = `X – תְּהִלָּה!` (the line may say more before it).
- *gu₃ … de₂* = `פּוֹנֶה אֶל`; *inim … gi₄* = `מֵשִׁיב … דָּבָר` (GEN; the Bilgameš poems).
- Further formulas and refrains (the Keš hymn's refrain, Šulgi's "I am …", Inana B's "my lady …") are fixed by the
  first translator of each text from the transliteration (`canon_tools.py rel`, `../../tools/sumerian_workbench.py conc`)
  and entered below (§5) by the coordinator.

## 4. Reserved and gated (checked by `canon_tools.py verify`; the corpus-wide Sumerian gates of
`netherworld_tools.py` apply too: `עולם`, `טרם`, `אלהים`, `קדש`, `כבוד`, `ברא`, `יצר`, `תהום`, `רקיע`, `קינה`, `שאול` …;
`שוב` only for *gi₄*, `טוב` only for *dug₃*, `עזב`, `לקח`, `ידע`, `עפר`, `טיט`, `גן`, `היכל`, `בכה`, `עוון` …)

The genre's biblical counterpart is the Psalms (royal and temple hymns, Zion songs): its vocabulary is reserved, or gated
to the Sumerian word that is its real counterpart (guidelines §5.2):
- reserved outright: `הלל` (and its forms), `מזמור`, `סלה`, `משיח` / `משח`, `צדק` / `צדיק` / `צדקה`, `תורה`, `ברית`,
  `חסד`, `גאל`, `צבאות`, `ישועה` / `הושיע`, `סלח`, `כפרה`;
- gated: `תהילה` (*za₃-mi₂*), `זמר` / `זמרה` (*en₃-du*, *nar*, *tigi*, *šir₃*), `בחר` / `בחיר` (*pa₃*), `ישר` /
  `מישרים` (*si sa₂*, *niĝ₂-si-sa₂*), `משפט` / `שפט` (*di*, *eš-bar*, *ku₅*), `רחמים` (*arḫuš*, *ša₃ la₂*), `חטא` /
  `פשע` / `עוון` (*nam-tag*, *ḫul* …), `גיבור` / `גבורה` (*ur-saĝ*, *kalag*, *nir-ĝal₂*, *ĝuruš*, *meš₃*), `דגן`
  (*ezina*, *ašnan*, *še*, Dagan).
- traps: Keš `כֵּשׁ`, Dagan `אִידִּין־דָּגָן`, Nanna `נַנָּה`, Agade `אַגָּדֶה`, Ebiḫ `אֶבִּיחַ` (+ the corpus-wide traps:
  Inana, Utu, Urim, An, Sumer, *me* `הַ־מֶה` …).
