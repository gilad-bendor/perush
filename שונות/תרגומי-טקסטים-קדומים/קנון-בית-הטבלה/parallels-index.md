# Parallels index: the school canon

The parallels of the twelve texts (the Decad's eight, the Tetrad's four), generated by `build_parallels_index.py` from
the annotated **הקבלה** bullets and filed by Bible verse. None of these cultures' texts is a source of the Bible: the
Sumerian school canon was copied c. 1800 BCE and is a shared world of motifs and idioms (the royal and temple hymn, the
divine warrior, the god's word), said so in each row. **Key findings** (Round 8 run 2):
- The Psalms carry 40% of the rows: the royal hymns ↔ Ps 2, 21, 45, 72, 110 (the king begotten, enthroned at the god's
  side, lover of justice, the poor's protector); the temple hymns ↔ the Zion songs and Ps 24 (who may approach the
  holy house: the Keš hymn's "the man who approaches – does not approach", KH 128–131).
- Real lexical contacts are few and recorded as [cognate]: `מֵישָׁרִים` ~ *niĝ₂-si-sa₂* through Akk. *mīšarum* (LB 38,
  Ps 99:4); `נוֹקֵד` ~ *na-gada* (SA; Amos 1:1); the Tigris.
- Phrases that meet the Bible word for word: Inana B 145 "her prayer – she took" ↔ Ps 6:10 `יְהוָה תְּפִלָּתִי יִקָּח`;
  Enlil A 2 "what comes out of his mouth does not change" ↔ Ps 89:35; Lipit-Eštar A 3 "the cedar's young shoot" ↔
  Ezek 17:22; Nisaba "writing the name on the clay of life" (EN) ↔ the book of life (Ps 69:29); the prison's
  door-bolt *si-ĝar* `מסגר` ↔ Ps 142:8, Isa 42:7 (Nungal A).
- Corrected false echoes of the rank-3 English (each recorded at its line): "created by An", "your divine creator",
  "the good shepherd", "justice and righteousness", "holy sceptre", "not yet" (IB 117, 122, 134), "vanished" for
  *saḫar* + *gi₄* "returned to dust" (IB 73 ↔ Gen 3:19).
