## What to look for
- "Had I unknown phrases … not repetition of what has been said, what the ancestors said" – Qoh 1:9–10 `אֵין כָּל
  חָדָשׁ תַּחַת הַשָּׁמֶשׁ`, 12:12 (`עֲשׂוֹת סְפָרִים הַרְבֵּה אֵין קֵץ`): gated (`חדש` only for mꜣw; `הבל` never).
- The dialogue with one's heart (`jb`) – compare the Man and his Ba; Ps 42:6, 12 (the soul addressed).
- The land in trouble, "no one is free from wrong" – a theme shared with Neferti.
