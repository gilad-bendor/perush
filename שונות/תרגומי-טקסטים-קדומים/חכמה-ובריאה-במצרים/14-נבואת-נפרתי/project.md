# Project 14: Neferti (`נבואת נפרתי`; the Egyptian has no word "prophecy")

## Scope (Round 9 run 4, 2026-11-16)
The whole text of "Neferti" on its main witness pPetersburg 1116B verso (18th dynasty, c. 1420 BCE; composed under
Amenemhat I, c. 1940 BCE), KEY `NF`, 71 lines: King Snefru summons a lector-priest to entertain him; Neferti foretells a
land in chaos – the sun veiled, the river dry, Asiatics in the Delta, a son as an enemy – and a king from the south,
Ameny, who will restore Maat. Labels: `renderings.md` §14.1.

## What to look for
- The frame: a king asks for "fine words, choice speeches" – a court entertainment, like Westcar (`5-פפירוס-וסטקר/`).
- The chaos: "I show you the land in calamity; what had not happened has happened" – Isa 19:1–15 (the oracle on
  Egypt: the Nile dries, brother against brother, Isa 19:2, 5–7), Mic 7:6, Amos 8:9, Joel 3:4.
- The saviour king "Ameny, the son of a woman of Ta-Seti", who unites the two crowns: Isa 11:1–9, 9:5–6 – the gates
  keep `משיח` and the root ישע out of the lines: the apparatus compares.
- The "vaticinium ex eventu": the text was written to praise Amenemhat I (say so plainly in the intro).
