## What to look for
- The woman "from outside", unknown in her town: "do not go after her … a deep water whose course is unknown" – Prov
  2:16–19, 5:3–6, 7:5–27 (`אִשָּׁה זָרָה`, `נָכְרִיָּה`): gated; the apparatus decides what is shared.
- Double the bread your mother gave you; she carried you, nursed you three years, took you to school – Ex 20:12, Prov
  1:8, 23:22, 31:1.
- Do not drink beer to excess; do not enter a stranger's house; keep away from the hot man; the god of the house, the
  offerings and the festival; build a house; marry young and have a son.
- The son's reply (AN 22–23): "a teaching cannot change a nature" – and the father's answer: the crooked stick is
  straightened, the lion and the monkey learn – Prov 22:6, 22:15, Jer 13:23.
