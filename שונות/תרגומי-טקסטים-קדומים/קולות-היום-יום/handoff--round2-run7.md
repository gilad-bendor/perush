**§5.2, lessons (one line each):**
- ★ **Write the transliteration as `` `@` `` and let the tool fill it from the base text**: retyped lines differed from the base twice in the first texts, never after. [everyday voices]
- ★ **Never write a Bible quotation, even "not as a quotation", before printing the verse**: every such note in this run had to be redone, and one was wrong (`לְכָה נָּא` for `לֶךְ נָא`, Gen 37:14); and write the reference in parentheses after the quotation (`` `…` (Book ch:v) ``), because the checker does not see `` `…`, Book ch:v `` (17 such quotations had gone unchecked). [everyday voices]
- **Anchor every gate at word start *and* end, and test it on the commonest Hebrew words of the genre**: `שבו`, `היינו`, `ברוך`, `מגן`, `לפתיתי`, `שבה` all tripped unanchored or one-letter-prefix gates. [everyday voices]
- **A pre-set formula can be wrong**: the renderings had *ezen nu-tuku* for proverbs 1.152–153 from the literature; the tablets have *giri₁₇-zal-še₃ nu-il₂*. Check every pre-set formula against the transliteration of its first occurrence. [everyday voices]
- **One Sumerian word, its Akkadian equivalent's Hebrew – unless the Sumerian is wider**: *ḫi-li* (= *kuzbu* `עֲגָבִים`) describes a mother's charm in 5.5.1, so it took `חֵן`; record such splits. [everyday voices]
