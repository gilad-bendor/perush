## Parallels to watch (look for; never "translate as")
- **Ex 12:13, 23** (*ha-mashḥit*, the night, the door): Išum the night-walker who guides lad and girl safely (21–22), "Išum is a door: before them (the Seven) it is barred" (27); Anunnaki / *gallû* rising (175–177). The root שחת is reserved (renderings §12.5).
- **Deut 32:22–25** (and 32:33, 42): the Seven's commissions (32–38: fire, lion, weapons, wind, "venom of the viper"): *imtu* `חֵמָה` and *bašmu* `פֶּתֶן` are cognates of Deut 32:24, 33; arrows (*uṣṣu* `חֵץ`), sword (*namṣaru* `חֶרֶב`).
- **Deut 31:17–18** (*hester panim*): Marduk's absence from his seat (123, 132–133, 170–178, 181–189) – the god who leaves his seat, and what follows; `הסתיר` is reserved.
- **Gen 6:1–4**: the Seven, warrior gods born of Anu by Earth (28–29); the seven sages sent down to the Apsû (147, 162; Gen 4:20–22, 6:4).
- **The flood** (Gen 6–9): Marduk's flood (132–146; the flood references' parallels index already lists most); the noise of mankind (41, 73, 82; Atra-ḫasīs; Gen 6:5, 11).
- The sleeping god (15–20; Ps 44:24, 78:65, 121:4; 1 Kgs 18:27); the litany "let X hear and tremble" (62–74; Ex 15:14–16); Išum's plea (102–103; Ex 32:12; Gen 50:20); the sharpened sword (88–91; Ezek 21:14–16); "spare no one" (37; Ezek 9:5–6); naming (29; Gen 5:2); giving orders, *ṭēma šakānu* (31; Ezra's `שָׂם טְעֵם`); the guardians at the gate (189; Gen 4:7, 3:24).
- **The soul of the ancients:** the god of war who cannot rest; the soldier's ethos against the city (47–59); the gods' need for quiet; the cult statue and its craftsmen (the god's "body", *šīr ilī*); the netherworld and heaven sewn together (*šipīt šamê u erṣeti*).

## Open decisions
1. (for the lead) *imtu* = `חֵמָה` [cognate] here, against Enūma eliš and Maqlû (`אֶרֶס`): the cognate rule; recorded in the report as a cross-corpus split.
2. **Tool: the `חמה` gate trips on `מלחמה`** (*tāḫāzu* = `מִלְחָמָה`, §12.3): line 6 `עשות מלחמה` is reported (prefix `מל` + `חמה`); 50 `המלחמה` and 97 `מ[לחמ]ה` escape only by the article and the brackets. The gate needs an exception for `מלחמה` / `מלחמת` (guidelines: widen the allow-list, do not bend the Hebrew). This is the only `verify ER` problem in 1–99.
3. ***ša* opening a topic** ("of X – its Y …", the resumptive suffix): rendered `של X – …` at 23, 41, 55, 71, 90; the fixed line 137 has `ואשר לבעלי הנפש` for the same construction. `verify` requires `השבעה` at 23, so `אשר לַשִּׁבְעָה` cannot be written. Either change 137 to `ושל בעלי הנפש – תולדתם התמעטה, …` (one construction, one Hebrew) or accept the split; recommended: change 137.
4. ***šaḫātu* = `נִרְתַּע`** (25, 26, 63; part 2: 119, 121), not §12.3's `נִבְהַל`: Gilgameš I 180, 188 (`אל תירתעי`, `לא נרתעה`) and Ludlul II 108 (`נרתע`) have *šaḫātu* `נרתע`, while `נבהל` is *parādu*'s in Ludlul I 46, the Theodicy 147 and Etana III b+14. Exod 15:15 `נִבְהֲלוּ` stays an [equivalent] in the apparatus (63).
5. ***ṭūdu* = `נָתִיב`** (96), not §12.3's `שְׁבִיל`: *kibsu* has `שביל` in Etana and Gilgameš; the other road words are taken (*urḫu* `דרך`, *ḫarrānu* `אורח`, *girru* `מסילה`, *padānu* `משעול`). (`נתיב` is Enūma eliš's *ḫarrānu* and LH's *urḫu*, both against the road rule of Etana / Gilgameš.)
6. ***ulmu* = `מַגְזֵרָה`** (5), because `גַּרְזֶן` is Gilgameš's *ḫaṣṣinnu* and `קַרְדֹּם` is *pāšu* (fixed line 156).
7. ***abātu* N = `נֶהֱרַס`** (35, 69): *abātu* is perhaps the cognate of `אָבַד` (t/d), but `אִבֵּד` is *ḫalāqu* D's (§12.3; 70, 103, 146). Kept as §12.3 has it; if the lead accepts the cognacy, the cognate rule would move `אבד` to *abātu* and *ḫalāqu* D to another verb.
8. ***nādu* "waterskin" = `נֹאד`** (58), its real cognate; the Descent of Ištar 98–99 has `נֹאד` for *ḫalziqqu* (renderings §5). By the cognate rule *nādu* keeps `נאד`; the lead may move *ḫalziqqu* (e.g. `חֵמֶת`, Gen 21:14) or record the split.
9. ***malku* = `מֶלֶךְ`** (65), its cognate (a West-Semitic word in Akkadian), sharing `מלך` with *šarru* (1, 28, 126…): a split recorded, not removed.
10. **37 `ולא תחמול`**, not §12.3's `ואל תחמול`: *lā* + present is `לֹא` + future (renderings §0, Gilgameš); *ē* + preterite is `אַל` (32 `יריב אל יהי לך`). `וְלֹא תַחְמֹל` (Deut 13:9) is the same form; Ezek 9:5 stays an [equivalent].
11. **27 `לפני[הם] נעול`** (masculine), not §12.3's `נְעוּלָה`: *edil* is masculine, its subject Išum (the door, *daltu*, would take *edlet*).
12. **29 `ו"הַשִּׁבְעָה" קרא את זכרם`**, not §12.3's `קרא את שמם`: the Akkadian has *zikru* (`זֵכֶר`, kept apart from *šumu* `שֵׁם` by §12.3's own row); *sebetta* is written {d}IMIN.BI, the divine name, so `הַשִּׁבְעָה`.
13. **93 = 191: `דבר X אשר אמר(ו) – … יָטַב עליו.`** – the preterite *iṭīb* as the past `יָטַב` (vocalized), not §12.4's future `ייטבו`; *amāt* is singular (`דבר`). 191 should read `דבר אֶרָּה אשר אמר – יָטַב עליו.`
14. **57 *kamānu* = `כַּוָּן`** [cognate; the Akkadian loan of Jer 7:18, 44:19], vocalized, with a footnote. The alternative is a plain `עֻגָה` (1 Kgs 19:6 `עֻגַת`), easier but hiding the loan. Recommended: keep `כַּוָּן`.
15. **13–14**: 13 `(אם)` for the asyndetic conditional (stative + *-ma*, eBL; prophecy-corpus practice), 14 the oath `אִם` (= "not", as Num 14:23). A reader of the clean translation needs the footnote of 14.
16. **36 *ḫiāṭu*** `*חפש*` (the dictionary sense, marked uncertain) against Jiménez's "overwhelm" (Stol): keep, or follow eBL.
17. **renderings §12.2 / §12.1 write *erkallu* `אֵירְכַּלָּה`**, but §0, §3, §5, the Descent of Ištar, Gilgameš and NE have `אִירְכַּלָּה`; the fixed line 135 has `אֵירְכַּלָּה`. One of the two should go (part 2 only; not in 1–99).
18. **190 / 191 and the optional suffix *(-šu)***: 190 *išmē(šū)-ma* `שמע אותו הנשיא מרדוך,` translates the bracketed *-šu* (the header rule: words in the normalization's parentheses are translated unmarked, as (u), (ina šulmi), (qurādu erra) in part 1); 191 *iqbû(šu)* keeps the coordinator's `דבר אֶרָּה אשר אמר – יָטַב עליו.` without `לו`, so that it equals 93. Either add `לו` at 191 or drop `אותו` at 190; recommended: leave as is (190 follows 92/163, 191 follows 93).
19. ***emqu* = `חָכָם`** (108; corpus §5) shares `חכם` with *apkallu* (162 `החכמים`): a split recorded.
20. ***ṣubātu* = `בגד`** in the fixed line 141 and in 181 (which repeats it), against the corpus's *ṣubātu* `שִׂמְלָה` (§0): kept in this text so that 181 echoes 141; a split recorded.
21. ***adi ulla***: 135 (fixed) `לעד` "for ever", 182 `עד אז` "until then" – one phrase, two senses by context.
22. **Uncertain renderings**: 112 *šubû* "pit-trap" `*פַּחַת*` (eBL's lemma *šubtu* "seat" is a slip; Jer 48:43); 113 *magšaru* `*כֵּילַף*` (all four axe words are taken); 117 *bibbu* transliterated `בִּבּוּ` (wild sheep / the plague-star; Gilgameš VIII 177); 118 *ramû* `שוכן` (with `ב־` for the accusative of place); 174 *eṭû* D `[תַּעֲלִיט?]` (`החשיך` is *ekēlu*'s in fixed line 140); 177 *mêsu* `ידרסו` (`רמס` is *raḫāṣu*'s).
23. **170 `(אם)`** for the asyndetic conditional (present + *-ma*), as 13.
24. **Words in this text with two Hebrew forms by Hebrew grammar only**: *elû* G `אֶעֱלֶה` (183) / Š `אַעֲלֶה` (166–167), vocalized.
