# Project 3: Nergal and Ereškigal (Akkadian)

## Scope
The whole myth as eBL `L/1/6` edits it, in two chapters, translated complete:
- **MB** – the Middle Babylonian version from **Tell el-Amarna** (AmaMB1a = BM E29865, AmaMB1b = VAT 1611; 14th c. BCE, a school text of the Egyptian court): 87 eBL labels (`1`–`17`, `a+1`–`a+44`, `b+1`–`b+26`), labelled here **`MB.1` … `MB.b+26`**. The short version: the gods' banquet, Namtar sent up for Ereškigal's portion, the god who did not rise, Nergal sent down with fourteen demons, posts them at the fourteen gates, seizes Ereškigal by the hair to behead her; she offers him marriage, kingship and the tablet of wisdom; he kisses her and wipes her tears.
- **SB** – the Standard Babylonian version (Sultantepe/Ḫuzirina NA, Uruk Hellenistic, Babylon LB): 333 eBL labels (`a+1`–`a+51`, `b+1`–`b+50`, `c+1`–`c+65`, `d+1`–`d+115`, `e+1`–`e+51`, `f+1`). The long version: Kakka goes down the stairway of heaven; Ereškigal sends Namtar up; Nergal does not kneel; Ea makes him a throne and gives him the taboos of the netherworld; Nergal (Erra) goes down, keeps the taboos, then breaks the last one; seven days in bed; he escapes up the stairway; Ea disguises him (bald, squinting, lame); Ereškigal's lament and ultimatum ("I will raise the dead"); Namtar searches the gods twice; Nergal goes down again, seizes her, seven more days; Anu's decree: he stays below for ever.
**Total: 420 entries.** Order in the files: MB first (`annotated-MB.rtl.md`), then SB (`annotated-SB-1.rtl.md` a+1–c+65, `annotated-SB-2.rtl.md` d+1–f+1).

## Comparisons (look for; never force – renderings §11)
Gen 28:12, 17 (the stairway of heaven, the messengers going up and down, "the gate of heaven"); Gen 32:27 ("let me go, for the dawn has risen"); Gen 40:1 (the cupbearer and the baker); Gen 18:4 (water for the feet); Esth 3:2, 5:9 (the one who does not kneel / rise); 2 Kgs 4:26 (`הֲשָׁלוֹם`); Gen 1:27 (*zikar u sinniš*); 1 Sam 28 and Deut 18:11 (the dead raised); Isa 14:9–15 (the netherworld rising; going down to the pit); Hos 2:4, 18 (the marriage formula); Lev 21:18–20, 2 Kgs 2:23 (the defects); Lev 12, 15 (impurity); Isa 25:8 (tears wiped); 2 Kgs 17:30 (Nergal of Cuthah); Song 8:6, Ps 139:8.
**The ancient mind:** hospitality and its taboos (do not eat the food of the dead – cf. Persephone); the court etiquette of the gods (rising before an envoy); a goddess who never knew "the play of maidens"; desire as the force that binds a god to death; kingship of the dead by marriage; the pantheon's politics (Anu, Enlil, Ea; Ea's tricks); the demons of disease as a god's retinue; Amarna as a school text in Egypt.

## Open decisions
(numbered; the translators add)
1. **Kakka's name across corpora** (SB a+1 …): renderings §11.2 fixes `כַּכָּה` ★ (vocalized always); the Enūma eliš translation has the same god, with the same eBL normalization *kaka*, as `גַּגָּא` (EE III 2, 3, 67 – the old reading Gaga). The lead should harmonize (EE III 69–70 are the very formula of SB a+23–a+24).
2. **`arkāt simmilat šamāmī`** (a+11, a+48; later d+26, d+73, d+102, e+19): eBL now reads *arkāt* as the plural noun *ariktu* "length(s)" ("all along the stairway of heaven"), not the adjective "long". The fixed formula `בְּמַדְרֵגוֹת הַשָּׁמַיִם הָאֲרֻכּוֹת` (§11.5, checked by `verify`) was kept; the apparatus says so. Option: `לְאֹרֶךְ מַדְרֵגוֹת הַשָּׁמַיִם`.
3. **`ellu` vs `ebbu`** (a+36, a+41 *ilāti ellēti*): *ellu* = `טָהוֹר` by the precedents (Etana MA h+11, Enūma eliš I 12). Renderings §11.3 gives *ebbu* (d+67 *ul ebbēk*) `טָהוֹר` too – two lemmas, one Hebrew word. Proposal: *ebbu* = `צַח` (Gilg. III 99 *ebbeti* `הצחה`), or the coordinator moves *ellu* instead.
4. **`mātu` "land" = `אֶרֶץ`** (a+38, a+43 *ina māti* `בארץ`, Gilgameš X 318, XI 218) while *erṣetu* = `הָאָרֶץ` is the netherworld in this text: "Ninurta, the mighty in the land" can be misread as "in the netherworld". Kept the precedent with a note; c+29 / d+94 (*ilū ša māti*) will meet it again.
5. **Two source words in `בָּא`**: *alāku* + ventive (a+8, a+30 `יבוא`; a+34 `אשר באת אלינו`, after Gilg. IX 49 *ša illikannâši* `הבא אלינו`) and *erēbu* (a+14 `בוא`, a+22 `בא`, after Ištar 15, 25). Both precedents kept; the later copies d+24, d+43, d+46 (*ša illikannâši*) should follow a+34.
6. **`ē taʾīr-ma`** (b+41–b+45; c+56–c+60 *ul iʾīram-ma*): rendered literally `אל תיגש` / `לא ניגש` (*âru* G "go up to, approach"; e+30 *iʾīršī-ma* "he went up to her"), against Foster's and Mitto's idiom "take care not to". If the coordinator prefers the idiom, all ten lines change together.
7. **The stative *našû*** in the taboos: `נושא לך` / `נושאים לך` for a person (b+40, b+42–b+44), `נִשָּׂא לך` for the vessel (b+45 *mesīt šēpī*): one form, two Hebrew forms by subject.
8. **eBL lemma slip** (b+20): *agâ* lemmatized as *agû* I "crown"; the edition's own note reads a carpentry tool ("axe"): rendered `כַּשִּׁיל` (not `כֶּתֶר`, not Jer 10:3's `מַעֲצָד`).
