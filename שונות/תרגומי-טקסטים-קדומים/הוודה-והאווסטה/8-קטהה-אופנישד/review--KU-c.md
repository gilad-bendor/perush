## Decided without change
- **Quotation marks in KU2.** KU1 quotes every speech (Death from 1.9a, closed 3.15b; checked line by line: every mark opens and closes). KU2 has no outer marks: the translator's header says that the adhyāya does not continue the story. The vocatives `גָּאוּטַמָה` (4.15b, 5.6b) and "I will tell you" (5.6a) show Death still speaking, and the KU2 intro says so ("ממשיך את תורתו של מָוֶת"). Wrapping 4.1a–6.17c in one quotation is an interpretive step: **for the coordinator.**
- **5.15a `ומניין האש הזאת?`**: *kutaḥ* is literally "whence", idiomatically "how much less" (the note says so, after Hume). A reader may take it as a real "where from?". I kept the odd word (fidelity); a coordinator could prefer `ומה גם האש הזאת`.
- **1.17b `לעולם`** (*atyantam*): it can be read "to the world" in a text where `עולם` = *loka*. The alternatives collide (`לנצח` ~ `הנצחי` *sanātana*, `לתמיד` ~ `תמידי` *śāśvata*, `לעד` reads `לְעֵד`), and the line end favours "forever". Kept.
- **1.26a `מבלים את זוהר כל החושים`** (`בִּלָּה` "wear out" / "have a good time"): the object decides it. **2.5b `השוטים`**: fits. **2.7a `אינם משיגים אפילו לשמוע`**: stiff, but √*labh* `השיג` is fixed (§11.4).
- **2.1b `ייטב לו`** (*sādhu*): generic Hebrew, not a calque of one verse; `טוב` is licensed for *sādhú* (§7.2).
- **1.18b `חבלי מָוֶת`, 4.2a `חבל מָוֶת הפרוש`**: real shared images, fixed and tagged [equivalent] (§11.4).
- **KU2 5.2a `הטוב`** (*vasu*): licensed (§7.2), though `הַטּוֹב` is also *śreyas* in KU1; a different adhyāya and context.
- Register: even across both adhyāyas; no vav-consecutive left (1.9b fixed).
