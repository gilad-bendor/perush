# Parallels index: rituals of sending away and of purification

Merged by Bible verse from the projects' parallels indexes (`build_parallels_index.py`). Project 1: the Hittite elimination
rituals of Ašḫella (AS), Uḫḫamuwa (UH), Puliša (PU) and Ambazzi (AM, AM2), translated 2026-10-30 (Round 4 run 6 sub 4).

## Key findings (the Hittite elimination rituals)
All the parallels are **typological** unless said otherwise: Hittite never met Israel's scribes. A historical route of the
rite (Kizzuwatna / northern Syria → the Levant) is argued by D. P. Wright (*The Disposal of Impurity*, 1987) and B. Janowski
& G. Wilhelm (1993) for the scapegoat; the notes state it and claim no more.
- **Lev 16:21–22 – the carrier and its burden.** The verb of the carrying is the same act (*peda-* `נשא` ↔ `וְנָשָׂא הַשָּׂעִיר
  עָלָיו`: AS 33, PU 26, 45, AM 40) and the release is *arḫa tarna-* `שִׁלַּח` (AM 41, 87, 168 ↔ Lev 14:7, 16:22). But the
  carriers differ: rams and an adorned woman (AS), a male sheep crowned with five-coloured wool (UH – **not a ram**, as the
  literature says: *UDU.NÍTA*), a war captive and a woman, then a bull and a ewe (PU), a little mouse (AM). The goat of Lev 16 is
  absent; MÁŠ.GAL / UZ₆ appear only as food for the gods.
- **The destination is a land, not a wilderness**: "the enemy's border … a place where *ours* do not arrive" (AS 35–36; the
  literature's "a place they do not know" is not on the tablet), "the road of the enemy" (UH 9), "back to the enemy land" (PU 26,
  45), "the high mountains, the deep valleys, the long roads" (AM 40). ↔ `אֶל אֶרֶץ גְּזֵרָה` (Lev 16:22): defined from "our"
  side, and inhabited – the evil is handed to "the evil people", the enemies (AM 22).
- **The hand-placing (AS 24) ↔ `וְסָמַךְ אַהֲרֹן אֶת שְׁתֵּי יָדָו` (Lev 16:21)**: the lords of the camp put their hands on the
  rams, but there is no confession; the list that follows (AS 38) is of victims (men, cattle, sheep, horses, mules, donkeys),
  not of sins. The Hittite evil (*idālu*) is a substance, never `עָוֹן`.
- **1 Sam 6 – the plague sent back to its god with gifts**: "whichever god made this plague" (F17: AS 17, 26, 81; UH 11, 15; PU
  42) ↔ `הוּא עָשָׂה לָנוּ אֶת הָרָעָה הַגְּדוֹלָה הַזֹּאת` (1 Sam 6:9); the unused animal (AS 88 ↔ 6:7), "whoever finds it"
  (AS 40 ↔ 6:8); the god appeased and turned "in favour" (F18) ↔ 2 Sam 24:16. Ambazzi's mouse is the carrier, while the
  Philistines' golden mice are the plague's image (AM 37 ↔ 1 Sam 6:5): a [divergence].
- **Lev 14 – no bird and no cedar** in the five texts ([absent]); their nearest things are the coloured wool (AS 10, UH 6, PU 32
  ↔ `וּשְׁנִי תוֹלַעַת`; m. Yoma 4:2, 6:6 on the crimson thread) and the released mouse.
- **Lev 16:26 – washing and return**: the hands rinsed in salt water, passage between two fires, "and they come away" (AS 58–67).
- **The fuller's simile** (AM 26–30 and copies; AM2 6–11): the gods are asked to cleanse the evil "as the fullers cleanse the
  stain and it turns white" ↔ Mal 3:2, Ps 51:9, Isa 1:18, Jer 2:22.
- **Words kept out of the translation** (reserved): `שָׂעִיר`, `עֲזָאזֵל`, the root כפר, `מִדְבָּר`, `עָוֹן`, `סָמַךְ`,
  `הִתְוַדָּה`, `גּוֹרָל`, `אֶרֶץ גְּזֵרָה`, `אִישׁ עִתִּי`, `נִרְצָה`, `אָשָׁם`; the apparatus shows where each would have fitted and
  why it does not.
