# Shared renderings: the corpus of sending-away and purification rituals (`טקסי-שילוח-וטיהור/`)

Binding for every project of this folder. Fixed 2026-10-24 by the Round 4 run 6 coordinator, before drafting project 1
(the Hittite elimination rituals of Ašḫella, Uḫḫamuwa, Puliša and Ambazzi). **The Hittite language tables are in
`../חוקים-ובריתות/renderings.md` §7 and §H2–§H9** (names, reserved and gated words, formulas, traps, the law corpus's
animals, purity terms and *šipant-*): they bind these rituals too, and `tools/hittite_tools.py` reads the H-tables of both
files. The translator brief is `../חוקים-ובריתות/translator-brief--hittite.md`.

**The danger specific to this corpus.** Leviticus 14 and 16 are the natural Hebrew for a rite that loads evil on an
animal and sends it away: `שָׂעִיר`, `עֲזָאזֵל`, `כִּפֶּר`, `נָשָׂא עָוֹן`, `שִׁלַּח`, `הַמִּדְבָּרָה`, `אֶרֶץ גְּזֵרָה`,
`סָמַךְ`, `הִתְוַדָּה`, `גּוֹרָל`, `שְׁנִי תוֹלַעַת`, `אֵזֹב`, `צִפֹּר חַיָּה`, `עַל פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה`. A translator who reaches for
them makes Ašḫella sound like Aaron. So the words whose Hittite counterpart exists are **gated** to it (`שִׁלַּח` =
*tarna-*, `נָשָׂא` = *peda-*, `צִפּוֹר` = *MUŠEN*, `אֶרֶז` = *ERIN*, `טִהֵר` = *parkunu-*, `טֻמְאָה` = *papratar*, `מַחֲנֶה` =
*KARAŠ*, `תַּיִשׁ` = *MÁŠ.GAL*, `עַכְבָּר` = *PÉŠ*, `מַגֵּפָה` = *ÚŠ*), and the rest are **reserved** (never in a line):
`שָׂעִיר`, `עֲזָאזֵל`, the root כפר, `מִדְבָּר`, `עָוֹן`, `פֶּשַׁע`, `סָמַךְ`, `הִתְוַדָּה`, `גּוֹרָל`, `תּוֹלַעַת`, `אֵזוֹב`, `זָבַח`.
What is then the same, and what differs (the enemy's land against the wilderness; a woman, a ram, a bull, a mouse
against a goat; the god of the enemy against Azazel), appears in the apparatus as the finding.

## R1a. Checked against the transliteration before the first line (Round 4 run 6 Sub4, 2026-10-30)
Every row of R1 was checked against the five HPM composites (AS, UH, PU, AM, AM2: 481 clauses). Corrections and additions,
**binding for the translator unless it proposes otherwise with a reason**:
- ***tarna-***: "release" (`שִׁלַּח`) only with *arḫa* / *parā*: the mouse let go (AM 41, 87, 168, 214), AS 53. ***anda tarna-***
  "let in, put in" is another act: the wool threads twisted together (AS 10, UH 6), the blood let into the cup (AM 234), "do
  not let the evil in" (AM 227) = **`הִכְנִיס`** (never `שִׁלַּח` there: the gate cannot see the preverb, the translator must).
- ***penna-*** "drive (away)" = **`הִסִּיעַ`** and ***unna-*** "drive (hither)" = **`נָהַג`** (the Laws: HL1 457 `יסיע`, 407 `ינהג`;
  IN 7.1, 19.2): R1's `נָהַג` for *penna-* is corrected. AS 20, 34, 43, 77, 90; UH 9, 18; AM 88 *penna-*; AS 75, 87; UH 5, 12, 27;
  PU 28 *unna-*.
- ***dai-*** "put" = **`שָׂם`** (the Laws and IN, the Sub2 ruling of the law file §7.10; `הִנִּיחַ` belongs to *dala-*). The rituals'
  *dāi* is very often *dai-* "puts" (AM 14, 19, 51, 94, 100, 103–105 …) and sometimes *dā-* "takes" (AS 60, 62; AM 15, 18, 36,
  42): decide each by context and say which in the פירוק. *tiyanzi* / *tianzi* (AS 24, 79) is the 3pl of *dai-*.
- **The hand-placing**: AS 24 *ANA UDU.ŠIR{ḪI.A} EN{MEŠ} KARAŠ QĀTĪ=ŠUNU parā tiyanzi* "the lords of the camp put their hands
  forth on the rams" = `שָׂמִים … אֶת יְדֵיהֶם` (*dai-* + *parā*); `סָמַךְ` is reserved (Lev 16:21): the comparison is the
  apparatus's.
- **The animals as the text has them:** AS's rams are *UDU.ŠIR* `אַיִל` ★ (always vocalized, trap); **Uḫḫamuwa's crowned animal
  is *UDU.NÍTA* `כֶּבֶשׂ`, a male sheep, not a ram** (the plan's "crowned ram" is the literature's); *UDU.'SÍG+MUNUS'* (AS 87–92)
  the ewe `רָחֵל`; *UDU.ÁŠ.MUNUS.GÀR* (PU 28, 47) `כִּבְשָׂה` (HL1); *GU₄.MAḪ* `פַּר`; *MÁŠ.GAL* `תַּיִשׁ`; *MÁŠ.ŠIR* (AM 232)
  licensed for `תַּיִשׁ` too (the translator qualifies it); *UZ₆* (AM 89, 95) `עֵז` ★ (trap); *ŠAḪ* `חֲזִיר`; *PÉŠ.TUR* `עַכְבָּר
  קָטָן`; *UDU* `שֶׂה` (pl. `צֹאן`). There is **no MUŠEN and no cedar (ERIN)** in these five texts: the bird and the cedar of
  Lev 14 are [absent] here (the coloured wool and the "Babylon stone" are their nearest things).
- ***KILĪLU*** "wreath, crown" (UH 7) = `עֲטָרָה`; ***ḫaršanallai-*** "crown, wreathe" (UH 8) = `עִטֵּר`, ptcp. (UH 12, 18)
  `מְעֻטָּר`; ***unuwant-*** "adorned" = `מְקֻשָּׁט` / `מְקֻשֶּׁטֶת` (AS 22, 32; PU 21, 23, 43, 47).
- ***ÚŠ*** and ***ḫenkan*** (UH 15) "plague, death" = `מַגֵּפָה` both; *akkiškittari* / *akkiškizzi* "(people) keep dying"
  (UH 2, AS 105) = `מֵתִים` (a verb: never `מגפה`).
- **The plague's god (F17):** *kuiš=wa (=kan) DINGIR-LUM kī ÚŠ-kan iyat* "whichever god made this plague" (AS 17, 26, 81; UH 11,
  15; PU 42) = **`"אֵיזֶה אֵל אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה אֶת הַמַּגֵּפָה הַזֹּאת`** (*iyan ḫarzi*: `עָשָׂה`, the perfect noted; UH 11 `אֵיזֶה אֵל
  שֶׁל אֶרֶץ הָאוֹיֵב אֲשֶׁר …`; UH 15 `וְאַתָּה, אֵיזֶה אֵל אֲשֶׁר עָשִׂיתָ …`); also AS 16 `"אֵיזֶה אֵל אֲשֶׁר …`. The indefinite

  enmity, of the enemy" = `שֶׁל אוֹיֵב` (the Laws: *kururi KUR-e* `ארץ אויב`); *KUR LÚ.KÚR* = `אֶרֶץ הָאוֹיֵב`.
