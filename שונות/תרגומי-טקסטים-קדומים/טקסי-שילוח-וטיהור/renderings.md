# Shared renderings: the corpus of sending-away and purification rituals (`טקסי-שילוח-וטיהור/`)

Binding for every project of this folder. Fixed 2026-10-24 by the Round 4 run 6 coordinator, before drafting project 1
(the Hittite elimination rituals of Ašḫella, Uḫḫamuwa, Puliša and Ambazzi). **The Hittite language tables are in
`../חוקים-ובריתות/renderings.md` §7 and §H2–§H9** (names, reserved and gated words, formulas, traps, the law corpus's
animals, purity terms and *šipant-*): they bind these rituals too, and `tools/hittite_tools.py` reads the H-tables of both
files. The translator brief is `../חוקים-ובריתות/translator-brief--hittite.md`.

**The danger specific to this corpus.** Leviticus 14 and 16 are the natural Hebrew for a rite that loads evil on an
animal and sends it away: `שָׂעִיר`, `עֲזָאזֵל`, `כִּפֶּר`, `נָשָׂא עָוֹן`, `שִׁלַּח`, `הַמִּדְבָּרָה`, `אֶרֶץ גְּזֵרָה`,
`סָמַךְ`, `הִתְוַדָּה`, `גּוֹרָל`, `שְׁנִי תוֹלַעַת`, `אֵזֹב`, `צִפֹּר חַיָּה`, `עַל פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה`. A translator who reaches for
them makes Ašḫella sound like Aaron. So the words whose Hittite counterpart exists are **gated** to it (`שִׁלַּח` =
*tarna-*, `נָשָׂא` = *peda-*, `צִפּוֹר` = *MUŠEN*, `אֶרֶז` = *ERIN*, `טִהֵר` = *parkunu-*, `טֻמְאָה` = *papratar*, `מַחֲנֶה` =
*KARAŠ*, `תַּיִשׁ` = *MÁŠ.GAL*, `עַכְבָּר` = *PÉŠ*, `מַגֵּפָה` = *ÚŠ*), and the rest are **reserved** (never in a line):
`שָׂעִיר`, `עֲזָאזֵל`, the root כפר, `מִדְבָּר`, `עָוֹן`, `פֶּשַׁע`, `סָמַךְ`, `הִתְוַדָּה`, `גּוֹרָל`, `תּוֹלַעַת`, `אֵזוֹב`, `זָבַח`.
What is then the same, and what differs (the enemy's land against the wilderness; a woman, a ram, a bull, a mouse
against a goat; the god of the enemy against Azazel), appears in the apparatus as the finding.
