## 4. False friends met in this corpus
- *tiāmtu* ↔ `תְּהוֹם`: a real cognate, but the Hebrew word is not a name of a goddess in Genesis; never render the goddess as `תְּהוֹם`.
- *apsû* ↔ `אֶפֶס` ("end"): the sound is close, the words are unrelated (Sumerian *abzu*). Note it once.
- *nabû* "to name" ↔ `נָבָא`: cognate, but not the Hebrew sense "prophesy".
- *šapattu* ↔ `שַׁבָּת`: the 15th day of the month, not a day of rest.
- *bukru* ↔ `בְּכוֹר`: in jB "son, offspring", not specifically the firstborn.
- *pašāḫu* "to rest" has nothing to do with `פֶּסַח`.

### 5.3 Traps of unvocalized Hebrew (string collisions with reserved words)
