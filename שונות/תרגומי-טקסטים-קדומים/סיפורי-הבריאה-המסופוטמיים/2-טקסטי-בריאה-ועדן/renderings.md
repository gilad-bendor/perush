# Renderings: the creation and Eden companions (project glossary)

Binding for the seven texts of this project, on top of the corpus renderings (`../renderings.md`), which inherit the flood corpus's (`../../סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/renderings.md`), and the Sumerian conventions of flood project 3 (`../../סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/3-סיפור-המבול-השומרי--זיוסודרה/renderings.md`).
Echo tags: guidelines §6.4. Section 1–3 were fixed by the coordinator **before drafting** (2026-10-16); translators add rows in §4 (one sub-table per text) and propose changes in `project.md`.
