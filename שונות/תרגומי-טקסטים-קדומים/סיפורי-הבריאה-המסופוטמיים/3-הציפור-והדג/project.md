## Why this text (guidelines §1, both purposes)
- **Genesis 1:20–23, the fifth day, which the commentary is editing now:** a Sumerian text about the creatures of the waters and of the air, created together by Enki in the marsh, each given its place and its *me*; their "natures" set against each other.
- **The ancient mind:** the school debate (*a-da-min₃*) as entertainment and training in rhetoric; insults, boasting and humour; the marsh economy (fowling, fishing, offerings, the temple and the palace of Šulgi); how a Sumerian looked at animals.

## Key findings for the commentary (Gen 1:20–23)
1. **One act for water-creatures and birds (14, 18–19)** – Enki settles "fish and birds" in the pools in one clause, fashions them, and fills the reedbeds with them, fish first, as in Gen 1:20 (`יִשְׁרְצוּ הַמַּיִם … וְעוֹף יְעוֹפֵף`). But both live in **the same habitat**, the marsh; there is no axis from water to sky and no "bird developing out of the swarm": they are siblings and neighbours, and the debate is a quarrel over a shared home (49–50).
2. **"Fill" (*si*, 19)** – the god fills the reedbeds with fish **and** birds; in Gen 1:22 the creatures fill the waters themselves by the blessing, and the birds multiply on the land. The text's other *si* are ironic (88: the bird "fills" the courtyard with droppings; 101: "I will fill your hand" = pay back).
3. **Place, not kind (20–21)** – each creature gets its *ki-gub* "standing place" and its *ĝiš-ḫur* "designs" (= Akk. *uṣurtu*, cognate of יצר). There is **no "kind"** (`לְמִינֵהוּ` [absent]); the order of creation is an order of places and rules.
4. **Fate, not blessing (1, 21, 165)** – "when a good fate was decreed" (*nam dug₃ tar*): the god decrees and teaches; he does not bless. At the end the judge again "makes known the designs and the straight *me*".
5. **The bird named by kind, the flight by a verb (47, 111, 129)** – *mušen* is the kind; *dal* "to fly" is the motion, **reduplicated** (*dal-dal*) exactly like `יְעוֹפֵף` (Polel). An Akkadian gloss (111) renders *dal* by *naprušu*. This supports the commentary's distinction `צִפּוֹר` (kind) / `עוֹף` (motion). The fish mocks the flitting as aimless (47 "your flitting – what is it?").
6. **"I am Bird: flying in heaven, walking on earth" (129)** – the closest verbal contact with `וְעוֹף יְעוֹפֵף עַל הָאָרֶץ עַל פְּנֵי רְקִיעַ הַשָּׁמָיִם`: the bird defined by the two realms, heaven and earth, with the reduplicated verb – but with two motions (flying in heaven, walking on earth), no "firmament", and as the bird's own boast.
7. **The water-creature as half-seen and slipping (58, 66, 117)** – the fish cannot see behind itself, "slips and slips" through the reedbeds out of the bird's sight, and hides in the deep (*engur*): an ancient description that fits the commentary's `רֶמֶשׂ` ("seen only in part") better than its `עוֹף` ("what slipped from the hand"). The swarm (`שֶׁרֶץ`) appears as the fish-spawn heaped like sheaves (119).
8. **Wings bound, wings spread (45–46, 111–113)** – the bird's wings are what the fowler ties (`כָּנָף` as the place where one seizes the bird), and what she spreads over the ruined nest (113 *a₂-ur₂ bur₂*) ↔ `יִפְרֹשׂ כְּנָפָיו` (Deut 32:11).
9. **Thunderbird and underwater being (63, 65, 82, 110–115)** – the bird calls herself the fish's *galla*-demon in his own waters and his "south storm"; in her revenge she becomes Anzu-like ("lion-faced, eagle-taloned"), whirls "like a tempest" in heaven and roars "like the Mistress"; the fish is a heavy fog hugging the bottom. This is the commentary's appendix case 15 in Sumerian dress – but **the mythic monster is the bird**, and the water-creature is not a chaos-monster (`הַתַּנִּינִם` [divergence]).
10. **"Good" for one of the two (168)** – the verdict says the bird's cooing is *dug₃* "good" (the word reserved for `טוֹב`): Gen 1:21 says `כִּי טוֹב` of all together. The bird wins by her **voice** – the "noise" the fish mocked (30, 40, 47–49) is her service in Ekur, at Enlil's and Šulgi's tables (168–174). The voice brings her close to gods and king; the fish is dumb.
11. **The nest law as a reading aid (104–108)** – the fish destroys the nest and eggs precisely when the mother is away feeding her young (↔ Deut 22:6–7 [divergence]; Isa 10:14 [equivalent]).
12. **Nothing of the fifth day's frame** – no numbered day, no evening and morning (Gen 1:23 [absent]); the prologue is "those distant days".

### The ancient mind (for the reader; see the intro)
The school debate as humour (the bird hums a lullaby while the fish rages, 54; insults as a catalogue of marsh and household life); the marsh economy (nets in furrows and orchards, slings, fattening houses, regular offerings of fish and fowl; 32–45, 67–78, 95–99); how a Sumerian "zoology" works (the fish defined by what it lacks, 59; the bird by what it does to people); the temple and the palace (Ekur, Enlil's table, Šulgi); Akkadian glosses in the manuscripts as traces of teaching (102–115, 165–166).

## Open decisions for the user
1. **Settled 2026-10-21** (the lead's cross-corpus harmonization: *mušen* = `צִפּוֹר`, never `עוֹף`, in all corpora; project 2's two lines and KAR 4 rev. 13 changed). **(coordinator, 2026-10-19)** *mušen* = `צִפּוֹר`, not `עוֹף` (corpus renderings §6.1): it keeps the Hebrew word of Gen 1:20 for the Sumerian verb "to fly" (*dal*) and lets the reader test the commentary's reading of `עוֹף` as a mode of motion. Project 2 has `עוֹף` for *mušen* in two lines (Enki and Ninḫursaĝa 18 `עוף השמים`, Song of the Hoe 83 `עוף האל`); recommendation: change them to `צִפּוֹר` for corpus consistency (not done; the user decides). **Translator's note:** this text confirms the decision: *mušen* and *dal* stand side by side in 129 ("I am Bird, flying …").
2. **Who judges (146–167)?** The fish proposes Enki (140) and they go to Eridug (143), but they address "King Šulgi, son of Enlil" (146). ETCSL makes Šulgi the judge and the speaker of 161 and 165–166 ("*Like* Enki … I am wise"), although 166 has no "like" and *{d}en-ki … -ke₄* is ergative, the natural agent of "answered" (167). We translate the forms (166 `אנקי, מלך הָאַפְּסוּ – … בקי אני – / לדג ולציפור משיב:`) and mark no speakers in 158–166. Options: (a) as now (Enki answers and gives the verdict, Šulgi is the king before whom the case is brought and whose table the bird adorns); (b) follow ETCSL and add `(שולגי:)` speaker notes. Recommendation: (a).
3. **Line 24, who frightens whom?** Both nouns carry *-e*; ETCSL reads "Bird frightened Fish". We follow ETCSL with `?`; the alternative ("the Fish of the *engur* frightened Bird from its [nest]") is in the note.
4. ***e₂-gal* = `הֵיכָל`** (40 var., 45A, 73, 173), the Sumerian word behind Akk. *ekallu* and Hebrew `הֵיכָל` (tagged [cognate]), rather than `אַרְמוֹן`. Hebrew readers may hear "temple"; the notes say "palace". Recommendation: keep (the corpus has *ekallu* = `הֵיכָל`).
5. **Three sound-words kept apart:** *za-pa-aĝ₂* = `שָׁאוֹן` (noise), *mu₇* = `הֶמְיָה` / `הָמָה` (cooing; Ezek 7:16), *akkil* = `רִנָּה` (cry). The pun of the verdict depends on `שָׁאוֹן` being the same word in the insults (30, 40, 49) and in the praise (170–171).
6. ***sig₁₀* = `שָׁת`** (102, 106, 109, 114, 149, 153; ↔ Ps 13:3, 84:4), always vocalized; *ki tag* of eggs = `לָאָרֶץ הֵטִיל` (22 = 149). Project 2 (Enki and Ninmaḫ) has *sig₉* = `הֵטִיל`; *sig₉* in this text means "quiet" (103, 105) and is rendered `דְּמָמָה`. No conflict inside this text; a lemma-level split across texts for the coordinator to note.
7. ***galam* = `נוֹכֵל`** (122), although `עָרוּם` (Gen 3:1) is its exact meaning, to keep `עָרוּם` reserved (project 2 renderings §3). Tagged [equivalent] in the index.
8. ***a₂-ur₂* "limbs" = `אֵבֶר`** (113), the biblical word for a pinion (Deut 32:11, Isa 40:31) and the modern word for a limb.
9. **Uncertain lines translated tentatively** (with `?` / italics): 6 (*šu-še₃ la₂* = `*מסר*`), 15 (food = living creatures, after ETCSL), 28 (*bir-ra-bi* = `*בפזיזות*`; ETCSL's "murderously" rejected), 29, 30, 68 (*su-bur₂* "quicksand" vs "slave"), 69, 125, 126 (*šakir₃* = `שַׁכְרוֹנִית`), 130 (*an* = the god An or "heaven"), 144.
10. **Mechanical bracket rule kept even in a certain formula** (121 `[משיבה]`: the root *gi₄-gi₄* is restored). Alternative: no brackets where the formula is certain. Recommendation: keep the rule.
11. **Hebrew-only puns, not exploited, noted:** `דָּג` / `דָּגָן` (97), `צִפּוֹר` / `צִפֹּרֶן` (110).

## Proposals for the corpus renderings
New Sumerian terms that later texts (EWO, other debates) will need; the full list with lines is in `renderings.md`.
- **Marsh and water:** *umaḫ* = `גֶּבֶא` (Isa 30:14); *sug* = `אֲגַם` (kept apart from *ambar* = `בִּצָּה`); *sur₃* = `חָרִיץ` (kept apart from *pa₅* = `תְּעָלָה`); *agargara* = `דְּגִיגִים`; *pu₂-kiri₆* = `פַּרְדֵּס` (Eccl 2:5), *nu-kiri₆* = `גַּנָּן`.
- **Herding:** *unud* = `בּוֹקֵר` (Amos 7:14); *tur₃* = `רֶפֶת`.
- **Sound:** *mu₇* = `הָמָה` / `הֶמְיָה`; *akkil* = `רִנָּה`; *mur ša₄* = `נָהַם`.
- **Verbs:** *sig₁₀* = `שָׁת` (vocalized; `עֵצָה שָׁת` for *sa₂ sig₁₀*); *gir₅* = `חָמַק`; *DU.DU* / *du-du* = `הִתְהַלֵּךְ`; *ad gi₄* = `נִמְלַךְ` (= Akk. *malāku*); *en₃ tar* = `דָּרַשׁ`; *kiĝ₂* (verb) = `בִּקֵּשׁ` (the noun stays `עֲבוֹדָה`); *gul* = `הָרַס`; *bal* = `הָפַךְ`; *dub₂* (outside the insult formula) = `הִרְעִיד`; *la₂* = `מָתַח` / of the neck `הִטָּה`; *keše₂* = `קָשַׁר`; *us₂* = `דָּבַק`; *kar* = `נִמְלַט` / `חָטַף` by sense; *saĝ-e-eš rig₇* = `הֶעֱנִיק בְּמַתָּנָה`; *šu ĝar gi₄* = `הֵשִׁיב גְּמוּל`.
- **Beings and things:** *gal₅-la₂* = `שֵׁד־גַּלָּה` (the netherworld constables; never for Akk. *šēdu*); *piriĝ* = `לָבִיא` (*ur-maḫ* stays `אַרְיֵה`); *ḫu-ri₂-in* = `נֶשֶׁר`; *nu-gig* = `הַגְּבִירָה` (shared); *za-gin₃* = `סַפִּיר`; *nidba* = `קָרְבָּן` (kept apart from *sa₂-dug₄* = `מִנְחָה קְבוּעָה`); *ĝišbun* = `מִשְׁתֶּה`; *banšur* = `שֻׁלְחָן`; *e₂-gal* = `הֵיכָל` [cognate].
- **Qualities:** *me-te* = `עֲדִי`; *ḫe₂-du₇* = `תִּפְאֶרֶת`; *nam-maḫ* = `רוֹמְמוּת` (*maḫ* = `נִשְׂגָּב`); *gal-zu* = `בָּקִי`; *gur₄* = `עָבֶה` (pride); *sag₉* = `נָאֶה`, noun `נוֹי`; *ku₇* = `מָתוֹק`; *zil₂* = `נָעִים`; *gun₃* = `סַסְגּוֹנִי`; *galam* = `נוֹכֵל` (decision 7).
- **Debate formulas confirmed** (corpus §6.3, no change): `X לY משיב/משיבה:`, `שנית – …`, *in … dub₂* = `חֵרֵף` (51, 80, 137), *šag₄-še₃ nu-gid₂* = `אל ליבו לא משך` (53, 83), `ויכוח שנאמר בין X וY:`, *diri(g)* = `עָלָה עַל`, `האב אנקי – תהילה!`; new: *gu₂ ki-še₃ la₂* = `הַטֵּה צַוָּארְךָ אַרְצָה` (79, 136), *ni₂-zu ad li-bi₂-gi₄* = `עם עצמך לא נמלכת` (87, 94).
