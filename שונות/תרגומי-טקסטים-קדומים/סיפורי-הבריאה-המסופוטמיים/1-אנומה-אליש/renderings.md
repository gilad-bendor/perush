# Renderings: Enūma eliš (project level)

Read after `../renderings.md` (the corpus file, which inherits the flood corpus's shared renderings). This file holds what is specific to Enūma eliš: the eleven monsters, the **fixed repeated passages**, the list of shared lines, and the project's own terms.
Tablet translators may add rows to §4 (with the line of first occurrence); they may not change §1–§3 without a note in the Changelog and a fix in every tablet that uses the row.

## 1. Tiāmat's eleven creatures
Set before drafting. They recur in I 141–143 = II 27–29 = III 31–33 = III 89–91, and as a group in IV 115, V 73–75. **None of them is ever `תַּנִּין`**: the comparison with `הַתַּנִּינִם הַגְּדֹלִים` (בראשית א:כא) is made in the apparatus [equivalent].

| # | Akkadian (eBL) | Hebrew | Meaning, notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *mušmaḫḫū* (I 134) | נְחָשִׁים אַדִּירִים | Sum. *muš-maḫ* "exalted serpent". |
| 2 | *ušumgallū* (I 137) | דְּרָקוֹנֵי־עֲנָק | Sum. *ušum-gal* "great dragon/serpent". The element *gal* "great" ↔ `הַתַּנִּינִם הַגְּדֹלִים` [equivalent]: note it. |
| 3 | *bašmu* (I 141) | פֶּתֶן | [cognate] Ugaritic *bṯn*, Hebrew `וְרֹאשׁ פְּתָנִים אַכְזָר` (דברים לב:לג; cf. תהלים נח:ה); perhaps also `בָּשָׁן` (תהלים סח:כג). A horned venomous serpent. |
| 4 | *mušḫuššu* (I 141) | נְחַשׁ־זַעַם | Sum. *muš-ḫuš* "furious serpent": the dragon of Marduk (Ištar Gate). |
| 5 | *laḫmu* (eBL lemma *Laḫamu*, I 141) | שָׂעִיר | "Hairy one": the same word as the god Laḫmu (I 10) and Ea's attendants (flood corpus `שְׂעִירִים`). [equivalent?] ↔ `לַשְּׂעִירִם` (ויקרא יז:ז). |
| 6 | *ugallu* (I 142) | שֵׁד־סְעָרָה | Sum. *u₄-gal* "great storm/day": the lion-headed storm demon. Vocalize `שֵׁד` (not `שַׁד`). |
| 7 | *uridimmu* (I 142) | כֶּלֶב־שׁוֹטֶה | Sum. *ur-idim* "mad dog / mad lion": the lion-man. |
| 8 | *girtablullû* (I 142) | אִישׁ־עַקְרָב | Scorpion-man (Gilg. IX). |
| 9 | *ūmū dabrūtu* (I 143) | סְעָרוֹת אַכְזָרִיּוֹת | *ūmu* "day; storm(-demon)" (flood corpus: `סְעָרָה` when storm). |
| 10 | *kulīlu* (I 143) | אִישׁ־דָּג | Fish-man. |
| 11 | *kusarikku* (I 143) | אִישׁ־שׁוֹר | Bison / bull-man. |

Group words: *nabnītu* "creature(s)" = `מִבְנִים`; *ištēn-ešret* = `אַחַד־עָשָׂר`; *imtu* "venom" = `חֵמָה` [cognate] (construct `חֲמַת`, suffixed `חמתך`, `חמתה`; the free form always vocalized, so that it does not read `חַמָּה` "sun"; 2026-10-30 harmonization, was `אֶרֶס`; ↔ `חֲמַת תַּנִּינִם` Deut 32:33).

Notes for the apparatus (Tablet I gives them in full; the copies refer back to I):
- 132 *ukkinnu* = `מוֹעֵצָה` (≠ *puḫru* `אֲסֵפָה`); *banû* = `בָּנָה` even here ("building strife").
- 133 *patāqu* = `עִצֵּב`; *Ḫubur* = Tiāmat as "mother".
- 134, 145 *lā maḫru* = `שֶׁאֵין לַעֲמֹד בְּפָנָיו`. 135 *attāʾu*: meaning unknown, hence `…`. 135, 144 *lā pādû*: `לֹא חָס` (*padû* "to spare").
- 137 *ušalbiš* = Š of *labāšu* [cognate לבש]. 138 *umtaššil* = D of *mašālu* [cognate: `וְתַמְשִׁלוּנִי` (ישעיהו מו:ה)].
- 139–140 are Tiāmat's words (jussives/precatives), without a speech formula.
- 146 *uštabši* (Št of *bašû*) = `הֵבִיאָה לִכְלַל הֲוָיָה` ↔ `וַיִּבְרָא אֱלֹהִים אֶת הַתַּנִּינִם הַגְּדֹלִים` (א:כא) [equivalent / divergence]: the monsters are brought into being by the chaos mother as weapons against the gods; in Genesis the `תַּנִּינִם` are created by God and blessed (א:כב).
- 149 *ummānu* = `חַיִל` (not `צָבָא`). 154 *qātukka ušmalli* ↔ the Hebrew idiom `וּמִלֵּאתָ אֶת יָדָם` (שמות כח:מא) [equivalent].
- 151 *rab sikkatti* "chief of the pegs" = a military title; 152 *karru* "knob, pommel"; *ina karri* is unclear (eBL "at the command post"): both marked `*…*`.
- 158 *likūn* = `יִכּוֹן` [cognate]; *ṣīt pî* = `מוֹצָא פִּי` [cognate].
- 161 *linīḫḫa* (D of *nâḫu*) = `יָנִיחַ`, "calm, appease" (cf. `וַהֲנִחוֹתִי חֲמָתִי`, יחזקאל ה:יג); *pîkunu* is plural in all manuscripts (eBL: "whatever you [gods] command").
