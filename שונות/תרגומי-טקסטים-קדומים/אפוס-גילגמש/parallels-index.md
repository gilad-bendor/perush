# Parallels index: the Epic of Gilgameš and the Bible

This is the corpus's main product for the commentary (`corpus.md`; guidelines §7.3).
It merges the per-tablet parallels indexes of both projects into one table ordered by Bible verse: Genesis by chapter, then Exodus–Deuteronomy by book, then the rest of the Bible, then rows without a specific verse.
Each row is filed under its first Genesis verse (otherwise its first verse). "Tablet, line": **SB** = the Standard Babylonian epic (`1-הנוסח-הבבלי-התקני/`, apparatus in `annotated-<tablet>.rtl.md`); **OB** = the Old Babylonian tablets (`2-הנוסח-הבבלי-הקדום/`). Gilgameš XI 1–208 (the flood) is indexed in the flood corpus (`../סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/parallels-index.md`), and Gilg. I 1–28 also there (as a flood reference).
The Hebrew rendering is ours, as it stands in the translation. Bible quotations are vocalized and checked with `tools/check_bible_quotes.py`.
**Generated** from the tables in the two `project.md` files by `python3 -I build_parallels_index.py`, which keeps everything above the heading "The table"; the key findings below are written by the corpus coordinator.
