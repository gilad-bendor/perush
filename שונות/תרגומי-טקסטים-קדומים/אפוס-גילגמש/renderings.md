# Shared renderings: the Epic of Gilgameš corpus

Binding for both projects of this corpus (`1-הנוסח-הבבלי-התקני/`, `2-הנוסח-הבבלי-הקדום/`). Read `corpus.md` first.
Written by the corpus coordinator before drafting (2026-10-17); later additions are dated in the Changelog. Tablet translators do **not** edit this file: they write proposals in their tablet's section of the project's `project.md`, and the coordinator merges them here between runs.

Echo tags follow guidelines §6.4: [cognate], [equivalent], [divergence], [absent].

## 0. What binds, in what order
1. **Gilgameš XI 1–208 as translated in the flood corpus** (`../סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/2-גילגמש-XI--סיפור-המבול/annotated.rtl.md` and its `renderings.md`). It is the same poem: every line of I–X, XII and XI 209–328 that repeats a line of XI 1–208 has the identical Hebrew, and every word that XI 1–208 renders keeps its rendering unless §8 below records a deviation.
2. **The flood corpus's shared renderings** (`../סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/renderings.md`).
3. **The creation corpus's shared renderings** (`../סיפורי-הבריאה-המסופוטמיים/renderings.md`, especially §3 and §5: the Gen 1–3 key terms).
4. **The law and treaty corpus's** `renderings.md` for legal, oath and curse terms.
5. **Gilgameš I 1–28** as translated in the flood corpus (`6-אזכורי-המבול/`, §6) is copied verbatim into `annotated-I.rtl.md` (two harmonizations, §8).
Where 1–4 disagree, the higher number yields, and the deviation is recorded in §8.

## 6. Reserved Hebrew (checked by `verify.py`)
Never in a `>` line: `אֱלֹהִים`; `תֵּבָה` (except the compound `תֵּיבַת־הַלּוּחוֹת` for *tupšennu* in I 24, from the flood corpus); `כְּרוּבִים`; `עֵדֶן`; `רָקִיעַ`; `עוֹלָם`; `טֶרֶם`; `פָּתַר` / `פִּתְרוֹן`; `צָבַר`; `נָד` / `נוֹדֵד` (for *rapādu*); `פֶּרֶא`; `הֶבֶל`; `שָׁמַר`; `חֶמְדָּה` / `נֶחְמָד`; `עֵזֶר`; `כְּנֶגְדּוֹ`; `יָצַר`; `בָּרָא`.
Only for their Akkadian counterpart: `נָחָשׁ` (*ṣerru*), `עָפָר` (*eperu*), `טִיט` (*ṭiṭṭu* / *ṭīdu*), ארר (*arāru* / *arratu*), `יָדַע` (*edû*), `שׂים` (*šakānu*), `קָרָא` (*nabû*), `לָקַח` (*leqû*), שוב (*târu*), `עָרוֹם` / `עֵירֹם` (only if an Akkadian word for "naked" occurs; Enkidu is never called naked in the SB text – his "nakedness" is the hair of I 103–105 and the clothing of II 34–35), `עֵץ` (*iṣu*).

## 7. Traps
- **Always vocalize:** `שַׁמְחַת`, `אֲרוּרוּ`, `שַׁכַּן`, `אִט`, `נַעֲשָׂה` / `נַעֲשֶׂה`, and `אוּתוּ` / `אוּטּוּ` if they occur.
- Unvocalized strings that collide: `שמחת` (Šamḫat / "joy of"); `ארורו` (Aruru / "cursed"); `שכן` (Šakkan / "dwelt"); `ענקידו` is not a spelling (always `אנקידו`); `ראם` / `ראם` (the wild bull) vs `ראם` "saw them": vocalize `רְאֵם`.
- *ṣēru* "back" in *eli ṣērīšu* "upon him, before him" is not *ṣēru* "open country".
- *ṭardu* "chased" ~ `טרד` "trouble" in Modern Hebrew: render `נִרְדָּף`.
- *amāru* "see" ≠ `אָמַר`; *nadû* ≠ `נִדָּה`; *ḫarimtu* ~ `חֵרֶם`; *kalû* "all" vs *kalû* "hold back" (`כָּלָא`, flood XI 133b only).
- *ilu* "god" in *kīma ili* is singular (`כְּאֵל`), never `כֵּאלֹהִים`.
- Every `עֵשֶׂב` stands for *šammu*; every `עֵץ` for *iṣu*: grep before inventing a plant word.

## 8. Deviations from the inherited renderings
- *ṣēru*: `שָׂדֶה` (flood XI) instead of Enūma eliš's `עֲרָבָה`: the same poem binds.
- *sakāpu*: `נָם` instead of Enūma eliš's `שָׁכַב`: Gilgameš needs `שָׁכַב` for *nâlu* (X 70).
- *edû*: `יָדַע` everywhere; flood XI 32 `הֵבִין` stays as it is (a different sense, "understand"), noted in the parallels index of XI.
- *saḫāru*: flood XI 150, 153, 156 render it `שָׁב` (the birds "returned"); the creation corpus reserves `שׁוּב` for *târu*. In I–X, XII and XI 209–328, *saḫāru* = `חָזַר` / `חִזֵּר אַחֲרֵי`, *târu* = `שָׁב`. **Open decision for the user:** align flood XI 150/153/156 to `חָזְרָה`.
- *niṣirtu*: flood §6 (I 7, 26) has `סוֹד`; flood XI 9 has `דְּבַר נְצוּרָה` and the creation corpus `נְצוּרָה`. Harmonized: I 7 `את הנצורה ראה`, I 26 `פתח הנצורה שלו` (the flood-§6 copy is changed to match, logged in its project.md).
- *maḫāṣu*: law corpus `מָחַץ` kept.
