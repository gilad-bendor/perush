# Project: the Epic of Gilgameš, Standard Babylonian version (Tablets I–X, XI 209–328, XII) → Hebrew

Read `../../translation-guidelines.md` first, then `../corpus.md`, the shared `../renderings.md` (binding) and `../translator-brief.md`. This file holds what is specific to the SB epic; each tablet has its own section below, written by its translator.

## Scope
- **Text:** the Standard Babylonian *Poem of Gilgameš* ("He who saw the Deep", *ša nagba īmuru*), Tablets I–X and XII complete as far as preserved in eBL, and Tablet XI 209–328.
- **Not here:** XI 1–208 (flood corpus project 2, embedded in the combined translation; see `../corpus.md`). The OB, MB and NA versions are project 2 (OB) or cited in the apparatus (MB/NA: eBL's `Parallels:` field names them).

- eBL `L/1/4`, *Poem of Gilgameš*, Standard Babylonian chapters I–XII: A. R. George (editor), revised by G. Rozzi and E. Jiménez (2026); translation George (with A. A. Fadhil and W. Khatabe for I). Generated by `../../tools/ebl_fetch.py` into `../../sources/eBL/Gilgamesh-SB-<T>/` (`reading-edition.md`, `glossary.md`, `manuscripts.md`, `chapter.atf`, `bibliography.md`; gitignored per guidelines §8).
- **Line numbering:** eBL = George 2003 (with eBL's later additions, e.g. I 208a–b; II a+1′…; X 292′–294′). The annotated entries follow eBL's line list exactly (including lines eBL lists as "[...]" only); `verify.py` checks it.
- Rank-3 check: George's English in eBL (T), consulted only after our own reading; for the OB parallels Streck's translation (SEAL). Rank 6, after drafting: Tchernichovsky 1924 (`../../sources/Ben-Yehuda--Tchernichovsky-1924/full-work--all-tablets.rtl.md`), for register and as a negative model. Heidel 1946 (rank 5) only for the history of interpretation.

- **I 29–91**: Gilgameš two-thirds god (48) and the oppressed young men and brides (65–91) ↔ 6:1–4 (`בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים … וַיִּקְחוּ לָהֶם נָשִׁים מִכֹּל אֲשֶׁר בָּחָרוּ`); "no rival" (63) ↔ `עֵזֶר כְּנֶגְדּוֹ`; Gilgameš "shepherd" (69) ↔ 4:2.
- **I 92–110**: the gods ask Aruru to create an equal for him (93–96 *lū maḫir*) ↔ 2:18, 20; Aruru washes her hands, pinches off **clay** (*ṭīda iktariṣ*: `טִיט קָרְצָה`, Job 33:6) and **throws it down in the wild** (100) ↔ 2:7–8; Enkidu "built" (*banû*, 101) ↔ `וַיִּיצֶר` / `וַיִּבֶן`; hairy, clothed like Šakkan, eating **grass** with the gazelles, drinking with the animals (103–110) ↔ 1:29–30, 2:19–20, 3:18; "he knows no people, no land" (106).
- **I 111–220**: the hunter (*ṣayyādu*) ↔ 10:9, 25:27; Šamḫat; six days and seven nights of love (192); the animals flee him (195–198) ↔ 9:2; "he became weak … but he had reason, wide understanding" (199–200) and "his heart, now **knowing**" (212, *mūdû*) ↔ 3:5–7 `וַיֵּדְעוּ`; **"You are handsome, Enkidu, you have become like a god"** (205) ↔ 3:5 `וִהְיִיתֶם כֵּאלֹהִים`, 3:22; "who do not know life" (231) ↔ 3:22; "I will change the order of things" (220).
- **I 242–298**: Gilgameš's dreams and his mother's interpretation (*pašāru*) ↔ Gen 37, 40–41; the "friend, counsellor" (294) ↔ 2:18.
- **II** (SB, with OB II): bread and beer put before Enkidu, who does not know how to eat (44–51) ↔ 3:6, 3:19; clothing (34–35) ↔ 3:21; the shepherds' camp (37) ↔ 4:2; Ḫumbaba "made by Enlil a terror to the people", "his voice the Deluge" (221–228) ↔ 3:24; "as for man, his days are numbered, whatever he does is wind" (234–235) ↔ Ps 90, Qoh 1:14; the cedar ↔ the Lebanon.
- **III**: Gilgameš "to establish a name" ↔ 11:4, 6:4; Ninsun's prayer; adoption of Enkidu.
- **IV**: five dreams on the road (interpretation); the journey formula.
- **V**: the cedar forest ("seat of the gods") ↔ the garden of God (Ezek 28, 31); Ḫumbaba's seven auras ↔ `לַהַט הַחֶרֶב הַמִּתְהַפֶּכֶת` (3:24); the cedar door.
- **VI**: Ištar's proposal and the list of her lovers (the gardener Išullānu offered dates: food and the garden ↔ Gen 2–3); the Bull of Heaven; **"seven years of chaff … I have stored grain"** (103–111) ↔ Gen 41 (`שֶׁבַע שְׁנֵי רָעָב`, `וְיִקְבְּצוּ`); the river drops seven cubits (118).
- **VII**: the gods' decision that one must die ↔ 3:22–23; the cedar door addressed; **Enkidu curses the hunter and Šamḫat** (94–131, *nazāru*, *arāru*) ↔ 3:14–19 `אָרוּר`; Šamaš's rebuke: "she gave you bread fit for a god, beer fit for a king, clothed you in a great garment" (134–137) ↔ 3:6, 3:21; the blessing (151–161); the dream of the **house of dust**, "where dust is their sustenance and clay their food" (184–192) ↔ 3:14, 3:19.
- **VIII**: the lament (the animals, the rivers, the forest mourn) ↔ 2 Sam 1:19–27; the funerary statue; the offerings.
- **IX**: fear of death ("shall I too die like Enkidu?"); Mount Mašu, the scorpion-people; the path of the sun, twelve double-hours of darkness; the **garden of gem-bearing trees** (172–194) ↔ Gen 2:9–12, Ezek 28:13.
- **X**: Šiduri, Ur-šanabi, the Waters of Death; the threefold question and answer (the lament); Gilgameš in a lion-skin ↔ 3:21; Ūta-napišti's speech on death (266–322): "they established death and life, the day of death they did not reveal" ↔ Deut 30:15, 19; Gen 2:17, 3:22; "we build a nest" (309, *qinnu*) ↔ 6:14.
- **XI 209–328**: the sleep test (seven loaves, 209–246) ↔ 3:22 (sleep as a figure of death); the washing and the new garment ↔ 3:21; **the plant "the old man grows young"** (283–300), dived for in the Apsû; **the snake (*ṣerru*) smells it, takes it and sheds its skin** (305–307) ↔ Gen 3:1–5, 22; "for whom have I toiled? I have not secured any good for myself; I have done good for the lion of the ground" (311–314) ↔ 3:17–19; the return to Uruk's wall (323–328 = I 18–23).
- **XII**: Enkidu goes down to the netherworld for the *pukku* and *mekkû*, breaks the taboos and is held fast; Nergal opens a hole (*takkapu*) and his **ghost** (*utukku*) rises "like a phantom" (*zaqīqu*) ↔ Deut 18:11, 1 Sam 28; the fates of the dead.

## Tablet I

### Scope
SB Tablet I, all 300 eBL labels (1–298 with 208a–b): the prologue (1–28, copied from the flood corpus), the hymn to Gilgameš (29–46), his birth and body (47–60), his oppression of Uruk and the complaint to the gods (61–91), the creation of Enkidu by Aruru (92–110), the hunter (111–175), Šamḫat and Enkidu (176–212), Enkidu's challenge (213–221), Šamḫat's description of Uruk and Gilgameš (222–242) and the two dreams with Ninsun's interpretation (243–296), and the close (297–298). Seventeen manuscripts (Nineveh NinNA1–3, Kalḫu KalNA1, Babylon BabNB1–5, Uruk UrkNB1 and the commentary quotation UrkNBQuo1); well preserved from 92 on, broken in 1–28, 51–90 and a few single lines (78, 134, 137, 139, 144). 208a–b exist only in UrkNB1 and are tagged `{נוסח אורוך}`.

### Sources consulted
- eBL `L/1/4` SB I (`../../sources/eBL/Gilgamesh-SB-I/`): `reading-edition.md` (N, T, L, notes, parallels, ATF), `glossary.md` (stems), `manuscripts.md`.
- eBL OB II (`../../sources/eBL/Gilgamesh-OB-II/reading-edition.md`) for the OB parallels of 192, 204–208a, 243–296 (cited in the apparatus where they change something).
- George's translation (T in eBL), after drafting; Tchernichovsky 1924 (`../../sources/Ben-Yehuda--Tchernichovsky-1924/full-work--all-tablets.rtl.md`, Tablet I) after the third pass, for register and as a negative model.
- The binding renderings: `../renderings.md`; flood corpus XI 1–208 and its renderings; creation corpus `renderings.md` §3, §5; law corpus (*ramû* D, *kullumu*, *lētu*, *mutu*); the format models (flood XI annotated; Enūma eliš VI annotated).

### Decisions
Renderings fixed in this tablet (beyond `../renderings.md`), with the rejected alternatives:
1. **I 1–28** unchanged (`verify.py`: 0 differences with the flood copy). Section headings split into 1–10, 11–23, 24–28; two apparatus additions marked "(תוספת, לוח I)" (11, 27).
2. **Repeated passages fixed at the first copy** and copied: 63–69 → 80–85; 74–75 → 89–90; 76 → 91; 108–110 → 173–175; 121–131 → 148–158; 138–143 → 160–164 (143 → 184); 179–185 → 187–191 (imperative → preterite); 209–210 → 216–217; 245–256 → 259–265, 277–283; 257–258 → 284–285; 266 → 289; 122 → 149, 267, 290 (and 221's second half); 123 → 135, 150, 268, 291.
3. **Quotation marks**: direct speech in `"…"`, speech inside Šamḫat's long speech (222–296: Gilgameš's dreams, Ninsun's answers, 293–295) and inside Enkidu's (219) in `'…'`, as in flood XI 39. The marks are the only difference in several repeated lines (see "Lines shared").
4. *eṣēru* D (49) = `צָרָה` (vocalized; not `יָצְרָה`); *ṣalmu* (49) = `צֶלֶם` [cognate] – the line now carries both words of Gen 1:27 and 2:7, which the commentary should see.
5. *maḫāru* "be equal" (95) = `יְהִי שָׁוֶה`; Št "make equal" (256, 264, 283, 288) = `הִשְׁוָה … עִם`; Št "come face to face" (112, 113) = `פָּגַשׁ פָּנִים אֶל פָּנִים` (the same stem in another sense; noted). Never `כְּנֶגְדּוֹ` / `עֵזֶר`.
6. *šubšû* (79) = `הֵבִיא לִכְלַל הֲוָיָה` (creation corpus §3), tagged ↔ `וַיִּבְרָא`.
7. *ūru* "pudenda" (179, 187) = `עֶרְוָה` (meaning; Lev 18, Gen 9:22), with the root link to `עֵירֹם` (עור) and `מְעוֹר` (Hab 2:15) in the apparatus. Rejected: `מָעוֹר` (exact cognate, but obscure); `עֵירֹם` (an adjective "naked", not the noun; and Enkidu is never "naked" in SB). Open (below).
8. *napīšu* "breath, scent" (180, 188) = `נְשִׁימָה` (kept apart from *napištu* = `נֶפֶשׁ`); tagged [equivalent] ↔ `נִשְׁמַת חַיִּים`.
9. *šārtu* (body hair) = `שֵׂעָר`, *šuʾʾuru* = `שָׂעִיר` [cognate] (103); *pērtu* (head hair) = `מַחְלָפוֹת` (Judg 16:13); *itqu* = `קְוֻצּוֹת`; *ḫanābu* = `לִבְלֵב` (not `צָמַח`, reserved for Gen 2:5, 9).
10. *lalû* = `עֹנֶג` in both 59 (Gilgameš's prime) and 193 (Šamḫat's delights), per `../renderings.md` §4; rejected `עֶדְנָה` (Gen 18:12, the right sense in 193, but the root of `עֵדֶן`).
11. *šipru* (183, 190) = `מְלָאכָה`, bound by XI 177 (*kala šipri* `כל מלאכה`); the Gen 2:2 echo is noted as false.
12. *ṭiābu libbu* (110, 171, 175) = `טוֹב לִבּוֹ` [cognate] ↔ `וַיִּיטַב לִבּוֹ` (Ruth 3:7); `טוב` stays reserved for the *ṭiābu/ṭābu* root.
13. *ilittu* (102, 172) = `מוֹלֶדֶת` ("offspring; birthplace"), not `תּוֹלָדָה` (Gen 2:4); *kiṣir Ninurta* (102) = `גוש נינורתה`, with the same `גוּשׁ` as the *kīma kiṣri ša Anim* formula (the link creation – strength – dream is visible).
14. *alāku* + ventive "come" (121, 148, 241, 266, 289) = `בָּא`, by sense; *erēbu* stays `בָּא` (115, 272) per XI. Two Akkadian verbs meet in one Hebrew verb here; see the proposals.
15. *warû* "lead" (138, 160, 165) = `הוֹבִיל`; I 207 *luttarrūka* is read as *warû* Gt (= OB II 56 *lurdēka*) against eBL's lemma *târu*, so `אוֹבִילְךָ`, not `אֲשִׁיבְךָ` (which would create a false echo of `עַד שׁוּבְךָ`).

17. *adāru* I "be gloomy" (65, 82) = `הִתְקַדֵּר` / `הִקְדִּיר`, distinct from *adāru* II "fear" = `חָרַד` (116); *erēpu* (117) = `מְעֻנָּנִים`.

19. *šērtu* (238) = `אַשְׁמָה` (`עָוֹן` is *arnu*, `חֵטְא` is *ḫīṭu* in XI 180).
20. *ṣalālu* (182, 189, 237) = `יָשֵׁן` per `../renderings.md` §5, also for the sexual "lie on" (`ויישן עלייך`); rejected `שָׁכַב` (reserved for *nâlu*: 129 *ušnīlu* = `הִשְׁכַּבְתִּי`).

### Open decisions for the user
1. *ūru* (179, 187): `עֶרְוָה` (chosen) or the exact cognate `מָעוֹר` (`מְעוֹרֵיהֶם`, חבקוק ב:טו), which would make the root link with `עֵירֹם` (ג:ז) visible in the line itself?
2. *kīma ili tabašši* (205): the shared formula is `כְּאֵל הָיִיתָ`; *tabašši* is a present ("you are (becoming) like a god", OB II 53 likewise). Keep the past of the formula, or `כְּאֵל אַתָּה`?
3. *nakāpu* (30 *muttakpu*): `מְנַגֵּחַ` (chosen, the natural word for a goring bull) or `נוֹגֵף` (the law corpus's *nakāpu* = `נָגַף`, cognate of `יִגֹּף`, שמות כא:לה)?

### Proposals for the shared renderings
1. **Names / epithets:** *Uruk ribīti* = `אוּרוּךְ־הַכִּכָּר`; *Rīmat-Ninsun* as in §1; *pukku* = `פּוּכּוּ` (vocalized; XII: *pukku* and *mekkû* `פּוּכּוּ וּמֵכּוּ`?); Nissaba = `נִיסַבָּה`; Lugalbanda, Nudimmud, Bēlet-ilī as used.

### Lessons for the guidelines
- **Check the edition's normalization against the ATF for every bracket, in both directions**: in Tablet I the normalization brackets words a manuscript preserves (150, 285) and leaves unbracketed words broken in all (73), and once has the wrong pronoun against all manuscripts (260 *ṣērīya* for *ṣērīka*).
- **eBL's `Parallels:` field can point to the wrong chapter** (I 254–265 → "SB II" 269–288, which are other lines): compare the normalizations before copying Hebrew from a listed parallel.
- **A homonym hidden by one Hebrew word can be the parallel** (*maḫāru* "stand opposite" and "be equal" ≈ `נֶגֶד`; *uznu* "ear" and "understanding"): note both senses at each occurrence, so the commentary sees the play the Akkadian makes.
- **Vocalize a common word whose unvocalized form creates a false echo next to a key word** (`רֵעַ` after `יודע`, I 212).
