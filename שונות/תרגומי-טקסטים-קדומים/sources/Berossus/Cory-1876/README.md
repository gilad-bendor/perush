## Assessment
Classic 19th-c. English translation (Hodges' edition adds Assyriological notes, e.g. Xisuthrus = "Ḫasis-adra"). Free and quotable, but old and free in places; use only as a secondary aid next to the Greek (DFHG) and modern translations (Burstein 1978; Verbrugghe & Wickersham 1996; De Breucker BNJ 680).
