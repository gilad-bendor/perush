## Content
Cosmogonic prologue (1–20: when An and Enlil had fixed the plans of heaven and earth, Enki organizes the rivers, marshes, sheepfolds, cities and kingship; he fills the marsh with fish and birds and assigns them their places); the quarrel (21–45A: Fish insults Bird first); Bird's reply and boasts (46–?); the alternation of speeches and acts (Fish destroys Bird's nest and young; Bird takes revenge, seizes Fish); the case before Enki; Enki's verdict for **Bird** (Bird is to be in the Ekur, its voice sweet to Enlil's ear); the colophon 188–190 "debate between Fish and Bird … because Bird was superior to Fish: praise be to Father Enki".
