## Usefulness
**Base text** (rank 1, with Mittermayer 2009) of `אגדות-מלכים/1-אנמרכר-ואדון-ארטה/`. The ETCSL English is rank 3: read
it only after forming a reading from the Sumerian; its "may they … address Enlil in a single language" (141–146) and
"shall change the speech" (150–155) decide the Gen 11 question and must be checked against the morphology.
