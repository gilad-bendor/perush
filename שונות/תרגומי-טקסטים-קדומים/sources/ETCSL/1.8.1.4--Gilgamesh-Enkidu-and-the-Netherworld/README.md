## Content
Five recensions in ETCSL's order:
1. **Version A** (Nibru, Urim and elsewhere): lines 1–303 with the pluses 152A, 163A, 287A–D, 293A–B; ETCSL has no lines 280–285 (a gap). The prologue (creation, the division of heaven, earth and netherworld; Enki's voyage, 1–26); the *ḫalub* tree, Inana's garden, the snake, the Anzud bird and the *ki-sikil-lil₂-la₂* (27–135); Bilgames fells the tree and makes the *ellaĝ* and *ekidma* (ball and mallet) (136–171); they fall into the netherworld (172–176); Enkidu's descent, Bilgames's instructions and their breach (177–242); the appeals to Enlil, Enki and Utu; Enkidu's shade comes up (243–252); the interrogation: the fates of the dead by their lives (253–303). Lines 172–303 = the Akkadian **Gilgameš XII** 1–153 (a translation of the Sumerian), already translated in `../../../אפוס-גילגמש/`.
2. **A version from Urim, UET 6 58** (28 lines): a different order of the interrogation.
3. **Another version from Urim, UET 6 59** (segments A 9 and B 11).
4. **A third version from Urim, UET 6 60** (17 lines): Bilgames's return to Unug, the statues of the dead, the libations ("my father and my mother, drink clear water"), the doxology.
5. **A version from Me-Turan** (segments A 24 and B ~71, after Cavigneaux and Al-Rawi 2000): the interrogation in another order, with new fates (the stillborn, the man burnt to death, the leper …).
