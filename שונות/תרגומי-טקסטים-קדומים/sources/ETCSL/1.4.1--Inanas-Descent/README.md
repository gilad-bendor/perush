## Content
412 composite lines + 28 pluses of single manuscripts (13A–13H, 30A–31A, 37A, 129A–B, 174A–B, 175A–B, 221A, 226A, 300A–301A, 305A–C, 319A, 325A, 376A, 379A) = 440 entries. Inana abandons her temples and descends (1–25, with her seven *me* and her garments); her instructions to Ninšubur (26–72); the gatekeeper Neti and Ereškigal (73–127); the seven gates and the stripping (128–164); Inana judged, killed and hung on a hook (165–172); Ninšubur's mourning and the appeals to Enlil, Nanna and Enki (173–221); Enki creates the *kurĝara* and the *galatur* from the dirt under his fingernails and gives them the plant and the water of life (222–253); Ereškigal in labour, the two creatures' sympathy, the corpse revived (254–281); Inana must give a substitute; the *galla* demons (282–305); Ninšubur, Šara and Lulal spared (306–347); Dumuzid found enthroned and handed over (348–367); his prayer to Utu and escape as a snake (368–381); the end of the story, fragmentary: Inana weeps, the fly, the decree – half the year for Dumuzid, half for his sister (382–410); doxology to Ereškigal (411–412).
