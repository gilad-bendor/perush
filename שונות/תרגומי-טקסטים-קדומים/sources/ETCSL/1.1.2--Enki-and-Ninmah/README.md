## Content
The birth of the gods and the gods' toil, digging canals (1–11); Enki asleep in the Engur; Namma wakes him; Enki has the mother goddesses nip off clay from the Abzu and form man to bear the gods' work (12–43); the feast; the contest between Ninmaḫ and Enki: Ninmaḫ forms six defective humans (a man with weak hands, a blind man, a man with paralysed feet, an "idiot", a woman who cannot give birth, a man with no male or female organ), and Enki decrees a fate and a living for each (44–82); Enki forms the Umul ("my day is far off"), whom Ninmaḫ cannot sustain (83–102); the quarrel and its resolution (103–142, damaged).
