**1-2.** Say to Lugal-ibila what Inim-Inana says:

**3-12.** Don't neglect the Sumerian language. For the second time, I am sending you a message in correct language. [note: 1 ms. adds:] ...... is ....... Don't let the children who sit before you go out from the school. However much anyone misleads you and says “I want to go” on account of his father, until you and I agree on a sign and a messenger comes to you, don't let the children out. ...... [note: the ms. which adds l. 4A has instead:] A father entrusts his child's well-being to your ...... . It is urgent.
