## Content
An old farmer (ETCSL: "Ud-ul-uru") instructs his son through the agricultural year: preparing and irrigating the field (2–22), the plough-teams and ploughing (23–45), sowing (46–63), the rites against vermin and the watering of the growing barley (64–73), the harvest (74–90), threshing, winnowing and measuring (91–109); 110–111: "the instructions of Ninurta, son of Enlil; Ninurta, faithful farmer of Enlil, your praise is good".
