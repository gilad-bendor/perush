## Content
**Old Babylonian version** (284 lines: 1–281 with 87A, 87B, 93A): Enlil gives kingship to Sargon; Inana makes Agade prosperous (1–56); "the word from the Ekur" — Inana abandons Agade, the gods withdraw its gifts (57–82); Naram-Suen's dream, seven years of mourning, two unfavourable extispicies (83–99); he mobilizes and demolishes the Ekur (100–148); Enlil brings down the Gutians; famine and death (149–192); the lament of the survivors (193–209); eight gods curse Agade (210–271); "and so it was" (272–280); doxology to Inana (281).
**Fragments of an earlier version from Nibru, Ur III period** (Segments A–F, 37 lines, each numbered from 1).
