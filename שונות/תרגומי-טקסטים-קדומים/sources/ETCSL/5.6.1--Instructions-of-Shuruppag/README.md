## Why it matters here
Šuruppag, son of Ubara-Tutu, gives counsel to his son **Ziusudra** (l. 1–13, repeated at 73–82 and 143–152). Gilgameš XI 23 calls Uta-napišti 'man of Šuruppak, son of Ubara-Tutu' — the Akkadian tradition knows this lineage; an Akkadian version of the Instructions exists (Lambert BWL; Alster 2005). Earliest versions: Fara and Abu Ṣalābīḫ (ED IIIa, c. 2600 BC). Standard edition: Alster, *Wisdom of Ancient Sumer* (2005).
