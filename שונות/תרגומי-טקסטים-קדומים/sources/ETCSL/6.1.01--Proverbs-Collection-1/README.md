## Content
Proverb collection 1 (SP 1 = Gordon 1959; Alster 1997 coll. 1): 194 proverbs in 350 composite lines (ETCSL segments A–?), from about 70 OB manuscripts (Nibru, Urim and others).

## Files
- `c.6.1.01.xml`, `t.6.1.01.xml` — ETCSL TEI source.
- `transliteration.md` (348 numbered lines), `translation.md` — conversions by `../../../tools/etcsl2md.py`.

## Line numbering
ETCSL composite line numbers (= ORACC). For the proverbs, the ETCSL TEI also numbers each proverb (`<lg n="1.9">`, line ids `c6101.A.1.9.10` = proverb 1.9, line 10); the ETCSL translation is one paragraph per proverb. Our files keep both: the line number as the entry label, the proverb number as the unit.
