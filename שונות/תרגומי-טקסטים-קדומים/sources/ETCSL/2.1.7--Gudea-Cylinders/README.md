## Content
Gudea, ruler (*ensi₂*) of Lagaš (c. 2125 BCE), builds the E-ninnu, the temple of Ninĝirsu at Ĝirsu. **Cylinder A** (30 columns, 812 lines kept + 4 lost): Enlil's decision; Gudea's dream; the journey to Nanše, who interprets it; the second dream and the god's own description of his house; extispicy, the purification of the city, the brick mould and the first brick; the materials brought from the mountains (cedar, stone, metals); the building, the stelae, the furnishing; colophon "the middle hymn". **Cylinder B** (24 columns, 488 lines kept, about 59 lost): the god's entry into the finished house, the installation of the divine household (counsellor, gatekeeper, chamberlain, musicians, herdsmen, farm manager, fisheries inspector, …), the seven days of feasting in which the master and the slave are equal, Ninĝirsu and Bau at the banquet, the blessing of Gudea; colophon "the last hymn". A first cylinder (the "cylinder X" before A) is lost; fragments are disputed.
