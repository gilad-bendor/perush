## Content
Enlil separates heaven from earth "to make the seed of the land come up from the earth" (1–7); at Uzumua ("where flesh grew") he puts the first humans' heads into the brick mould and they break through the earth (8–20); the Anuna praise the hoe; the hoe's deeds in building, agriculture and burial, a long chain of puns on *al* "hoe" (21–110).
