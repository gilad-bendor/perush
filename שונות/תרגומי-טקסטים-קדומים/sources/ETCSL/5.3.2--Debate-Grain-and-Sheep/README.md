## Content
The cosmogonic prologue (1–36): when An begot the Anuna on the hill of heaven and earth, there was no grain, no sheep, no weaving, no clothing; mankind "did not know bread … like sheep they ate grass with their mouths"; the gods create Sheep and Grain on the Holy Mound, are not sated, and give them to mankind (26–36); Enki and Enlil send them down to the land (37–42); the two prosper (43–64), drink and quarrel (65–70); the debate (71–181); Enki's and Enlil's verdict for Grain (182–193).
