## Content
Hymnic praise of Enki (1–60); Enki's self-praise and the Anuna's praise (61–139); Enki's journey and blessings of Sumer, Urim, Meluḫḫa, Dilmun, Elam and Marḫaši (140–249); he fills the Tigris with his semen, organizes the waters, the marshes, the sea, the rain, the fields, the hoe and brick-mould, building, the steppe, the sheepfold, the boundaries and weaving, and appoints a deity over each (250–385: Enbilulu, Nanše, Iškur, Enkimdu, Ezina, Kulla, Mušdama, Šakkan, Dumuzi, Utu, Uttu); Inana's complaint that she alone has no domain, and Enki's answer listing her powers (386–471); praise (472).
