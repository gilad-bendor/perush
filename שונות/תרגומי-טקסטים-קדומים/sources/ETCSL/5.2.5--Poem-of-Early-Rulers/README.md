## Why it matters here
The "Ballad of Early Rulers" (Sumerian version from Sippar; bilingual versions known from Emar and Ugarit): "…he who, **like Ziusudra, sought life**?" (l. 10) — Ziusudra as the proverbial man who gained eternal life, alongside Alulu, Etana and Gilgameš. The ETCSL translation page t.5.2.5 was not archived; the translation is in translation.md (from the XML).
