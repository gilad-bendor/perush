## Content
The Hoe, "child of the poor", provokes the Plough (1–19); the Plough boasts of its royal festival, its harvests and the gleaning poor (20–62); the Hoe answers with its year-round work, the dykes, the bricks and houses, the gardens and the wells, and mocks the Plough's four months of work (63–178A); a voice (Storm?) and Enlil end the quarrel (179–193); the Hoe wins: "praise to Nisaba" (194–196).
