## Content
Dilmun, the pure land without illness, old age or predators (1–28); Ninsikila's request and Enki's fresh water brought up from the earth (29–64); Enki's unions with Ninḫursaĝa, Ninnisig, Ninkura and Uttu (65–185); the eight plants that Enki eats and names; Ninḫursaĝa's curse, "until he is dead I shall not look upon him with the eye of life" (186–219); the fox, the return of Ninḫursaĝa, and the eight healing deities born for Enki's eight aching body parts, among them Nin-ti for the rib (220–281); the fates decreed for them (282–305).

## Manuscripts
Composite after Attinger 1984 (ZA 74). Three manuscripts: UET 6/1 1 (Ur, the long "Ur version", with lines the Nippur manuscript lacks), CBS 4561 (Nippur, PBS 10/1 1) and AO 6724 (TCL 16 62). ETCSL's composite marks only some manuscript differences (notes such as "possible insertion point for additional lines in a ms. from Urim" after l. 49, the passage 49A–49M?, and "1 line not in the ms. from Nibru"); the Ur manuscript must be compared line by line with the composite (done in the project's annotated file). The Ur manuscript alone is in ORACC as P346086 (`../../ORACC/epsd2-literary--P346086--Enki-and-Ninhursaga-Ur-UET6-1/`).
