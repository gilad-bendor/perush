## Why it matters here
**The Sumerian Flood Story** ("Eridu Genesis", Jacobsen's name): creation of mankind and animals, kingship descends from heaven, five antediluvian cities (Eridug, Bad-tibira, Larag, Zimbir, Šuruppag), the gods' oath to destroy mankind, Enki's warning to king **Ziusudra** through the wall (Segment C 18–27 — cf. Gilg. XI 20–22 'Reed-fence, reed-fence! Wall, wall!'), the flood of seven days and seven nights, Utu's light entering the boat, sacrifice, and Ziusudra's grant of eternal life in Dilmun (Segment E). Direct Sumerian ancestor of the Atra-ḫasīs/Gilgameš XI tradition. Only one main manuscript (CBS 10673+10867, Nippur, OB; Poebel 1914 = PBS 5 no. 1) — roughly a third of the text survives.
