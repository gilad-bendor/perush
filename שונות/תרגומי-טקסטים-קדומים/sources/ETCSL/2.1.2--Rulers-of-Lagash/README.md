## Why it matters here
Opens with **'After the flood had swept over and brought about the destruction of the countries; when mankind was made to endure, and the seed of mankind was preserved…'** (l. 1–16) — a Lagaš counter-piece to the SKL; useful for the 'seed of mankind' (numun nam-lu₂-ulu₃) phrase also in the Flood Story C 23.
