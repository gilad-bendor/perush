## Why it matters here
In the **Me-Turan version, Segment F** (ETCSL lines F 1–22, 23–37, 116–130): Gilgameš "reached Ziusudra in his dwelling place" and restored rites forgotten since the Flood; Enki recalls to An and Enlil that "after the assembly had made the Flood sweep over to destroy the seed of mankind … **Ziusudra alone, although a human being, remained alive**". The Sumerian background to Gilgameš's journey to Uta-napišti (Gilg. X–XI). Three versions (two from Nibru, one from Me-Turan) are in the composite; current standard: Cavigneaux & Al-Rawi, *Gilgameš et la Mort* (2000), and George, *The Babylonian Gilgamesh Epic* (2003).
