## Content
Enlil begets Summer and Winter on the mountain and decrees their fates (1–25); their works (26–68); their gifts to Enlil's house E-namtila (69–104); the quarrel and the insults (105–287); the appeal to Enlil, who decides for Winter, "the farmer of the gods" (288–309); the reconciliation and the banquet (310–315); colophon (316–318). Set at the Ur III court of Ibbi-Suen (230).
