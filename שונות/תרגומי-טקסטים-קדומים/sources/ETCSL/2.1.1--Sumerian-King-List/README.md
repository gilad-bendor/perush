## Why it matters here
Antediluvian kings with enormous reigns (Eridug: Alulim 28,800 years …), ending (l. 32) with **Šuruppag: Ubara-Tutu**, then l. 39–40 **"Then the flood swept over. After the flood had swept over, and the kingship had descended from heaven, the kingship was in Kiš"** (a-ma-ru ba-ur₃-ra-ta). Parallels Berossus' ten antediluvian kings (Alōros … Otiartes/Ardates → Xisouthros). ETCSL's composite does **not** list Ziusudra as king; he appears in some other king-list sources (e.g. W-B 62, and the Ur version of the Flood Story) — compare Berossus.
