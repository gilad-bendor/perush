# Cassuto 1951 – *האלה ענת* (Hebrew translation of Ugaritic myths, public domain)

- **Title:** משה דוד קאסוטו, *האלה ענת: שירי עלילה כנעניים מתקופת האבות* (Jerusalem: Bialik Institute, 1951; 3rd ed. 1958).
- **Source:** Project Ben-Yehuda, https://benyehuda.org/read/31020 (marked "נחלת הכלל", public domain);
  plain text from `https://benyehuda.org/download/31020?format=txt` (redirects to an S3 link; retry if the connection
  resets – it did once on 2026-10-23).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-23 (Round 4 run 1). **Licence:** public domain (Ben-Yehuda) – committed.
- **What is here:** `cassuto-1951--ha-ela-anat.txt` (9,176 lines, as Ben-Yehuda gives it): the introduction (the
  discovery, the script, the language, the parallel traditions in Israel – "המסורות המקבילות בישראל"), then the texts in
  Hebrew-letter transcription with a facing vocalized Hebrew translation and a commentary.
- **Texts covered:** Virolleaud's *V AB* (= KTU 1.3) and *VI AB* (= KTU 1.1 ?), with the parallel passages of other
  tablets (e.g. KTU 1.5 I 1–3 in the introduction and notes). Not the whole Baal Cycle.
- **Line numbering:** Virolleaud's (columns A–F, his line numbers), which differ from KTU's: align by content (e.g.
  Cassuto V AB D 37–39 = KTU 1.3 III 40–42).
- **His transcription convention** (adopted, with small additions, for the Hebrew apparatus of `מיתוסים-אוגריתיים/`):
  the Ugaritic consonants in Hebrew letters, with a superscript bar for the extra phonemes (`תֿ` = ṯ, `חֿ` = ḫ, `דֿ` = ḏ),
  and the three alephs vocalized (`אַ` a, `אִ` i, `אֻ` u).
- **Usefulness:** rank 5–6 (guidelines §3): 1950s readings, many superseded; a register check and a witness to the
  history of interpretation (he was the first to read the Baal texts against the Bible in Hebrew). Consult **after**
  drafting; never copy (it is public domain, but our rendering must be our own reading of KTU³).
