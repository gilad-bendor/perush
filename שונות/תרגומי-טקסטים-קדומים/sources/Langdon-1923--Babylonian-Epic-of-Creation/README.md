# Langdon 1923 – *The Babylonian Epic of Creation*

- **Title:** S. Langdon, *The Babylonian Epic of Creation, Restored from the Recently Recovered Tablets of Aššur. Transcription, Translation and Commentary*, Oxford: Clarendon, 1923.
- **URL:** https://archive.org/details/babylonianepicof00languoft (University of Toronto scan).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16 (`babylonianepicof00languoft_djvu.txt`, archive.org OCR).
- **License:** public domain in the US (published 1923) and in Israel (Langdon died 1937).
- **Contents:** OCR text: introduction (with a tablet-by-tablet synopsis, "Bk. I 29–54" etc.), transliteration, translation, commentary, and the akītu-festival context. The PDF is not downloaded (re-download from the URL).
- **Line numbering:** Langdon's; it incorporates the Aššur tablets (KAR) but predates most of the modern joins. Align by content.
- **Usefulness:** rank 5 (history only). Useful for the festival context (recitation on the 4th of Nisan) and for the older readings.
