## Contents (what it edits)
Synoptic edition + English translation + philological commentary + glossary of **all Akkadian Flood witnesses**:
- OB: Ms. **C0** (Schøyen MS 5108, early OB, "Larsa" two-column recension, George 2009 = CUSAS 10, 2); Ms. **C1 (+) C2** (Ipiq-Aya's late OB Sippar recension, Tablet III: BM 78942+78971+80385 (+) MAH 16064), cols. i–viii incl. colophon.
- Later: **Ark** Tablet (Finkel 2014; with hand copy pp. 63–65); **J** (Nippur, CBS 13532, Hilprecht 1910); **J1** (Ḫattuša, KBo 36, 26); **I** (Ugarit RS 22.421); **I1** (RS 20.161+); **I2** (RS 94.2953); **W** (Nineveh, DT 42); **U** (Nineveh, BM 98977+99231); **z** (LB, MMA 86.11.378A). R (K 4539) and W1 (Sm 365) listed but excluded from the reconstruction.
- **Gilgameš XI** (Flood section) — full edition, pp. 103–129.
- Tables of textual parallels between all manuscripts (pp. 4–13), literary discussion (ch. 3).

## Line numbering
Manuscript-siglum + column + line as on the tablet (e.g. `C1 i 33'`, `C2 ii 13'`, `Ark 1`, `U rev. 5'`), primes for lines counted from the first preserved line. **Not** the Lambert & Millard "Tablet III i 33" composite numbering, though for C1/C2 the column/line numbers largely coincide with L&M's Atra-ḫasīs III. Gilgameš XI uses George 2003 line numbers.

## Assessment
The single best, most up-to-date, legally free base for all Akkadian Flood texts (OB, MB, NA, LB, Gilg. XI) in one consistent system, by the same editor as SEAL (the SEAL pages are essentially the online version of this edition, with lemmatisation). Uses Wilcke's unpublished collations. Recommended primary base text for the Hebrew translation of Atra-ḫasīs III and for parallel apparatus to Gilg. XI. Limitations: covers the Flood episode only (not Atra-ḫasīs I–II); NC-ND licence.
