# English Wikipedia – transcriptions of three Hebrew inscriptions

- **What:** the transcriptions printed in the English Wikipedia articles "Gezer calendar", "Yavne-Yam ostracon"
  (Mesad Hashavyahu) and "Ketef Hinnom scrolls" (KH2), as of 2026-10-31. Licence **CC BY-SA 4.0** (attribution:
  the Wikipedia contributors of those articles; the editions behind them: Naveh 1960 for MH, Barkay et al. 2004 for
  KH2). Committed with this attribution.
- **URLs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=<article>&action=raw` (the API answered 429 to scripts; the
  raw page works).
- **In this folder:** `gezer-calendar.tsv`, `mesad-hashavyahu.tsv`, `ketef-hinnom-2.tsv` – square script, our
  conventions (no final forms; `[ ]` for Wikipedia's `{}` restorations).
- **Our changes:** KH2 line 1 `[...]` added before the uncertain ה (the line's start is lost); GZ line 5 divided
  `קצר וכל` (Wikipedia: `qṣrw kl`).
- **Status as a base:** an open text of a modern find whose critical edition is ©; used under the coordinator's
  decision of 2026-10-31 (a question for the user in `reports/round-5/run-4.md`). Bases of `מילים-באבן/2-…` (KH) and
  `3-…` (MH, GZ).
