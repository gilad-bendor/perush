# CDLI P464355 – Laws of Lipit-Ištar, composite (RIME 4.01.05.add10), with 13 exemplars

- **Text:** the Laws of Lipit-Ištar (LI), king of Isin (c. 1934–1924 BCE): prologue, laws, epilogue (blessings and curses) and colophon. Sumerian, known from Old Babylonian school copies (Nippur and elsewhere).
- **Edition:** CDLI composite **P464355** = Q000613, "RIME 4.01.05.add10 (Codex Lipit-Ishtar) composite"; CDLI cites RIME 4 (1990) and **C. Wilcke, "Gesetze in sumerischer Sprache", Fs Krecher (2014/2015), 455–612**, on which the composite is based. Transliteration and an **English translation on every line** (`#tr.en`; translator not named). No normalization (Sumerian). Roth 1997 (SBL WAW 6) and Steele 1948 are not owned.
