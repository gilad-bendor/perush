# CDLI P432130 – Laws of Ur-Namma, composite (RIME 3/2.01.01.20), with 9 exemplars

- **Text:** the Laws of Ur-Namma (or of his son Šulgi), Ur III, 21st c. BCE: prologue (Ur-Namma's reforms: freedom, standard measures, protection of the orphan and the widow), laws, epilogue (curses). Sumerian, known from OB copies.
- **Edition:** CDLI composite **P432130** = Q000947, "RIME 3/2.01.01.20 (Codex Ur-Namma) composite"; CDLI cites Kramer 1954 (OrNS 23), Finkelstein 1965 (AS 16), ISET 2 (1976), RIME 3/2 (Frayne 1997), **M. Civil, CUSAS 17 (2011) 221–286** (the Schøyen tablet MS 2064 with many new laws) and **Wilcke, Fs Krecher (2014/2015)**. Transliteration and **English on 628 lines** (`#tr.en`, with **variant notes** `# P257233 has instead …`). No normalization. Roth 1997 not owned.
- **Exemplars:** ex. 01 **Ist Ni 3191** (P226580, Nippur, the first-published tablet), ex. 02 Ist Si 277 (P226588, Sippar), ex. 03 IM 85688+ (P226319), ex. 04 BM 54722+ (P226629), ex. 05 Sb 7 (P226987, no ATF), ex. add06 **MS 2064** (P250820, Schøyen; Civil 2011), ex. add07 UM 55-21-71 (P257233), add08 BM 139526 (P464330), add09 AO 10638 (P464329) – the last two without links. Each linked exemplar line points (`>>Q000947 <label>`) to its composite line.

## Numbering
- **Composite lines** (CDLI): prologue 1–181 (with 127a/b, 128a/b, 130a/b, 176a/b); the laws begin at **181.a** (*u₄-ba tukum-bi lu₂-u₃* "At that time: if a man …") and run 182–474, then the sections **b001–b038, c001–c032, d001–d043, e001–e029, f001–f008** (Civil's additional laws, mainly from MS 2064, after `$ n lines broken`); epilogue **g009–g028′**. 628 labelled lines, many of them `[...]`.
- **The composite has no § marks.** Cite the laws by **Roth 1997's numbering** (§1–§37 for the laws known before 2011, mapped by content in `חוקים-ובריתות/6-חוקי-אור-נמה/project.md`) and by composite line; the b–f laws by composite line (Civil 2011 numbers them separately; not owned).
