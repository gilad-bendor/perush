## Contents
Re-edition of MM 818 (Montserrat; = Bab 36669,18, CDLI P432850; Atra-ḫasīs I) using the Babylon excavation photographs, re-dated to the Middle Babylonian period, plus discussion of VAT 17099 and the MB Babylon recension. Concerns Tablets I–II, not the Flood proper.

## Assessment
Secondary for the Flood; useful for the MB Babylon recension of Atra-ḫasīs I–II (cf. SEAL nos. 1560, 27123, 27126).
