# Records only: the gap laws of LH, and historical Hebrew translations

Recorded 2026-10-17 (Laws of Hammurabi project, `חוקים-ובריתות/2-חוקי-חמורבי/`), so that the next session does not search again.

## The gap laws (§66–§99; Roth's "gap ¶ a …")
The CDLI composite P464358 (base text) does not contain them (see `../CDLI/P464358--Laws-of-Hammurabi-composite/README.md`). Searched 2026-10-17:
- **CDLI:** the 26 exemplar records of RIME 4.03.06.add21 (ex. 02–26, Syria 58 083) have **no ATF**; ex. 27 (YBC 6516) covers §165–175 only.
- **eBL fragmentarium** (transliteration search): no LH duplicate found with the phrases tried.
- **Cuq 1918**, *Les nouveaux fragments du code de Hammourabi sur le prêt à intérêt et les sociétés* (Mémoires de l'Académie des Inscriptions 41): archive.org `lesnouveauxfragm00cuquoft`, NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT; OCR saved here (`Cuq-1918--nouveaux-fragments_djvu.txt`). It gives Scheil's 1916 text of the Louvre fragment of the loan laws (Cuq's "Art. 89–96", 1918 readings) in transliteration with a French translation; the OCR is poor (signs and diacritics garbled). **Not usable as a base text**; a later session could use the PDF page images.
- **Ungnad 1909**, *Keilschrifttexte der Gesetze Hammurapis* (archive.org `keilschrifttexte00hammuoft`, public domain): hand copies of the stele and of the OB, NA and NB fragments – cuneiform only.
- **Kohler, Peiser, Ungnad, *Hammurabi's Gesetz*** I–VI (1904–1923; archive.org `hammurabisgesetz04hamm`, `…05hamm`, `bub_gb_2QUqAAAAYAAJ`): vol. II and later volumes contain the newly found fragments in transliteration and German (not downloaded; check page images).
- **Modern editions (©, not obtained):** Driver & Miles 1955 (vol. II, text); Borger, *Babylonisch-assyrische Lesestücke*; Roth 1997 (SBL WAW 6); Richardson 2000/2004; Finet 2002.
**Result:** the gap laws are not translated in this round (open decision in the project).

The OCR has no Hebrew text (the Hebrew is lost in the djvu); the PDF page images have it. Public domain (Müller d. 1912). Not downloaded.
