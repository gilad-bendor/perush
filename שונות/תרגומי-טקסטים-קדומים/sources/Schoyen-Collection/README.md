## Files
- `atra-hasis-b-ms-5108.html/.md` + `atra-hasis-a-ms-5108_l.jpg` — **MS 5108** = Ms. **C0**, the earliest (early OB, "Larsa") two-column Atra-ḫasīs tablet, Tablet II col. iv (flood decision; Ea and Atra-ḫasīs) and col. viii. Ed. A. R. George, *Babylonian Literary Texts in the Schøyen Collection* (CUSAS 10, 2009) no. 2 (paid).
- `atra-hasis-a-ms-2950.html/.md` + `atra-hasis-a-ms-2950_l.jpg` — **MS 2950** = CUSAS 10, 3: OB fragment (upper half, 11+1 lines) mentioning swollen animals and mankind and Watram-ḫasīs speaking to Ea; position in the epic uncertain (plague episode of Tablet I, or the gap in III iii–iv). Not included in Wasserman 2020.

The `.md` files are clean text extractions of the catalogue descriptions (popular-level; no transliteration).

## Assessment
Descriptive only. The actual texts: MS 5108 is fully edited in Wasserman 2020 / SEAL no. 1518; MS 2950 only in George 2009 (CUSAS 10, 3) — a gap for free sources. CDLI has photo/line art for both (P254176, P252009 in `../CDLI/`).
