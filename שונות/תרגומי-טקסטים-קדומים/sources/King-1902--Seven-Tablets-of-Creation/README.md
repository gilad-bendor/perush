# King 1902 – *The Seven Tablets of Creation* (Enūma eliš)

- **Title:** L. W. King, *The Seven Tablets of Creation, or the Babylonian and Assyrian Legends Concerning the Creation of the World and of Mankind*, 2 vols., London: Luzac, 1902.
- **URLs:** vol. I (transliterations, translations, introduction, appendices): https://archive.org/details/the-seven-tablets-of-creation.-vol.-1 ; vol. II (plates, cuneiform copies): https://archive.org/details/seventabletsofcr02kinguoft (marked NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16 (OCR text of vol. I only: `vol-1_djvu.txt`).
- **License:** public domain (published 1902; King died 1919). The vol. I scan is a user upload of the public-domain book.
- **Contents:** `vol-1_djvu.txt` – archive.org's OCR of vol. I. The translation of Tablet I begins at line ~5942 ("1. When in the height heaven was not named"). OCR quality is fair for the English; transliterations are unreliable (diacritics).
- **Line numbering:** King's own; it follows the then-known fragments, so it diverges widely from eBL (Heinrich) wherever new manuscripts were found (much of Tablets II–III, V, VI). Tablet V was known only in small parts. Align by content.
- **Usefulness:** rank 5 (history of interpretation; guidelines §3). It records the first readings (e.g. "chaos, Tiamat"; "he made the heavens as a covering"; the bow) that the older comparative literature (Gunkel, Heidel) argued from. Never take a reading from it without the eBL edition.
