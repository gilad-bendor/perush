## Files (local only)

- `as12.txt` — `pdftotext -layout` extraction (the OCR has errors in diacritics and sign indices; use for the translation and the commentary, never for the transliteration).

## Content
Introduction (a summary of the eleven "songs", the historical setting, the manuscripts known in 1940); transliteration and translation of the composite on facing pages (pp. 16–71; Kramer's 436 lines, the same numbering as ETCSL); commentary.

## Usefulness
**Rank 5** (pre-1990): the first edition, historically important (the first full Sumerian city lament, and the first comparison with Lamentations). Readings are outdated in places (Kramer's "song" for *kirugu*; many Emesal forms misunderstood in 1940). Use as a literal check and for the history of interpretation only.
