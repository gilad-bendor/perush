# Arno Poebel, *Historical and Grammatical Texts* (PBS V), 1914 — cuneiform copies (plates), incl. No. 1 = CBS 10673 (Flood Story)

- **Author:** Arno Poebel. **Series:** University of Pennsylvania, The University Museum, Publications of the Babylonian Section, vol. V. Philadelphia 1914. 85 autograph plates + 40 photographic plates. Transliterations/translations are in PBS IV/1 (historical) and VI/1 (grammatical).
- **URL:** https://archive.org/details/historicalgramma00poeb (Princeton Theological Seminary Library scan). Alternative: https://archive.org/details/historicalandgr00sectgoog (Google/Harvard).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Licence:** public domain in the US (1914); author d. 1958 (life+70 → PD everywhere from 2029). Open full-view on archive.org.
- **Files:** `historicalgramma00poeb.pdf` (19.3 MB, 264 pp.), `historicalgramma00poeb_djvu.txt` (OCR of the few printed pages), `archive-metadata.json`.

## Contents relevant here
- **No. 1** (first autograph plates): hand copy of CBS 10673 (Sumerian Flood Story); photographs on the photographic plates (ORACC cites "PBS 5 1; ib. pl. 86ff. [photo]").
- Also Nippur king-list tablets used for the Sumerian King List (see PBS IV/1 "New Lists of Kings" for the numbers).

## Line numbering
Columns i–vi of the tablet as copied (obv. i–iii, rev. iv–vi).

## Assessment
The only published hand copy of the main manuscript; together with the CDLI photo (`../CDLI/P265876/`) the basis for checking any reading. Plates are images — no usable OCR.
