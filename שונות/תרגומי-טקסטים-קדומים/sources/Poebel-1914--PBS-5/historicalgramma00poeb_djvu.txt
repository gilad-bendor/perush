FOREWORD 


In this volume are presented eighty-five autograph plates 
containing one hundred and fifty-eight texts copied by Dr. 
Poebel between the spring of 1912 and the spring of 1914, 
during which time he was at work upon a selected series of 
tablets which had been assigned to him from the Babylonian 
collections of the Museum. 

The title of the volume sufficiently indicates the character 
of the texts chosen for immediate publication. In Volume IV 
will be found transcriptions and translations of a portion of 
the historical texts, and in Volume VI will be found transcrip- 
tions and translations of a portion of the grammatical texts. 
It has been Dr. Poebel’s purpose to continue these transcrip- 
tions and translations until all of these texts shall have been 
dealt with in the same manner. It is the plan of the Museum 
in the event of this purpose being realized to publish these 
additional translations as continuations of Volumes IV and VI. 

In addition to the autograph plates, this volume contains 
forty photographic plates of tablets selected froth the same 
series. 

It is due to Dr. Poebel to state that he has had no oppor- 


tunity of inspecting the plates in this volume.
