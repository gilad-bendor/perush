## Line numbering
The sample shows numbered lines ("1 (מִי) רָאָה מַעֲמַקִּים…"). The translators say they edited the Akkadian themselves on the basis of George 2003 plus all fragments published since. Expect George/eBL numbering (to be verified with the book).

## Assessment
**Most important comparison text for this project.** It is the first Hebrew translation made by Israeli Assyriologists directly from an up-to-date Akkadian text (Wasserman also edited *The Flood: The Akkadian Sources*, 2020, which is saved separately in `../Wasserman-2020--The-Flood-Akkadian-Sources/`). The register is deliberately contemporary Hebrew ("העברית שלנו כיום"), not pseudo-biblical, and vocalized. It is the best source for current Israeli Assyriological Hebrew name spellings. Recommended purchase: the ₪26 e-book is the cheapest.
