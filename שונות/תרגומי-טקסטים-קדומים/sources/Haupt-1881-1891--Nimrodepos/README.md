- **Editor:** Paul Haupt (1858–1926)
- **Works:**
  1. *Das babylonische Nimrodepos. Keilschrifttext der Bruchstücke der sogenannten Izdubarlegenden mit dem keilinschriftlichen Sintfluthberichte nach den Originalen im Britischen Museum copirt und herausgegeben.* Leipzig: Hinrichs, 1884 (Abt. 1) – 1891 (Heft 2: Tablets XI–XII; the Flood tablet text is on pp. 95 ff. and the composite "vollständiger Text" on pp. 134–150). ## Line numbering
This is Haupt's line numbering of XI, the basis of Jensen, Ungnad, Muss-Arnolt, Thompson and Heidel (see Heidel README for offsets against George).

## Assessment
These are the first complete, accurate cuneiform copies of the epic (Thompson's preface: "very accurately copied"). They are of historical and palaeographic interest only. They were superseded by Thompson 1930 and George 2003 (whose plates include all manuscripts), and by photos (CDLI/eBL/BM). Haupt 1881 is a historical-interpretive piece.
