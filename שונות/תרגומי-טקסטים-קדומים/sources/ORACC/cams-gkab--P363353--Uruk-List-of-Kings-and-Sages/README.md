## Line numbering
ORACC: obverse `o 1`–`o 16`, reverse `r 1`–`r 9` (25 lines). A ruling after o 7 separates the seven antediluvian king/sage pairs (o 1–7) from the postdiluvian section (o 8 ff.); a double ruling after o 11 separates the *apkallū* from the human *ummânū* (o 12 ff.). The beginnings of o 1–8 are broken: the word "after the flood" that some modern discussions supply before o 8 is **not** in the preserved text.

## Usefulness
The only open, line-by-line edition of this tablet. It pairs each antediluvian king (Ayyalu = Alulim, Alalgar, Ammeluana, Ammegalana, Enmeušumgalana, Dumuzi, Enmeduranki) with his *apkallu* (Uan = Oannes, Uanduga, Enmeduga, Enmegalama, Enmebuluga, Anenlilda, Utuabzu), then continues after the ruling with Enmerkar's sage Nungalpiriggal and with human scholars down to Esarhaddon's Aba-Enlil-dari "whom the Aramaeans call Aḫiqar". It is the cuneiform background of Berossus' fish-men sages (Oannes) and of the Greek lists of ten antediluvian kings. Used in `סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/6-אזכורי-המבול/`.
