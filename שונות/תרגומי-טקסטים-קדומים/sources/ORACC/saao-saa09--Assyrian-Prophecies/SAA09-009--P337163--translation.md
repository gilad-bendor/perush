# SAA 9 009 (P337163), Parpola 1997, translation

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** [O prote]ge of Mullissu, [...] of the Lady of Arbela!  
**[o 3]** [They] are the strongest among the gods; they [lov]e and keep sending their love [to] Assurbanipal, the creation of their hands. [They g]ive him heart for the sake of his life:  
**[o 8]** I roam the desert desiring your [lif]e. I cross over rivers and oceans, I traverse mountains and mountain chains, I cross over all rivers. Droughts and showers consume me and affect my beautiful figure. I am worn out, my body is exhausted for your sake.  
**[o 16]** I have ordained life for you in the assembly of all the gods. My arms are strong, they shall not forsake you before the gods. My shoulders are alert, they will keep carrying you.  
**[o 20]** I keep demanding life for you with my lips; [...] your life; you shall increase life.  
**[o 22]** [O favourite of] Nabû, may your lips rejoice! [I keep spe]aking good words about you [in the assembly of] all [the gods]; I roam the desert [desiri]ng [your life].  
**[o 26]** [In woe I will r]ise and slau[ghter] your enemy; [your ...] will [...] and retur[n] to his country.  
*((Break))*  
*(ruling)*  
**[r 1']** May Mullissu and the Lady of Arbela keep Assurbanipal, the creation of their hands, alive for ever!  
*(ruling)*  
**[r 4']** By the mouth of the woman Dunnaša-amur [of Arbe]la.  
**[r 6']** Nis[an] 18, eponymy of Bel-šadû'a, governor of Tyre (650).  
*(ruling)*
