# SAA 9 008 (P334828), Parpola 1997, translation

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/saao/saa09/P334828/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** Words [concerning the Elam]ites.  
**[o 2]** [God] says as follows:  
**[o 3]** "I have go[ne and I ha]ve come."  
**[o 4]** He s[ai]d (this) five, six times, and then:  
**[o 5]** "I have come from the [m]ace. I have pulled out the snake which was inside it, I have cut it in pieces, and I have broken the mace."  
**[o 8]** And (he said): "I will destroy Elam; its army shall be levelled to the ground. In this manner I will finish Elam."  
*(ruling)*
