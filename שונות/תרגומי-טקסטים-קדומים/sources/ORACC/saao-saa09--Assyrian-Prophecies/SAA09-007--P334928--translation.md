# SAA 9 007 (P334928), Parpola 1997, translation

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/saao/saa09/P334928/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** The prophetess Mullissu-kabtat (has said):  
**[o 2]** "It is the word of Queen Mullissu: Fear not, Assurbanipal!  
**[o 3]** "Until I have done and given to you what I promised, until you rule as king over the sons of the bearded courtiers and the successors of the eunuchs, [I will look af]ter you in the Palace of Succession; [your father] shall gird the diadem [...].  
**[o 8]** "[The ... king]s of the lands shall say to one another: ['Come, let us] go to Assurbanipal! The king has witnesses! [Whatever the god]s decreed for our fathers and forefathers, [let him now] arbitrate between us.'  
**[o 12]** "[Mullis]su [has] said: [You shall ru]le over [the king]s of the lands; you shall show them their frontiers and set the courses they take.  
**[o 14]** "Secondly, let me tell you: I will finish the land of Gomer like (I finished) Elam.  
**[r 1]** "... I will break the thorn, I will pluck the bramble into a tuft of wool, I will turn the wasps into a squash.  
**[r 3]** "Like a centipede, like a ...! You say, 'What does it mean, like a centipede, like a ...?' Like a centipede I will enter Egypt, like a ... I will come out.  
**[r 6]** "You whose mother is Mullissu, have no fear! You whose nurse is the Lady of Arbela, have no fear!  
**[r 7]** "I will carry you on my hip like a nurse, I will put you between my breasts (like) a pomegranate. At night I will stay awake and guard you; in the daytime I will give you milk; at dawn I will play 'watch, watch your ...' with you. As for you, have no fear, my calf, whom I (have) rear(ed)."  
*(ruling)*
