# AkkLove LAOS4-02 (P251898) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/P251898/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** Daughter of an exile – my heart rejoiced; (she) took away the laugh of (my) mother.  
**[o 4]** My mood plunged into the infesting heart –  
**[o 6]** her lovemaking is an infesting love.  
**[o 7]** She is sweet as honey,  
**[o 8]** She is fresh like wine to the nose.  
**[o 10]** (My) mood is (fixed on her) fruits.  
**[o 11]** As life, mentioning her name is insatiable,  
**[o 13]** Though she is distant, her merriment sets in motion.  
**[o 14]** 14 [One who has] a loved-one chases (lit. sees) a dream:  
**[o 15]** my heart [is broken], my mood is agitated!  
**[o 16]** [Had] Ištar schemed this affair, lovers could not [ever] be seen!  
**[o 18]** My he[art] rejoiced! My mood became cheerful!  
**[o 19]** ... is the truth. Restless and constantly trembling:  
**[o 20]** I (Text: he) awoke to the noise of swallows, writhed in (my) bedroom on the bed:  
**[r 22]** The lov[e-charm] returned into m[y hear]t!
