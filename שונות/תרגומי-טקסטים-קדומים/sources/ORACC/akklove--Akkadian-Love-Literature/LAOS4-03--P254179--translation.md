Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** I am [...]  
**[o 2]** I shall place [...]  
**[o 3]** I shall be attentive, I shall watch out [...]  
**[o 4]** The boy is not aware of the (girl) watching him.  
**[o 5]** To you I am very much attracted(?),  
**[o 6]** I have not devoted myself to anyone:  
**[o 7]** Rejo[ice in me, co]me quickly, O beloved!  
**[o 8]** Your sweetness coiled around my [he]art(?).  
**[o 9]** I kept forgetting my words,  
**[o 10]** I cannot make my mind which is like a dream.  
**[o 11]** I think of the (girl) before me.  
**[o 12]** I who am blooming, my fate (lit.: god) is with you!  
**[o 13]** I encircle (you) like a wife, so that (you?) will not become “stiff”.  
**[o 14]** I talk about you constantly,  
**[o 15]** I am consumed, I am troubled, I am distorted(?),  
**[o 16]** I craved you again – (and) became distressed.  
**[o 17]** I saw your face: you are a god!  
**[o 18]** I implore you: let your heart rejoice in me!  
**[r 19]** It is you who makes me flourish,  
**[r 20]** Verily! a lover awaits for you!  
**[r 21]** I am chosen – submissive, indeed a slave for you!  
**[r 22]** Truthful she is: me – your favorite sign!  
**[r 23]** My fruits are unforgettable,  
**[r 24]** My attractiveness is unattainable.  
**[r 25]** I will be suitable for you, I will be submissive to you,  
**[r 26]** Verily! My love is your gift!  
**[r 27]** A loving (girl) brings luck to ...  
**[r 28]** At daytime (when) he went away from me –  
**[r 29]** Gossiping concerning me was not cut off from their (the other women’s) mouth.  
**[r 30]** Those who slander me are not gloomy, day and night.  
**[r 32]** Let your heart be (amicably) thrumming, may it not accept the lies!  
**[r 33]** To the heart ...  
**[r 34]** Many things my ... is full.  
**[r 35]** ... me ...  
**[b.e. 36]** ... not ...
