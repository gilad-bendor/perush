# AkkLove LAOS4-26 (P296059) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/P296059/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** Love-charm! Love-charm!  
**[o 2]** His two horns are gold, his(!) tail is pure lapis-lazuli:  
**[o 4]** Placed in the heart of Ištar.  
**[o 5]** I raised my voice to her, but she did not turn to me,  
**[o 6]** I gazed (at her) but she did not look at me.  
**[o 7]** If she is ‘chaste’, may her sweetheart perish.  
**[o 9]** If she is a cloister-lady, may her provider perish.  
**[o 11]** May the nubile girl, a daughter of a gentleman  
**[o 12]** fall at my cry, at my shout!  
**[o 14]** May the dough fall (out) of her hands, (as well as) the little one on her arms.  
**[r 18]** Do not attach your house to me!  
**[r 19]** Do not look after the task under you hands!  
**[r 21]** Look at me like (a cow held with) a halter!  
**[r 22]** Lick me like (a cow licking) a calf!  
**[r 23]** Why, my love, did you cover your head like (with) a p.-cover?  
**[r 25]** (Why) like (with) a belt did you girdle you loins?  
**[r 27]** My l[ove-making] is (as good) as oil,  
**[r 28]** ... ... ... you (f.).  
**[r 29]** ... come forward, to me!  
**[r 30]** ... ... name(?).
