# AkkLove LAOS4-07 (X001001) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/X001001/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** Come in, shepherd, Ištar’s lover,  
**[o 2]** Spend the night here, shepherd, Ištar’s lover,  
**[o 3]** At your entering, my father is happy for you,  
**[o 4]** my mother, Ningal, is rejoiced over you.  
**[o 5]** She served you oil in a bowl.  
**[o 6]** At your entering, the bolts rejoice over you,  
**[o 7]** the door itself shall be opened [for you]!  
**[o 8]** You, wooden bolt – what do you know?  
**[o 9]** What do you know? The entrance of my darling!  
**[o 10]** Indeed, I love, I love, oh, lusty one.  
**[o 11]** He freed his dogs ...  
**[o 12]** (saying:) "Let me come in front of Ningal!"  
**[o 13]** After her bride entered in front of Ningal,  
**[o 14]** they divided the sweet cake in the bowl.  
**[o 15]** The illustrious one is worried about the dogs and shepherds.  
**[o 16]** - Why are they insulting (him)? He brought and offered.  
**[o 17]** He went and came back, he brought and offered,  
**[o 18]** Yes, the illustrious one is worried about the shepherds’ affluence.  
**[o 19]** The company is well, the king is well,  
**[o 20]** Dumuzi is well, Ištar’s lover.  
**[o 21]** [... loosen,] loosen your sandals!  
**[o 22]** [...] unpack your nets!  
**[o 23]** [...] we shall eat, oh, lusty one!  
**[o 24]** [...] oh, lusty one!  
**[o 25]** [...] he refuses, he won’t bring.  
**[o 26]** [...] we shall eat, oh, lusty one!  
**[o 27]** [...] we shall sprinkle!  
**[o 28]** [...]oh, lusty one!  
**[o 29]** [...] the cry of your flock,  
**[r 30]** [...] your lambs,  
**[r 31]** [...] the cry of your flock,  
**[r 32]** [...] neglect(?) our sheepfold!  
**[r 33]** Recite for me [his name/number], he wouldn’t let (it) go!  
**[r 34]** [...]  
**[r 35]** Recite for me his name/number, he wouldn’t let (it) go!  
**[r 36]** Ištar went into his sheepfold,  
**[r 37]** opened her mouth and said to him:  
**[r 38]** "How sweet is the water, the water of your sheepfold!  
**[r 39]** your water murmurs, It is the water of (your) animal stall!"  
**[r 40]** 'I shall sing on the heroic shepherd.'  
**[r 41]** Total 39 lines.  
**[r 42]** Series: 'O darling loving me.'  
**[r 43]** Library tablet of Taqšītum, son of Meme-Enlil, administrator of the temple of Ištar.
