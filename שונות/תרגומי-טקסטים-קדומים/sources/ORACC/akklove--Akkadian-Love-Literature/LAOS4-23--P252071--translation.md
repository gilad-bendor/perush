**[o 7]** I have torn the thorn, I will be sowing a vine!  
**[o 9]** I have poured water onto the fierce fire.  
**[o 12]** Love me as your lamb,  
**[o 14]** Encircle me as your small cattle.  
**[o 16]** Look at me!
