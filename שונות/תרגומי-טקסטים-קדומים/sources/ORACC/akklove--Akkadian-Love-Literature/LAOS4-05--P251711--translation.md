# AkkLove LAOS4-05 (P251711) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/P251711/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** A woman she is! May they not talk favorably with e[ach other(?)]!  
**[o 2]** May they not [kis]s(?) [each other] in the course of night!  
**[o 3]** May they not tell each other whatever is pure in their hearts!  
**[o 4]** Oh exalted one, great daughter of Sîn!  
**[o 5]** The mistress of the en, you are superior: who can be seen next to you?  
**[o 6]** A lady through true ordinances, the great one of the lord, how majestic you are!  
**[o 7]** [Conceived(?)] from a pure heart, you surpass your bearing mother!  
**[o 8]** The one(m.) that makes the field flourish...  
**[o 9]** the pure ... they will s[ing(?)].  
**[t.e. 1']** The true goddess of ordinances, greatly superior, the head of the noble ones.
