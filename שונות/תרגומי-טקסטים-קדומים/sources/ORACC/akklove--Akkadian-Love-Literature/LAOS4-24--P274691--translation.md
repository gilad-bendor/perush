# AkkLove LAOS4-24 (P274691) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).

**[o 1]** Barrier-like she is alienated, entangled as a tiny berry-fruit.  
**[o 3]** Like an orchard fruit come out over him!  
**[o 4]** Let the Tigris carry for you(f.) charcoal, sangû, copper, lead of Susa!  
**[o 7]** Let it carry hither sangû! Oh you, who pierces your(f.) eyes!  
**[o 8]** Incantation (to calm) the fire of the heart.
