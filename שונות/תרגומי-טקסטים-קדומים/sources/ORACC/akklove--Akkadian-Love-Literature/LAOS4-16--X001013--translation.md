**[o i 1]** (He:) Yell! Do not bother to reply!  
**[o i 2]** Not so much talking!  
**[o i 3]** My decision is made,  
**[o i 4]** I will not change for you a word, anything I said.  
**[o i 6]** He who lies on his back for a woman is a weevil from the city wall.  
**[o i 8]** If he is not serious he is no match for a man.  
**[o i 9]** (She:) May my righteousness appear before Ištar the queen!  
**[o i 11]** May my love prevail, may my slanderer come to shame!  
**[o i 13]** At Nanāya’s command I have always (taken upon me) to adore you, to charm you, to take care of (my) baby!  
**[o i 16]** Where is my match?!  
**[o i 17]** (He:) I remember more than you your tricks of the past.  
**[o i 19]** Give up! Go away! Report to your counselor that we are (now) awake!  
**[o i 22]** (She:) I will catch you, and this very day I shall reconcile your love with mine.  
**[o i 24]** I keep on praying to Nanāya;  
**[o i 25]** (So that) I shall accept your peace, my lord, forever, as a gift.  
**[o i 27]** (He:) I shall lay siege upon you,  
**[o i 28]** I shall gather my clouds on you.  
**[o i 29]** May your supporter take away your seductiveness, put an end to your complaint!  
**[o i 31]** Accept the truth!  
**[o i 32]** ...  
**[o ii 1]** (He:) [...] she/he was aw[ake(?)]  
**[o ii 2]** and does not exist.  
**[o ii 3]** Nothing is in my heart ... will be paid out to her.  
**[o ii 5]** I yearn for my [lo]ve.  
**[o ii 6]** (She:) May queen Ištar [pour] confusion over the one who loves you not!  
**[o ii 8]** May she, like me, [be afflicted] with sleeplessness!  
**[o ii 9]** May she be dazed and [restless] all night long!  
**[o ii 10]** (He:) I despise the girl who does not wo[rship (me)].  
**[o ii 11]** I do not desire for the girl who does not [fawn].  
**[o ii 12]** I shall not give her [my love-charm].  
**[o ii 13]** Talking in order to dis[agree]  
**[o ii 14]** Why [does it exist?]  
**[o ii 15]** I shall stop [her/my] slanderers,  
**[o ii 16]** I shall not hear what [...]  
**[o ii 17]** To the m[idst of darkness] I have thrown away my love:  
**[o ii 19]** Why do you keep bothering [me]?  
**[o ii 20]** (She:) My (ominous) signs trouble me:  
**[o ii 21]** My upper lip becomes moist,  
**[o ii 22]** While my lower lip trembles!  
**[o ii 23]** I shall embrace him, I shall kiss him,  
**[o ii 24]** I shall gaze at [him],  
**[o ii 25]** I shall achieve my victory [...] over [my slanderers],  
**[o ii 27]** And to my lover [I shall] gracefully [...]  
**[o ii 28]** When our sleep [...]  
**[o ii 29]** We shall achieve [victory]  
**[o ii 30]** ...  
**[r iii 1]** (She:) ...  
**[r iii 2]** In [...]  
**[r iii 3]** [My(?)] gazelle [...]  
**[r iii 4]** I run but I cannot [reach him],  
**[r iii 5]** She gave him to Ištar as a gift.  
**[r iii 6]** (He:) As for those (women) who keep telling you:  
**[r iii 7]** “Y[ou] are not the only one!”  
**[r iii 8]** Stop! I have taken my love away, and will not [return (it)].  
**[r iii 9]** I have removed [it] from your body,  
**[r iii 10]** I have taken my charms thousands of miles away.  
**[r iii 11]** (She:) I seek [your] pleasures,  
**[r iii 12]** My lord, I yearn for [your] love.  
**[r iii 13]** Because in your delights [...]  
**[r iii 14]** Let them be veiled, would they never [...]  
**[r iii 15]** I shall talk [to you] day and night.  
**[r iii 16]** (He:) Let me repeat it twice and thrice;  
**[r iii 17]** I swear not to say anything kind to you (lit. satisfy my mouth with kindness),  
**[r iii 18]** Take your place by the window orifice:  
**[r iii 19]** Come on, catch my love!  
**[r iii 20]** (She:) My eyes are very tired,  
**[r iii 21]** I am sleepless from gazing at him,  
**[r iii 22]** It seems that he walked across my street?  
**[r iii 23]** The day has gone by, where is [my darling (or: my lord)]?  
**[r iv 1]** (She:) To catch him [...]  
**[r iv 2]** The one [...]  
**[r iv 3]** What [...]  
**[r iv 4]** Come on, let me hold [his] k[nees],  
**[r iv 5]** I shall sit and wait maby he is on his way to me!  
**[r iv 6]** (He:) I have sworn to you by Nanāya and Hammurāpi the king;  
**[r iv 7]** Indeed, I am telling to you the truth;  
**[r iv 8]** Your love is nothing more to me than agony and grief.  
**[r iv 10]** (She:) They(f.) ... because I trust my loved one.  
**[r iv 11]** My slanderers outnumber the stars of heaven.  
**[r iv 13]** May they hide! May they be scarce!  
**[r iv 14]** May they hide themselves forever right now!  
**[r iv 15]** And as for me, I sat down, listening to the words of my lord.  
**[r iv 17]** (He:) My one, not ugly are your features – (they are) as before,  
**[r iv 19]** (When) I stood by you,  
**[r iv 20]** And you leaned your sh[oulders] (against me).  
**[r iv 21]** [Mag]irtum (“Favorable”) is your name,  
**[r iv 22]** “Wise la[dy]” – your title.  
**[r iv 23]** The other woman is indeed our misfortune,  
**[r iv 24]** By Ištar!  
**[r iv 25]** [...Nice]ly you reply.
