# AkkLove LAOS4-04 (P252226) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/P252226/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** I scorn the girl who does not adore (me),  
**[o 2]** I have no desire for the girl who does not play up to (me),  
**[o 3]** I shall not give her (my) allures!  
**[o 4]** I shall stand over her!  
**[o 5]** Talking in order to disagree - why does it exist?  
**[o 7]** Shall I give my love where the heart of darkness is?  
**[o 8]** No one can control [me/it]!  
**[o 9]** Break off, leave! You ma[ke me speech]less,  
**[o 10]** (there is) not much to talk (about)!  
**[o 11]** What I said is said and [...]  
**[o 12]** I will not ch[ange it for y]ou!  
**[o 13]** The one who [lies down] for a woman,  
**[o 14]** is (nothing but) a weevil of the wall!  
**[o 15]** If he does not th[rive],  
**[o 16]** he is no match for a man.  
**[o 17]** You(f.) were born the daughter of a substitute,  
**[o 18]** with no dowry!  
**[o 19]** You have a mole on (your) forehead!  
**[o 20]** As long as you show no respect, pu[tting yourself] to shame,  
**[o 21]** I shall tell you where your (right) place is!  
**[o 22]** You do not listen to me, you.  
**[o 23]** (By) following your heart, mounting the clouds,  
**[o 25]** You keep chasing lovers away!  
**[o 26]** This goes over (the limit)! Where is the source of your rebellion?  
**[o 27]** Ask the previous women!  
**[o 28]** (You are) like a field of salt,  
**[o 29]** (can I) be happy with all (this)?  
**[o 30]** I was happy with the fruit,  
**[o 31]** (can I) be happy with all (this)?  
**[r 32]** and [...]  
**[r 33]** mouth [...]  
**[r 34]** The loved one [...]  
**[r 35]** You should not put [...]  
**[r 36]** To your canal – no one will come near it!  
**[r 37]** Your lord, your task –  
**[r 38]** do not place (them) in the salt!  
**[r 39]** your field is all too well known!  
**[r 40]** The (fact) that you did not bring for me (good) news from your womb, as a baby of men – should I swallow (that) potsherd?  
**[r 43]** I will release the bitch!  
**[r 44]** One who bolts down a stone in order to release you  
**[r 45]** when would he have his word?  
**[r 46]** Verily, you, when (somebody) came near [you],  
**[r 47]** you were convulsing like the goddess Belili,  
**[r 48]** you were dancing at dawn (instead of) sleeping.  
**[r 50]** You are imposing grief on yourself!
