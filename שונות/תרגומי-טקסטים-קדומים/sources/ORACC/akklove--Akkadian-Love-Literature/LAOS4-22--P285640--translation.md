# AkkLove LAOS4-22 (P285640) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/P285640/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** Enki loves the love-charm,  
**[o 3]** The love-charm, Ištar’s son, [sit]ting in [her?/his? l]ap,  
**[o 5]** Turning here through the sap of the incense-tree.  
**[o 6]** You, Oh two beautiful maidens, are blooming!  
**[o 8]** To the garden you come down, indeed come down to the garden!  
**[o 10]** You have drunk(?) the sap of the incense-tree.  
**[o 12]** I have seized now your(f.) drooling mouth (lit. “mouth of sap”),  
**[o 13]** I have seized your(f.) shining eyes,  
**[o 15]** I have seized your(f.) urinating vulva (lit. “vulva of urine”).  
**[o 17]** I leaped to the garden of Sîn,  
**[o 19]** I cut the poplar tree for her day.  
**[r 21]** Encircle(f.) me between the boxwood trees, as the shepherd encircles the flock,  
**[r 23]** As the goat (encircles) its kid, the sheep its lamb, the mare its foal!  
**[r 25]** His arms are adorned,  
**[r 26]** Oil and (the sound of) harp – his lips.  
**[r 28]** A cup of oil in his hands, a cup of cedar fragrance on his shoulders.  
**[r 30]** The love-charms have persuaded her, driven her to ecstasy –  
**[r 32]** Now I have seized your(f.) lustful mouth (lit. “mouth of sexual attraction”).  
**[r 33]** I conjure you(f.) by the name of Ištar and Išḧara:  
**[r 35]** ‘Until his neck and your(f.) neck are not entwined – you(f.) shall not find peace!’
