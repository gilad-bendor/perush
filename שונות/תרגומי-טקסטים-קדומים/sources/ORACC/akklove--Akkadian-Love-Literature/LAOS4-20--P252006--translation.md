# AkkLove LAOS4-20 (P252006) – translation (Wasserman 2016)

Web: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/akklove/P252006/html

Project translation extracted from the ORACC HTML view (rank 3 in our hierarchy; consult only after our own reading).
Each paragraph: [first label – last label of the transliteration lines it translates] text. "## " = section heading.

**[o 1]** [Keep] her apart! keep [her] apart!  
**[o 2]** [The] daughters of Anu, the lights of heaven, [in day-ti]me(?) purified the sky of Anu.  
**[o 5]** Love came about, twittering over the people; May Love twitter over me!  
**[o 7]** Let me cast (a spell), let me speak, let me utter words of love (lit.: let me speak, let me love):  
**[o 8]** “Think of me as an ašnugallum-snake!  
**[o 9]** May your face rejoice as a wild cow!  
**[o 10]** Do not wait on your father’s counsel,  
**[o 11]** Do not heed your mother’s advice!”  
**[o 12]** If she is a hierodule may she [...] her support;  
**[o 13]** If she is a cloister-lady may she [...] her gift;  
**[o 14]** If she is a harlot may she clean(?) her tavern, may she throw herself at me!  
**[o 15]** The incantation is not mine:  
**[o 16]** (it is) the incantation (which) Ea and Ištar have created.
