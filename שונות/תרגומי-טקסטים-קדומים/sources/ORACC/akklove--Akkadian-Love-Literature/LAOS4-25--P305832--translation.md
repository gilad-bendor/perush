**[r 26]** You(m.!) are fierce, you(m.!) are furious, you are going up(?) – be at peace my heart! Make firm your(m.) two foundations ...  
**[r 27]** Let me throw on your(m.) heart ice (and) frost!  
**[r 28]** When you see me – be soothed(m.) like (one who drinks) beer, like a ... rel[ease (me)]!  
**[r 29]** Because you(m.?) are sitting while I am kneeling, (because) I am standing at [your s]ervice, let me cast a spell on you(m.), let me take the spell of vertigo that is in front of [you].
