# CUC – the Copenhagen Ugaritic Corpus (base text of the Ugaritic corpus `מיתוסים-אוגריתיים/`)

- **What:** a Text-Fabric dataset of 278 tablets of KTU (*Die keilalphabetischen Texte aus Ugarit*, 3rd ed., Dietrich,
  Loretz & Sanmartín 2013): every sign with its KTU³ reading marks (restored, damaged/italic, omitted, excised,
  alternative readings), word division, columns, sides and lines; plus per-tablet **machine word parses**
  (`auto_parsing/0.2.7/KTU 1.x.tsv`: surface form, morphological parse, **DULAT** lemma, POS, short English gloss) and
  a few **human-reviewed** parses (`reviewed/`: KTU 1.3 complete, 1.5 cols. I–II, 1.14, letters 2.10–2.16, 2.38).
- **Who:** Christian Canu Højgaard, Martijn Naaijer (CACCHT project), with Tania Notarius (University of the Free
  State) and volunteer assistants (Polis, Jerusalem).
- **Version:** v0.2.8 (2026-07-15), Zenodo record 21380577; concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10695308.
  URL: https://zenodo.org/records/21380577 (zip: `DT-UCPH/cuc-v0.2.8.zip`, 5.0 MB).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-23 (Round 4 run 1).
- **Licence:** **CC BY-NC 4.0** (v0.1.7 and earlier were CC BY 4.0). The content is git-ignored, like eBL
  (guidelines §8); only this README is committed. Our translations (original work) are committed, with attribution.
- **What is here (all git-ignored, re-obtainable):**
  - `cuc-v0.2.8.zip` – the release as downloaded;
  - `cuc-v0.2.8-unzipped/DT-UCPH-cuc-0408967/` – extracted (the `tf/0.2.8/` dataset, `auto_parsing/`, `reviewed/`,
    `lexicon_and_grammar/ugaritic_lexicon.txt` – the DULAT-based lexicon the parser uses);
  - `text/KTU-1.x.md` – generated by `python3 -I tools/cuc2md.py md sources/CUC/cuc-v0.2.8-unzipped/DT-UCPH-cuc-0408967
    <tablet…>`: each KTU line with the folder's bracket marks and CUC's parses (merged per lemma).
  Restore: `python3 -I tools/sources_sync.py fetch --only '^CUC/'`, then the `cuc2md.py md` line above.
- **Line numbering:** KTU's (column in Roman numerals : line), e.g. `III:38`; one-column tablets by line number
  (`1.23:62`). This is the numbering of the translations, and of every modern translation (Smith & Pitard, Pardee,
  Wyatt), so no concordance is needed. CUC's line division follows KTU³.
- **Notes on the format** (found 2026-10-23, `tools/cuc2md.py`): the `cert` feature is `True` for a **certain** letter
  and `False` for a damaged one (KTU's italics): restored letters are always `False`. Each word's slots end with two
  blank slots that stand for its trailer (the divider `.` or a space); blank *restored* slots inside a word's span are a
  lost stretch (`[…]`). `x` is a lost letter, `alt` an alternative reading of a sign (`b/d`).
- **Not in CUC:** KTU 1.8 (= part of 1.3 in KTU³), 1.9–1.13 (so not 1.12 "Baal and the Devourers"), 1.148.
- **Usefulness:** high. The text is KTU³'s, the best published reading (EUPT's new edition does not yet cover the Baal
  Cycle: checked 2026-10-23). The parses are a first pass only: they list several POS candidates per form and have
  errors (e.g. *l* as "no" in 1.3 III 38; *aqht* as "Most Obedient"): **every form must be parsed by the translator**.
