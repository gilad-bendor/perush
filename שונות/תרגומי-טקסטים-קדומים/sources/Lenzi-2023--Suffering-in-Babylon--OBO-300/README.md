## Usefulness
**Rank 3 (translation) and rank 4 (philology) for the whole of Ludlul**, the most recent full study (after Oshima 2014 and Annus & Lenzi 2010), and the only one we have. Its chapters 5–9 are the best guide we have to Ludlul on its own terms (divination, the exorcist, the body, the prayers, the ancient commentary). Consult it *after* forming your own reading of each line (guidelines §3).
