## Contents relevant here
- pp. 9–70: **"A New Creation and Deluge Text"** — transliteration, translation and commentary of CBS 10673 (Poebel's "Ziugiddu" = Ziusudra), including discussion of An, Enlil, Enki as creators and of the age of the tablet.
- pp. 73 ff.: "New Lists of Kings" (early Sumerian King List fragments) and the chronological system — background to SKL / Berossus' antediluvian kings.

## Line numbering
By tablet column (I–VI) and line, Poebel's own; correlate with ETCSL segments via Civil 1969 / ETCSL bibliography.

## Assessment
Historically essential (first edition, 1914) and the source of the copy used by all later editors; philologically superseded by Civil 1969, Jacobsen 1981 and ETCSL. OCR of transliteration is poor — use the PDF page images.
