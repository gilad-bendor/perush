# Tallqvist 1895 – *Die assyrische Beschwörungsserie Maqlû*

- **Title:** Knut L. Tallqvist, *Die assyrische Beschwörungsserie Maqlû nach den Originalen im British Museum herausgegeben*, vol. I: Einleitung, Umschrift, Übersetzung, Erläuterungen (Acta Societatis Scientiarum Fennicae XX/6, Helsingfors 1895; archive.org dates the scan "1900").
- **URL:** https://archive.org/details/dieassyrischebes00tall (University of Toronto scan, 2010); the text file: https://archive.org/download/dieassyrischebes00tall/dieassyrischebes00tall_djvu.txt
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-23.
- **License:** public domain (published 1895; the author died 1949). Committed.
- **In this folder:** `dieassyrischebes00tall_djvu.txt` – archive.org's OCR of the whole volume (German introduction, transliteration, German translation, notes). The PDF (13.5 MB) is not kept: re-download it from the URL.
- **Line numbering:** Tallqvist's tablets I–VIII follow the Nineveh copies known in 1895; his line numbers often differ from Abusch's eBL edition (which adds many manuscripts, restorations and `a`-lines). Align by content.
- **Usefulness:** rank 5 (guidelines §3): the first edition and translation, for the history of interpretation and as a literal check of the German only. **Never take a reading from it** (and never from its OCR, which garbles the transliteration): eBL's edition is the base text. No open modern second translation of Maqlû exists (Abusch 2015 / 2016 and Meier 1937 are not open), so eBL's English (Abusch) is the only rank-3 translation; the translators compare it with their own reading, and cmawro's glossary senses help with single words.
