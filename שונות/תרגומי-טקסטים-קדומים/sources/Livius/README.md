## Line numbering
The `[11.n]` markers roughly follow the traditional (Thompson/Kovacs) count. They are close to George up to ~150 and then about 1 line behind (e.g. [11.202] "Previously Ut-napištim was a human being" = George XI 203).

## Assessment
This is the same text as Kovacs, but the line markers make it the handiest way to locate Kovacs lines. It adds no independent philological value.
