## Line numbering
Jensen uses Haupt's numbering (close to Thompson's; behind George by up to ~10 lines in the 190s).

## Assessment
This was the first full scientific transliteration and translation of the whole SB epic and the foundation for Ungnad and others. Its readings are obsolete: names such as Ṣit-napištim/UT-napištim, Ninib, Ia-bani (= Enkidu), Irkalla; Jensen's eccentric theories. It is of historical value only, but it is the edition Tchernichovsky's circle knew (Simchoni cites it).
