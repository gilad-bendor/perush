## Contents
Day argues that Genesis (both J and P) depends on **Atrahasis** rather than on Gilgamesh XI. He compares Berossus, the Sumerian flood story and Gilgamesh XI (bird scene; XI 49 and 197 "Atra-hasis"). Editor's notes point to the two Hebrew versions of the epic: **Shifra & Klein 1996** and **Uri Gabbay & Tamar Weiss, *המיתולוגיה הבבלית* (Tel Aviv: Mapa, 2003)**. The Atrahasis quotation in the article is from Gabbay–Weiss p. 28.
