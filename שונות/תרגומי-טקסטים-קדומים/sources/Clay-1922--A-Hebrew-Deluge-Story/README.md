## Contents
Ch. I "An Ancient Hebrew Deluge Story": new edition, with copy and photographs, of the dated **OB Morgan tablet** (MLC 1889 = BRM 4, 1, CDLI P363404; first published by Scheil 1898; = Lambert & Millard Ms. **B**, Atra-ḫasīs Tablet II, copied in Ammi-ṣaduqa's reign). Appendix: transliterations and translations of **A** (the Morgan tablet), **B** (the "late redaction" = NA Assyrian recension K 3399+, CT 15, 49), **C** (the small BM fragment with Ea's instructions on building and loading the ship = DT 42, Ms. W), **D** (Sumerian flood story, Nippur). Also discusses Hilprecht's Nippur fragment (CBS 13532) and Berossos.

## Line numbering
Clay's own column/line numbering for each fragment; predates the Lambert & Millard reconstruction and the Tablet I–III arrangement.

## Assessment
Historically important (first edition of the OB Atra-ḫasīs II tablet; hand-copies/photos of the Morgan tablet are still useful), but the readings and the "Hebrew" thesis are long obsolete. Use only for its plates and history of scholarship, not as a base text.
