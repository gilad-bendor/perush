## Files (git-ignored)
- `Mittermayer_2009_Enmerkara_und_der_Herr_von_Arata.pdf` — the book: introduction (history of research, the
  manuscripts, Arata, the "Namšub des Nudimmud", the invention of writing), **9. Rekonstruierter Text mit Übersetzung**
  (pp. 114–153: composite and German translation), **10. Partitur** (pp. 154–219: the score of all manuscripts),
  **11. Kommentar** (pp. 220 ff.), indexes, plates (copies of the Ur tablets UET 6).
- `mittermayer-2009.txt` — `pdftotext -layout` of the PDF. **The OCR layer is poor for the Sumerian** (spaced or
  merged signs, indices as plain digits, misread diacritics); the German translation and the commentary are mostly
  readable. Never take a Sumerian reading from this text file without checking the PDF page.

## Line numbering
Mittermayer numbers the composite 1–636+ like ETCSL (which follows Cohen 1973 and Vanstiphout); her notes flag the
lines a single manuscript adds (e.g. after l. 11 = ETCSL 11A). Align by content where they differ.

## Usefulness
**The current critical edition** (rank 1 for readings, rank 3 for the translation, rank 4 for the commentary) of
Enmerkar and the Lord of Aratta: used in `אגדות-מלכים/1-אנמרכר-ואדון-ארטה/` as the second opinion on every line
(after our own reading of ETCSL + ORACC), and for the cruxes (the Nudimmud spell 134–155, the invention of writing
500–506, the end 588–636).
