## Usefulness
Rank 4: the ancient scholars' own explanations of rare words of the two poems ("X = Y"), a window onto how Babylonian scholars read these texts (guidelines §1, second purpose). Use them in the apparatus where a line's rare word is explained by the ancient commentary.
