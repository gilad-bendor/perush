# Millard 1967 — "A New Babylonian 'Genesis' Story"

- **Citation:** A. R. Millard, "A New Babylonian 'Genesis' Story" (The Tyndale Biblical Archaeology Lecture, 1966), *Tyndale Bulletin* 18 (1967): 3–18. DOI: https://doi.org/10.53751/001c.30683
- **URL:** https://tyndalebulletin.org/article/30683-a-new-babylonian-genesis-story.pdf
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Access basis:** Publisher open access (Tyndale Bulletin's full back archive is free on tyndalebulletin.org).

**Relevance:** Written just after Lambert & Millard's reconstruction of Atrahasis (Millard co-edited the 1969 edition). It sets out Atrahasis as a creation-to-flood "primeval history" parallel to Gen 1–9, compares it point by point with Gilgamesh XI and Genesis, and argues that Genesis was not borrowed from any Babylonian text known to us. It is the classic early English statement of the Atrahasis ↔ Genesis comparison.
