# Hammerly-Dupuy 1968 — "Some Observations on the Assyro-Babylonian and Sumerian Flood Stories"

- **Citation:** Daniel Hammerly-Dupuy, "Some Observations on the Assyro-Babylonian and Sumerian Flood Stories," *Andrews University Seminary Studies* 6/1 (1968): 1–18.
- **URL:** https://www.andrews.edu/library/car/cardigital/Periodicals/AUSS/1968-1/1968-1-01.pdf (AUSS archive: https://digitalcommons.andrews.edu/auss/)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Access basis:** Publisher's free archive (Andrews University digitizes the whole AUSS back run).

**Relevance:** A survey of the cuneiform flood texts known before Lambert & Millard: the Sumerian (Ziusudra) story, the OB and LB Atrahasis fragments, Gilgamesh XI and Berossus. It covers their relative dates and their points of agreement with and difference from Genesis. It is useful for the history of the comparison and for Berossus, but it is out of date on the texts.
