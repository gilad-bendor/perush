# Clines 1972–3 — "Noah's Flood I: The Theology of the Flood Narrative"

- **Citation:** David J. A. Clines, "Noah's Flood: I: The Theology of the Flood Narrative," *Faith and Thought* 100/2 (1972–73): 128–142.
- **URL:** https://biblicalstudies.org.uk/pdf/ft/100-2_128.pdf (the same file is on mail.biblicalstudies.org.uk)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Access basis:** Free on biblicalstudies.org.uk (Rob Bradshaw), which digitizes *Faith and Thought* with the permission of the Victoria Institute.

**Relevance:** A close reading of the theology of Gen 6–9: the pattern of sin, judgment and mitigation, the flood as un-creation and re-creation, and the reversal of Gen 1. Clines contrasts it at points with the Mesopotamian flood stories. It is good for the *meaning* of the differences, such as moral cause versus divine noise or caprice, and the covenant.
