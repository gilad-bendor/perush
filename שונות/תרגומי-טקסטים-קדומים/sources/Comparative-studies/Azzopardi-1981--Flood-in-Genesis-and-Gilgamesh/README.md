# Azzopardi 1981 — "The Flood in Genesis and the Epic of Gilgamesh"

- **Citation:** John Azzopardi S.J., "The Flood in Genesis and the Epic of Gilgamesh," *Melita Theologica* 32/1–2 (1981): 5 pp.
- **URL:** https://www.um.edu.mt/library/oar/bitstream/123456789/32694/1/The_flood_in_genesis_and_the_epic_of_Gilgamesh.pdf (record: https://www.um.edu.mt/library/oar/handle/123456789/32694)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Access basis:** Institutional repository (University of Malta OAR@UM), free download.

**Relevance:** A short, student-level comparison of Gen 6:5–9:17 with Gilgamesh XI covering the J/P doublets, the shared sequence of motifs (building the boat, the animals, the flood, the birds, the sacrifice) and the contrast between the gods "gathering like flies" and Gen 8:21–22. It is useful as a compact checklist of motifs, but it is theologically framed and dated.
