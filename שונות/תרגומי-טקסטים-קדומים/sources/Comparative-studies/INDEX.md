# Comparative studies: Mesopotamian flood accounts ↔ Genesis 6–9

Compiled 2026-10-16. **Access** key:
- **OA-downloaded**: open-access, public-domain or rights-holder copy, saved locally.
- **in-repo**: already present in a sibling folder under `../`.
- **free-web**: free to read online but not openly licensed, so not downloaded.
- **login-gated**: academia.edu, ResearchGate or similar.
- **paid**: publisher or JSTOR paywall.
- **library-only**: print only.
- **unofficial copy**: a third-party upload exists but its legality is unclear, so it was not downloaded or linked as a source.

"(unverified)" means the bibliographic or access details come from catalogues or search snippets and were not checked against the item itself.
