# Jacobsen 1939 — *The Sumerian King List* (AS 11)

- **Citation:** Thorkild Jacobsen, *The Sumerian King List*, Assyriological Studies 11 (Chicago: University of Chicago Press / Oriental Institute, 1939; 4th impr. 1973).
- **URL:** https://isac.uchicago.edu/publications/sumerian-king-list (PDF on isac-assets.s3.amazonaws.com)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Access basis:** Free PDF distributed by the publisher (UChicago ISAC, formerly the Oriental Institute, Electronic Publications Initiative).

**Relevance:** The standard edition of the King List, including the antediluvian kings and the formula "then the flood swept over". It is background for Gen 5 (ten antediluvian generations, long lifespans) → the flood → Gen 11, and for the Ziusudra and Berossus traditions. It is not a flood-comparison study as such.
