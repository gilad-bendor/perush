# Battenfield 1971 — "Atra-hasis: A Survey"

- **Citation:** James R. Battenfield, "Atra-hasis: A Survey," *Grace Theological Journal* 12/2 (Spring 1971): 3–22.
- **URL:** https://biblicalelearning.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Battenfield-Atra-Hasis-GTJ.pdf
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16
- **Access basis:** Free digitization by Ted Hildebrandt (Gordon College / biblicalelearning.org). The PDF header says "Copyright © 1971 by Grace Theological Seminary. Cited with permission."

**Relevance:** A survey of Atrahasis written right after the 1969 Lambert–Millard edition: its plot, the noise motif, creation of man from clay and the blood of a slain god, and the flood. It compares the work with Gilgamesh XI and Gen 1–9, from a conservative evangelical perspective, with a useful bibliography of the period.
