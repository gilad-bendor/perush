## Line numbering
King's: col. I 1–32 (K 3401 + Sm 2118 obverse, with restorations and variants from K 4470 col. I and BM 47449) and
col. II 1–17 (K 4470 col. II). Westenholz 1997 (*Legends of the Kings of Akkade*, text 2) and Lewis 1980 number the
lines continuously and add K 7249 (CT 46 46), which King did not know; their numbering is not available to us
(records only), so `אגדות-מלכים/2-…/` uses King's labels `I.1` … `II.17`.

## Usefulness
**Base text** (rank 5 as an edition, but the only open transliteration of the legend reachable on 2026-10-21) of
`אגדות-מלכים/2-אגדת-הולדת-סרגון/`. Its readings are 1907 readings (e.g. *e-ni-tum* translated "lowly", the gardener
logogram unread, *{amêlu}nâḳ-mê* for LÚ.A.BAL, l. 9 *ḫi-ib-[…]-li-[…]*): every one is checked against the eBL
dictionary and, where possible, against eBL's transliteration of BM 47449 (`../eBL-fragmentarium--Sargon-Birth-Legend/`),
and the modern readings known only from translations are flagged as such in the annotated file. The current editions
(Westenholz 1997; Lewis 1980) are records only (`../_records-only--Legends-of-kings/`).
