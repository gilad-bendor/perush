# The Legend of Sargon, King of Agade – King 1907, vol. II, Appendix I (pp. 87–96)

L. W. King, *Chronicles concerning Early Babylonian Kings, including records of the early history of the Kassites and
the country of the sea*, vol. II: *Texts and Translations* (Studies in Eastern History III; London: Luzac, 1907),
Appendix I, "The Legend of Sargon, King of Agade. [K. 3401 + S. 2118, K. 4470, and No. 47449.]". Public domain.

**Transcribed by hand from the page images** (`page-images/p087.png` … `p096.png`, rendered from the PDF in this
folder), 2026-10-21, **not** from the PDF's OCR layer (which garbles the cuneiform type and many readings). King's
cuneiform type is not reproduced; his transliteration, his translation and his footnotes are given exactly, line by
line, in modern Unicode:
- King's superscript determinatives are written in braces: `{m}` (personal name), `{amêlu}` (LÚ), `{ālu}` (URU),
  `{nâru}` (ÍD), `{ilu}` (DINGIR), `{KI}`, `{pl}` (plural marker). The italic/roman distinction of the print is not
  kept: King prints in roman (= a logogram in the cuneiform, read by him) e.g. *šarru*, *iddî*, *bâbi*, *abi*, *aḫ*,
  *nâru*, *šanâti*, *nišê*, *šadê*, *erû*, *elûti*, *šaplûti*, *rabî(i)*, *arki*, *eli*, *Puratti*, *Dilmun*,
  *Dûr-ilu*, and the numerals *III*, *IV*. These are King's readings of logograms, not syllabic spellings.
- `ḳ` = King's q (dotted k); `â ê î û` as printed; `[ ]` restorations as printed (King: "Conjectural restorations are
  placed within brackets"); `[. . .]` / `.....` = gaps or signs King leaves unread.
- Footnote numbers are King's (renumbered per page in the print); they are given after the line they belong to.

King's statement of method (p. 87, n. 1): "In the present edition the text of Col. I is taken from the obverse of
K. 3401 + S. 2118 with restorations and variant readings from K. 4470, Col. I, and No. 47449; Col. II is taken from
the second column of K. 4470." The copies: CT 13 pl. 42 f.; earlier 3 R 4 no. 7 (K 3401); Pinches, PSBA 18 (1896)
257 (No. 47449 = BM 47449).

## Col. I (K 3401 + Sm 2118, with K 4470 col. I and BM 47449)

**I 1** Šarru-ukîn² šarru dan-nu šar A-ga-de{KI} a-na-ku³
- King: "Sargon, the mighty king, the king of Agade, am I."
- n. 2: No. 47449 [two signs], *Šarru-ukîn*. n. 3: No. 47449 omits *a-na-ku*.

**I 2** um-mi¹ e-ni-tum a-bi ul i-di²
- King: "My mother was lowly, my father I knew not,"
- n. 1: No. 47449 [sign], *ummi*. n. 2: No. 47449 [signs], *abu la i-ši*, "a father I had not."

**I 3** aḫ³ abi-ia i-ra-mi ša-da-a⁴
- King: "and the brother of my father dwelleth in the mountain."
- n. 3: No. 47449 [signs], *a-ḫu*. n. 4: No. 47449 [signs, one in brackets], *šadâ i-ra-am-mu*. In K. 3401 + S. 2118,
  ll. 2 and 3 are written in one line, divided by [a divider sign].

**I 4** a-li {ālu}A-zu-pi-ra-a-ni⁵ ša i-na a-ḫi {nâru}Puratti šak-nu
- King: "My city is Azupirânu, which lieth on the bank of the Euphrates."
- n. 5: K. 4470 [signs], *{ālu}A-zu-pi-ra-nu*.

**I 5** i-ra-an-ni um-mu⁶ e-ni-tum i-na pu-uz-ri u-lid-an-ni⁷
- King: "My lowly mother conceived me, in secret she brought me forth."
- n. 6: K. 4470 [signs], *um-mi*; No. 47449 [sign], *ummu*. n. 7: No. 47449 [signs], *ina pu-uz-zu u-lid-da-an-ni*;
  [*zu*] is probably a mistake of the scribe for [*ri*].

**I 6** iš-kun-an-ni i-na ḳup-pi ša šu-ri¹ i-na² iddî bâbi-ia ip-ḫi
- King: "She set me in a basket of rushes, with bitumen she closed my door;"
- n. 1: K. 4470 [signs], *šu-u-[ri]*; No. 47449 [signs], *iš-ku-na-an-ni ina ḳu-up-pu ša šu-u-šu*. There is a word
  *šûšu*, "sprout" or "twig," but [*šu*] is probably a mistake of the scribe for [*ri*]. n. 2: No. 47449 [sign], *ina*.

**I 7** id-dan-ni³ a-na [nâ]ri ša la e-li-e-a
- King: "she cast me into¹ the river, which (rose) not over me."
- n. 3 (King's translation n. 1): K. 4470 [signs], *id-dan-an-ni*, "she gave me to the river."

**I 8** iš-ša-an-ni nâru a-na eli {m}Ak-ki {amêlu}nâḳ-mê u-bil-an-ni⁴
- King: "The river bore me up, unto Akki, the irrigator it carried me."
- n. 4: K. 4470 reads [signs], *u-bi-la-an-ni*.

**I 9** {m}Ak-ki {amêlu}nâḳ-mê i-na ḫi-ib-[. .]-li-[. .] lu-u¹ u-še-la-an-ni
- King: "Akki, the irrigator, with . . [. . .] lifted me out,"
- n. 1: So K. 4470; K. 3041 [sic, for 3401] + S. 2118 omits [*u*].

**I 10** {m}Ak-ki {amêlu}nâḳ-mê a-na ma-ru-ti-šu [. . . . . . . . . .] u-rab-ban²-ni
- King: "Akki, the irrigator, as his own son [. . . . .] reared me,"
- n. 2: K. 4470 reads [signs], *ba-an*.

**I 11** {m}Ak-ki {amêlu}nâḳ-mê a-na {amêlu} . . . . . -ti-šu lu-u [iš]-kun-[an-ni]
- King: "Akki, the irrigator, as his gardener appointed me."
- (King leaves the logogram after *{amêlu}* untransliterated in ll. 11–12; his translation "gardener" implies the
  reading LÚ.NU.GIŠ.SAR/KIRI₆ = *nukaribbu*.)

**I 12** i-na³ {amêlu} . . . . . -ti-ia {ilu}Iš-tar lu-u i-ra-man-ni-ma
- King: "While I was a gardener the goddess Ishtar loved me,"
- n. 3: K. 3401 + S. 2118 probably reads [sign], *ina*.

**I 13** [. .]IV šanâti{pl} šarru-u-ta lu-u e-pu-uš
- King: "and for [. . . .]-four years I ruled the kingdom."

**I 14** [nišê]{pl} ṣalmât ḳaḳḳadi¹ lu-u a-be-el lu-u aš-[pur]
- King: "The black-headed [people]s I ruled, I gov[erned];"
- n. 1: K. 4470 [signs], *ṣal-mat ḳaḳ-ḳadi*.

**I 15** [šadê](e)² dannûti{pl} ina aḳ-ḳul-la-te ša erû{pl} lu-u ub-[bit]
- King: "mighty [mountain]s with axes of bronze did I des[troy]."
- n. 2: K. 4470 [. . .] [sign], *[šadî](i)*.

**I 16** [lu-u] e-dil-li ša-di-i³ e-lu-[ti]
- King: "I bolted fast the upp[er] mountains;"
- n. 3: K. 4470 [signs], *šad-di-i*.

**I 17** [lu-u] at-ta-[nab]¹-lak-ka-ta ša-di-i šap-l[u-ti]
- King: "I burst through the low[er] mountains."
- n. 1: "It is possible that the text is corrupt and that *tab* ([sign]) has been written by mistake for *nab*
  ([sign]); otherwise we must assume the existence of an Ittatafal form of the verb. We may compare an Iftatael form
  *al-ta-tap-pa-rak-ka* (in K. 3426, Obv., l. 14, published in *Cuneiform Texts*, Pt. XIII, pl. 48); here also the
  scribe may have made a mistake, writing [signs] for [sign], which would give the regular Ifteal form
  *al-ta-ap-rak-ka*."
