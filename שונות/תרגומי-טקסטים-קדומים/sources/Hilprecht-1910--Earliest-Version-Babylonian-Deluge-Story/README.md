## Contents
Edition (transliteration, translation, photo and autograph plate "at the end of the book") of the **Nippur fragment CBS 13532** (= Lambert & Millard / Wasserman Ms. **J**, MB; Hilprecht dated it c. 2100 BC). The fragment contains Ea's instructions to build the boat ("…a great ship build… let its name be 'Preserver of life'").

## Line numbering
Hilprecht's obverse/reverse line numbers (the sides are now inverted relative to modern editions: Hilprecht's "Reverse" is the inscribed flood passage). Modern numbering: Wasserman 2020 "J" lines; eBL CBS.13532 `@reverse 1'–14'`.

## Assessment
Of historical value only (plate/photo still useful; transliteration and dating are outdated, and the book's treatment of the "temple library" is controversial). For the text use Wasserman 2020 / SEAL no. 1562 / eBL `CBS.13532.atf`.
