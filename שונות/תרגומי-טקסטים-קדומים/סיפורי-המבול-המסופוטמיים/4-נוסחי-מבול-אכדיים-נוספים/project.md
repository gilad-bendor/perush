# Project: The other Akkadian flood versions → Hebrew

Read `../../translation-guidelines.md` first, then `../corpus.md` and the shared `../renderings.md`. This file holds only what is specific to this project.

## Scope
Every Akkadian flood text **other than** the OB Sippar Atra-ḫasīs III (project 1) and Gilgameš XI (project 2), each translated in full as a text of its own, one section per manuscript (= per recension):

| § | Siglum (Wasserman 2020) | Tablet | Date, place | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ark** | Ark Tablet (Simmonds coll.; cast BM) | late OB or early MB (c. 1750–1600 BCE?), unknown provenance | Ea's address to the wall; building instructions for a **round** rope boat (coracle); ribs, stanchions, cabins; bitumen inside and out; loading; the hero's anxiety; **animals "two by two"**; provisions; "seal her door". 60 lines, first person after l. 12. |
| 2 | **C₀** | Schøyen MS 5108 (CUSAS 10, 2), col. iv | early OB, Larsa area? | The gods' decision; Atra-ḫasīs kneels and weeps before Ea; Ea's answer. iv 1′–19′. (iv 1′–7′ are also in Atr. II's appendix: identical Hebrew.) |
| 3 | **J** | CBS 13532 (Nippur) | MB | Ea's instructions: a reed boat named "Life-Keeper", a strong roof, beasts and birds, family. |

## Decisions
1. **One section per manuscript**, each with a short Hebrew introduction. In the clean translation each section is a text of its own.
2. **Shared lines.** Where the Akkadian is identical to Gilg. XI or Atra-ḫasīs (OB I–III, C₀ iv 1′–7′ in Atr. II's appendix), the Hebrew is identical; where it differs, it differs exactly there. Marked `[מקבילה: …]`.
3. **Echo control decisions specific to these texts** (details in `renderings.md`):
   - Ark 18–19 *kīdātiša / qiribša* → `לְחוּצוֹתֶיהָ / לְקִרְבָּהּ`: "outside" is the only natural Hebrew word for *kīdu* [equivalent with `מִחוּץ`]; *qerbu* / `קֶרֶב` is a real cognate, so `מִבַּיִת` is **not** borrowed.
   - Ark 52 *šanā* → `שְׁנַיִם שְׁנַיִם`: *šanā* is the distributive of *šina* "two", cognate of `שְׁנַיִם`; Hebrew expresses the distributive by repetition. Real [cognate], not a borrowed echo. Reading uncertain (`⌈ša⌉-na`), marked.
   - Ark 51 *nammaštû* → `הַנָּע (בַּשָּׂדֶה)`, not `רֶמֶשׂ`: "what moves" is the etymological sense (*namāšu* "move"); `רֶמֶשׂ` would pre-empt the comparison. The parallel with `הָרֶמֶשׂ` is noted [equivalent].
   - J r. 9′ / W 3′ *ṣulūlu*, *ṣullulu* → `קוֹרָה`, `קֵרָה` (Gilg. XI 31 `קָרֵה`): roofing, not sealing.
   - *naṣāru* → `נָצַר` [cognate] (Atr. II); J r. 8′ *nāṣirat napištim* → `נוֹצֶרֶת נֶפֶשׁ` (the boat's name; cf. `וְנֹצֵר נַפְשְׁךָ`, משלי כד:יב).
   - *eṣēru / uṣurtu / eṣertu* → `צָר / צוּרָה` (Atr. I's choice: cognate of יצר; cf. `צוּרַת הַבַּיִת`, יחזקאל מג:יא). Gilg. XI 60 used `שִׂרְטֵט`; changed to `צַרְתִּי` in the harmonization pass.
   - W 6′ *bāba turru* "close the door" → `הָגֵף`, not `סְגֹר` (`וַיִּסְגֹּר`, ז:טז): the parallel is noted [divergence/equivalent].
   - I₂ 1 *rēš arḫi* → `רֹאשׁ הַיֶּרַח` (*warḫu* = ירח [cognate], as Atr. II `יוֹם הַיָּרֵחַ`), not `רֹאשׁ הַחֹדֶשׁ`.
   - z v 18′ *tādirti libbi* → `קַדְרוּת לֵב` (*adāru* "be dark" = קדר), not `עִצְּבוֹן` (which would echo ה:כט; ו:ו).
   - z v 4′–8′ *kūm* → `בִּמְקוֹם אֲשֶׁר`, distinct from Gilg. XI *ammaku* `תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר`.
4. **Numbers.** *šār* (ŠÁR) = `שָׁאר` (3,600; shared with Gilg. XI). *šūši* "sixty" is rendered by its value (1 *šūši* = שישים, 5 *šūši* = שלוש מאות), with the notation in the apparatus. Units supplied by Wasserman are in ( ): (כּוֹר), (קוּ).
5. **Names.** Sîn (Ark 45) = `סִין`; Nuʿme-Rašap (I r. 5′) = `נֻעְמֵי־רַשַׁף`; Antu = `אַנְתּוּ`; Ninlil = `נִינְלִיל`; Anzû = `אַנְזוּ`; Zisudra (z v 17′) = shared `זִיוּסוּדְרָה` (spelling noted); *Ut-napištim* = shared `אוּתְנַפִּשְׁתִּי` (z's mimated form is the source of the common `אותנפישתים`: noted).
6. **I₂ 2:** translated with Wasserman (*ina aḫiya* "at my side"); the eBL / NABU 2008/70 collation *ina šuttīya* "in my dream" is given in a footnote, since it changes the "warning" column of the synopsis.
7. **MB Babylon (§6):** MM 818 (= eBL MB Bab₂) parallels Atra-ḫasīs OB I 38–67 and is already translated, line by line, in `../1-אתרחסיס/` (Tablet I, ll. 51–56 filled from it). VAT 17099 (SEAL 1560, Tablet II) and SEAL 27123/27126 have no text in SEAL. They are listed in the annotated file, not re-translated.

Observations for the commentary:
1. **Inside and out** (`מִבַּיִת וּמִחוּץ`, 6:14) and **two by two** (`שְׁנַיִם שְׁנַיִם`, 7:9, 15) are attested in Mesopotamia **only in the Ark Tablet** (18–19, 52); the god **sending the animals** to the hero (`יָבֹאוּ אֵלֶיךָ`, 6:20) **only in W 10′**; a **window made and then a bird sent** (8:6–8) **only in I₂** (and, less closely, Gilg. XI 137); **"no more flood"** (9:11) **only in z v 13′**. Each of Genesis's "unique" details has a parallel in one minor recension, never in Gilg. XI.
2. **Who shuts the door**: the hero in every Mesopotamian version where it survives (OB III, Ark, W, U, Gilg. XI); in Genesis, God.
3. **The boat's shape**: round in OB III(?), Ark and W; a cube (or circle) in Gilg. XI; a long ship in Berossus (5×2) and Genesis (6×1).
4. **The date**: the Mesopotamian versions tie the flood to the moon's disappearance (C₀, OB III, Ark, I₂, W); Genesis and Berossus give a calendar date.
5. **The warning**: a dream in OB III, Gilg. XI, Berossus (and possibly J, I₂); a wall in OB III, Ark, I, U, Gilg. XI, Sumerian; direct speech in C₀ and W (and Genesis).

## Lessons for the guidelines
- **Translate the minor recensions together with the main texts.** Five of Genesis's "unique" flood details (inside and out; two by two; animals sent to the hero; a window made and then a bird sent; no more flood) are each attested in only one small tablet (Ark, W, I₂, z). A comparison that uses Gilg. XI alone misses them.
- **eBL corpus chapters can quietly re-collate a line.** I₂ 2 *ina šuttīya* "in my dream" (eBL, NABU 2008/70) is not in Wasserman 2020, and it changes a motif. Always compare the base edition with the eBL chapter of the same manuscript.
- **The eBL Fragmentarium has unpublished identifications** (BM 38917: a late duplicate of W with new lines). Search it by genre before declaring a corpus complete (`api/fragments/query?genre=…`).
- **Shared-line coordination across parallel sessions:** draft with your own Hebrew, keep a list of every line that touches another project, and re-sync at the end by exact string comparison. Lemma-level consistency (not only identical lines) matters too: Atr. III and the Ark Tablet both have *nammaššû*.
- The Bible-quote checker normalizes `־` but **not the hyphen-minus** that the Psalms/Proverbs files use for maqaf: quotations from those books must keep the `-`.
