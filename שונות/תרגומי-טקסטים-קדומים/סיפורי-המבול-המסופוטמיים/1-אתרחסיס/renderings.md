# Renderings: Atra-ḫasīs (project level)

The corpus-level `../renderings.md` binds first. This file holds words and formulas used in Atra-ḫasīs and not in the shared file.
Each tablet's translator edits only that tablet's section. **Formulas that recur across the tablets** (the noise passage, the plague formula, the elders/heralds passage, the speech formulas) are fixed at their **first occurrence**, in Tablet I, and the later tablets follow them; a later tablet that needs a change should note it in its own section and in `project.md`, so the three tablets can be aligned.

Columns: Akkadian | Hebrew | rejected alternatives | reason / tag | lines.
