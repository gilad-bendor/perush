1. **sīrāšu / kurunnu (Gilg. XI 73).** Giving `סֹבֶא` to *sīrāšu* takes the word that Gilg. XI used for *kurunnu*, and the shared file gives *kurunnu* nothing. I used `סֹבֶא מובחר` for *kurunnu* ("fine beer"). Please add a *kurunnu* row.
2. **tabāku = `יָצַק` for solids (Gilg. XI 160).** *attabak qanâ erēna asa*: "I heaped reed, cedar and myrtle". `יצקתי קנה, ארז והדס` is odd Hebrew, since `יָצַק` is used of liquids and metal. Applied as decided. Suggest `יָצַק` for liquids (XI 66) and `צָבַר` or `עָרַם` for solids (XI 160), or a note.
3. **naṣāru = `נָצַר` vs. the formula's `שְׁמֹר`.** The shared formula row renders *šuṣṣirī* (naṣāru Š, Atr. III i 21) `שְׁמֹר`, while §3a says *naṣāru* = `נָצַר`. I kept `שמור` in i 19 and i 21 (the formula), and changed vi 19 and viii 17 to `נצר`. This breaks the Tablet III translator's frame (`שמר` … `שמר`). One rule is needed.
4. **manzāzu outside the dove passage (Erra I 134).** `מקום עמידתם של כוכבי השמים` is heavy. The row was made to avoid `מָנוֹחַ` in Gilg. XI 150, 153. Consider limiting it to that sense.
5. **kibrāti = `קְצוֹת הָאָרֶץ` (Gilg. XI 140, Ugarit I 3).** "The earth" is not in the Akkadian. Applied without parentheses, as an idiomatic rendering of the plural. If §6.8 is meant strictly, it should be `קצות (הארץ)`.
