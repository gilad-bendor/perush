# Project 3 – the reaper's plea and the calendar

- **Texts:** (MH) the Mesad Hashavyahu / Yavne-Yam ostracon (KAI 200), c. 630 BCE, 14 lines; (GZ) the Gezer calendar
  (KAI 182), 10th c. BCE, 7 lines + the marginal signature (`m`).
- **Bases:** English Wikipedia ("Yavne-Yam ostracon", "Gezer calendar"; CC BY-SA 4.0) →
  `../../sources/Wikipedia--Hebrew-inscriptions/`. MH = Naveh's 1960 text in substance (IEJ, ©), as Wikipedia gives it;
  GZ: word division ours.
- **Decision (minor, by the coordinator):** a Wikipedia transcription of an inscription counts as an open base text
  (CC BY-SA, attributed), checked against the photograph-based literature cited in the apparatus; noted as a question
  for the user in the run report.
- **Product:** reading + apparatus.
- **Open:** MH 5 `לפני שבת` ("before the Sabbath" or "before my stopping"); MH 12 `ואמלא` = "and if not" (followed) or
  "and I will fill"; GZ `ירחו` (dual "two months" – followed – or "his month").
