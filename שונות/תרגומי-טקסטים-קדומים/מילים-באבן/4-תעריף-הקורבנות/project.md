# Project 4 – the Marseille sacrificial tariff (CIS I 165, KAI 69)

- **Scope:** all 21 lines (Punic, 4th–3rd c. BCE; Marseille, Musée d'archéologie méditerranéenne). Key `MT`.
- **Base:** G. A. Cooke, *A Text-Book of North-Semitic Inscriptions* (Oxford 1903), no. 42, pp. 112–113 (public domain),
  read by us from the archive.org page images (leaves 140–141; item `textbookofnorths00cookuoft`) →
  `../../sources/Cooke-1903--North-Semitic-Inscriptions/marseille-tariff.tsv`. One row per stone line; numerals `{n}`.
- **Rank 3–5:** Cooke's (= Driver's) translation and notes (pp. 113–120, OCR read per line); English Wikipedia's
  transliteration (after KAI, © – consulted for line division only, not used as a base).
- **Product:** Hebrew translation with the transcription (guidelines §6.7: Punic is translated). Renderings and gates:
  `../renderings.md` §4.
- **Open:** the meaning of `צועת`, `קצרת`, `יצלת`, `שלבמ`, `שצפ`, `חזת`, `אגננ`, `צצ` (transliterated); `בדצ` (`*שׁוֹנָה* מֵ־`, Cooke's "other (?) than") and
  `למבמחסר באטומטא` (`*עוֹד חֲסֵרוֹת* … *בָּאַטּוּמְטָא*`) rendered tentatively; line 1's first word (`ב[עת`, "tariff"?); `אלמ` "the gods" or "the god".
