# Project 1 – the Mesha stele (KAI 181)

- **Scope:** all 34 lines of the stele (Moabite, c. 840 BCE; Louvre AO 5066). Key `MS`.
- **Base:** H. F. B. Compston, *The Inscription on the Stele of Méšaʿ* (London 1919), pp. 11–13, the square-script text
  (public domain; proofread on English Wikisource) → `../../sources/Compston-1919--Mesha-Stele/mesha.tsv`. Line numbers
  = the stone's. Four bracket slips of the Wikisource page (lines 6–7, 10–11, 16–17, 19) fixed against Compston's own
  translation; `הר` (1) read `הד`; `ב[נס` (3) read `ב[מת` (Compston's translation: "High place of salvation").
- **Rank 4 consulted:** Cooke 1903 no. 1 (PD; archive.org); Finkelstein, Naʾaman & Römer 2019 (line 31; HAL, open);
  he.wikisource "מצבת מישע" (CC BY-SA; a Compston-based text with glosses: register reference only).
- **Not obtained:** Langlois 2019 (*Semitica* 61; RTI readings; the HAL record has no file) – records only; Ahituv,
  *הכתב והמכתב* (©).
- **Product:** transcription + cautious vocalized reading + apparatus (no translation; guidelines §6.7).
- **Open:** lines 4 (`השׄלכנ` vs `המלכנ`), 12 (`רית`, `אראל דודה`), 18 (`כ[לי]` vs `[א]ראלי`), 30–31 (`נקד`; "house
  of David" / "Balak"): when Langlois's RTI readings are open, re-check these lines (the reading verifier will show
  every letter that moves).
