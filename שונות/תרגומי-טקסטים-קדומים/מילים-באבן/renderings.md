# מילים באבן – renderings, conventions, gates

Binding for every text of the corpus (guidelines §6.6). The nearest model is the Ugaritic corpus
(`../מיתוסים-אוגריתיים/renderings.md`): a cousin language, where the cognate is chosen whenever the meaning matches,
and the danger is understanding too much.

## 1. Transcription and transliteration
- **Square script, diplomatic:** no final letter forms (the scripts have none); `·` = the original's word divider
  (dot or short stroke); `|` = the Mesha stele's vertical stroke; `[ ]` = restored (the base edition's restorations);
  `[...]` / `[..]` = lost letters; a letter followed by U+05C4 `ׄ` = an uncertain letter (Compston's overline; Wikipedia's
  "h/w"); `{n}` = a numeral sign (Punic), written in digits.
- **Latin:** generated by `stone_tools.py translit` (ʾ b g d h w z ḥ ṭ y k l m n s ʿ p ṣ q r š t; an uncertain letter
  with a ring above, `å`). Never typed by hand.

## 2. The vocalized reading (Hebrew, Moabite)
- The consonants are the inscription's, in order; the vowels are a **proposal** written with Tiberian signs as notation.
  Final letter forms, maqaf, spaces and punctuation are ours. A word broken across a line ends / begins with `־`.
- **Moabite forms vocalized as Moabite:** plural ‎-īn (`מְלָכִן`, `שְׁלֹשִׁן`, `אֲלָפִן`); 3ms suffix ‎-ō(h) written ה
  (`אַרְצֹה`, `בְּנֹה`, `לֹה`); `אָנֹךְ` "I" (ʾnk – Moabite writes final long vowels, so no ‑ī); `שָׁת` "year";
  `בָּנִתִי` "I built" (no medial yod); the Gt `אֶלְתַּחֵם`; `קִר` "town"; `אֵן` "there is not"; `הֻא` "he".
- **Judahite / Israelite forms:** the 3ms suffix -ō written ה (`עַבְדֹּה`, `אֲשֵׁרָתֹה`); the 2ms suffix written ‑k
  (`עַבְדְּךָ`, `אֵלֶיכָ`); a doubled kk written once (`יְבָרֶכָּ` – non-final כ with qamats, so that the letters match);
  the northern ‑yaw names (`אֲמַרְיָו`) beside the Judahite ‑yāhū (`הוֹשַׁעְיָהוּ`).
- **The Name** `יהוה` is never vocalized (in Mesha, ʿAjrud, Ketef Hinnom), nor is a broken `יה־` / `־וה`.
- **Uncertain words stay unvocalized** (Mesha 31 `בת וק אש`, 33 `דה`, 34 `שדק`), and the apparatus gives the readings.
- **Glosses** only in **פירוק**, for words a modern reader would misread: Moabite `קר` "town" (not "cold"), `רית`,
  `אשוח` "reservoir", `רחמת` "young women" (not "wombs"), `גרנ` "boys" (not "threshing floors"), `שת` "year",
  `משמעת` "subject"; Hebrew `ענה ל־` "testify", `נקי מ־` "free of", `ואמלא` = "and if not".

## 5. For the Aramaic texts still to come (Fekheriye, Sefire)
Fix before their first line: *ṣlm* `צֶלֶם` and *dmwt* `דְּמוּת` [cognate; Gen 1:26, 5:3] reserved for them (and
`דְּמוּת` never for any other word); *ʿdy* `עֲדֵי` (with the debated `עֵדֹת` and the Akkadian *adê* `אֲמָנָה` of the
law corpus in the apparatus – a split for the lead); the futility-curse formula; *gwgl* "canal-inspector"; the gods'
names transcribed (`הַדַּד` as in the Ugaritic corpus).

## 6. Traps (always vocalized, or avoided)
`מזרח` (guild / east), `שאר` (flesh / rest), `חלב` (milk / fat), `אמר` (lamb / said), `איל` / `אייל`, `פעמים`
(feet / times), `יובל` (ram / jubilee), `זר` (coin / stranger) – checked by the tool in translation lines. In readings:
`קר` / `קִר` "town" (not "cold"), `הא` = `הֻא` (not the interjection), `ימה` = `יָמֵהֻ` "his days" (not "seaward").
