## Method

- One file per excerpt, `annotated-<KEY>.rtl.md`, in the corpus format (guidelines §7.2): the base line (`@` +
  `python3 -I -B dossier_tools.py fill`), one Hebrew line, פירוק (from the source's own lemmatization where it has one:
  AED, VedaWeb, TLHdig), תרגומים (formed after the reading; AED's German only after drafting: `data MK --tr`), הקבלה,
  הערות. `translation.rtl.md` is built from `translation--head.rtl.md` + `translation--intro-<KEY>.rtl.md` + the `>`
  lines (`dossier_tools.py build`), and verified.
- Renderings: `../renderings.md` §11 (the comparison word `נָחָשׁ`; the never-words; names).
- **What the dossier can show** (world list §5A): that Gen 1:21's *created* `תַּנִּינִם` stand alone against a world where the
  sea monster is a rival (Ugarit, Hatti, India, the North), a parent (Hesiod's Keto, the Egyptian waters' greed), or a
  slain body (Japan). The parallels are typological (no contact) except for Ugarit, Egypt and Hatti (contact cultures),
  and the Greek of the LXX (the translators' own vocabulary).
  - **Egypt (WC, AP):** the water monster as a *made* thing and as an *uncreated* enemy. In pWestcar a lector priest
    (*ḥry-tp*, the Hebrew `חַרְטֹם`) makes a wax crocodile that, thrown into the water, "becomes a crocodile" (*ḫpr m* =
    `וַיְהִי לְ־`) – the miracle of Ex 7:9–12, where Aaron's staff and the חַרְטֻמִּים' staffs "become `תַּנִּינִם`"; in the
    Apophis book the serpent of the primeval water is cursed with childlessness ("you shall not conceive, none shall be
    born to you"), burned daily in wax and drawn as a serpent and as a crocodile-faced figure – the exact reverse of Gen
    1:21–22, where the great `תַּנִּינִם` are created and blessed "be fruitful and multiply". Ezek 29:3 and 32:2 turn the
    Egyptian image round: Pharaoh, Apophis's foe in the papyrus, is himself "the great `תַּנִּים`".
- Egyptian words follow `../../חכמה-ובריאה-במצרים/renderings.md` (§3 names, §4 key terms, §10.1–10.12; checked with
  `egypt_tools.py lex`): *msḥ* `תִּמְסָח` (never `תַּנִּין`), *ḥf* / *ḥfꜣw* `נָחָשׁ`, *Rꜥ* `רַא` ★, *ḥry-tp* `חַרְטֹם` [cognate],
  *ḫꜣꜥ* `הֵטִיל`, *šꜣs* `שׁוּט`, *jṯi̯* `תָּפַס`, *ḥtp* `נָח`, *sḏ.t* `שַׁלְהֶבֶת`, *ḫ.t* `אֵשׁ`, *sḫr* `הִכְרִיעַ`, *mḏꜣ.t* `מְגִלָּה` (`סֵפֶר` =
  *šfd*). New in the dossier (proposed to the Egyptian corpus, see the report): *mnḥ* `שַׁעֲוָה`, *š* `אֲגַם`, *ḏꜣ.t* `עֹמֶק`, *ḥry-pr*
  `סוֹכֵן הַבַּיִת`, *nḥꜣ* `מְסֻכָּן` (as AM 4.8), *nsr.t* `לַהַט` (the goddess `נֶסֶרֶת`), *wꜣwꜣ* `גַּחֶלֶת`, *dm/tm* `כָּלָה`, *šp*
  "perish" `אָבַד` / "be blind" `סוּמָא`, *sḥtm* `הִשְׁמִיד`, *ḥtm* `הִכְחִיד`, *sswn* `רִסֵּק`, *qꜣjs* `כָּפַת`, *snḥ* `אָסַר`, *hꜣrp*
  `שָׁקַע` (as AM 10.1), *sḫd* `צָלַל`, *ṯs.t* `פַּסְלוֹן־קְלָלָה`, *sb-n-sḏ.t* / *sb-n-ḫ.t* `קָרְבַּן־שַׁלְהֶבֶת` / `קָרְבַּן־אֵשׁ`.
  `dossier_tools.py verify` gates the loanwords and key words of WC/AP by their source word (`LICENSED`: `תמסח` – msḥ,
  `חרטום` – ḥry-tp, `הבנים` – hbnj, `שעווה` – mnḥ, `נחש` – ḥf/ḥfꜣw, `פרעה` – pr-ꜥꜣ) and traps an unvocalized `רא`.
