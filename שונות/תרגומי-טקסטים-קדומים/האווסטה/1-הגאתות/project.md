# Project 1 – the Gāthās of Zaraϑuštra (`1-הגאתות/`)

## Scope
The 17 Gāthās, whole: the Ahunavaitī (Y 28–34), the Uštavaitī (Y 43–46), the Spəṇtā.mainyu (Y 47–50), the
Vohu.xšaϑrā (Y 51), the Vahištōišti (Y 53) – 238 stanzas, 869 verse lines. One KEY per Yasna chapter (Y28 … Y53);
annotated files `annotated-<KEY>.rtl.md`, clean files built by `../avesta_tools.py build`.

## Base edition and numbering
CAB's linguistic corpus (XML), its verse lines, Geldner's stanza numbers (CAB's own div numbers differ: its `corresp`
gives Geldner's); labels `28.1a` = Y 28.1, line a. The liturgical repetitions and the Young Avestan frames are left
out (`../corpus.md`). Mills 1887 (SBE 31) numbers Y 28 one stanza higher (his 28.1 is the preface Y 28.0).

## Sources consulted
CAB (text; the online dictionary with Bartholomae's lemmas: `ave`, `avelemma`); Bartholomae 1904, *Altiranisches
Wörterbuch* (OCR, local: `aiw`); rank 5: Mills 1887, Moulton 1913 (public domain, OCR: `pd`). The modern editions and
translations (Insler 1975, Humbach 1991 / Humbach & Faiss 2010, Kellens & Pirart 1988–91, West 2010) are not open:
not used, and never quoted from memory.
