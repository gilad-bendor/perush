# Renderings: the Lament for Sumer and Urim (project 3)

The corpus `../renderings.md` binds (names §1, Emesal §2, refrains §3, key terms §4, reserved strings §5, the runs' merges §9), and so does the Hebrew of the Lament for Ur (`../1-הקינה-על-אור/`) for every shared word. This file holds what this poem adds: its own refrains and formulas (fixed before drafting, 2026-11-07, Round 6 run 4), its grammar decisions, its names, and the words first met here.
