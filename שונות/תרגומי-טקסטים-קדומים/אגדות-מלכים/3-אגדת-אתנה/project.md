## Scope
The whole Etana epic as eBL edits it (L/1/9), all six chapters, **585 lines**:
| Key | eBL chapter | Lines | Manuscripts (eBL sigla) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OBM | Old Babylonian, Morgan | 54 (1–16, a+1–a+13, b+1–b+16, c+1–c+9) | BabaOB1 (MLC 1363, the "Morgan tablet"; Scheil's copy RT 23) | the gods without a king; kingship comes down from heaven; Etana takes a wife?; the serpent's revenge; the eagle in the pit; Etana frees him: "open my eyes to what is hidden" |
| OBS | Old Babylonian, Susa | 48 (1–24, a+1–a+24) | the Susa tablet | the oath's curses; both give birth under the poplar; the shared hunt; the eagle's plot; the fledgling; the serpent's lament |
| MA | Middle Assyrian, Aššur | 160 (a+1 … i+18) | AššMA1–2 (at least two tablets) | the oath, the friendship, the hunt, the plot, the ambush; Etana's dream; the rescue; the dreams; the flight |
| I | Standard Babylonian I | 40 (1–29, a+1–a+11) | Nineveh | the city planned, no king, Ištar seeks a shepherd; Etana's wife's dream |
| II | Standard Babylonian II | 152 | Nineveh | the shrine of Adad and the poplar; the oath; the plot; Šamaš's ambush; the pit; Etana's prayer; the eagle found |
| III | Standard Babylonian III | 131 (1–56, a+1–a+23, b+1–b+45, c+1–c+7) | Nineveh | the speech of birds; the first flight (Etana gives up); return; the dreams; the second flight to the gates of the gods |

Base text: `../../sources/eBL/Etana-OB-Morgan/` … `Etana-SB-III/` (`reading-edition.md`: normalization N, Foster's
translation T, lemmas L, notes, parallels, ATF; `glossary.md`; generated by `tools/ebl_fetch.py` on 2026-10-21; git-
ignored, restorable with `tools/sources_sync.py`). Edition: Z. J. Földi (ed.), rev. A. Hätinen, E. Jiménez,
G. Rozzi (eBL 2026, with J. Novotny's digital edition); translation B. R. Foster. The end of the epic is lost.

## Files
- `annotated-OBM.rtl.md`, `annotated-OBS.rtl.md`, `annotated-MA.rtl.md`, `annotated-I.rtl.md`, `annotated-II.rtl.md`,
  `annotated-III.rtl.md`: one per eBL chapter; entries `### <KEY>.<eBL label>` (`### II.45`, `### MA.c+3`), in eBL's
  order. The first line of each file after the title is `<!-- כותרת־נוסח: … -->` (the version heading of the clean
  translation).
- `translation--intro.rtl.md` (the coordinator), `translation.rtl.md` (generated: `python3 -I ../kings_tools.py build ET`;
  it prints the eBL label without the chapter key, under the version heading).
- Tools: `python3 -I ../kings_tools.py verify ET [--strict]`, `data ET <from> <to> <out>` (the eBL block of each line +
  its related lines in all six versions with the Hebrew already given), `pars ET <from> <to>`, `lines ET <from>-<to>`,
  `grep all '<regex>'` (all corpora, Sumerian and Akkadian).

## Line numbering
eBL's labels (George-style: `a+1` = the first preserved line after a gap of unknown length). The literature's older
numbering (Kinnier Wilson 1985; Foster; Dalley; Haul 2000) differs (e.g. SB II = Kinnier Wilson's "Tablet II"); eBL's
`Parallels:` field gives the correspondences between the versions. No concordance to older numbering is made here.
