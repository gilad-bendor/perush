  Lagaš, whose lines 1–31, 99–108, 117–124 are their §4 (`../../סיפורי-המבול-המסופוטמיים/6-אזכורי-המבול/`): those lines were
  copied here with their apparatus, and `ur3_tools.py verify KL|RL` keeps the two copies identical (guidelines §1).
- **Base:** ETCSL composites (`../../sources/ETCSL/2.1.1--…`, `2.1.2--…`); ORACC Q000371, Q000372. Rank 3: ETCSL's English.
