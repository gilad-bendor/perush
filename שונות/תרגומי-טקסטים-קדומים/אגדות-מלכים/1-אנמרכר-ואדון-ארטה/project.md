## Base text and versions
- **Rank 1:** ETCSL c.1.8.2.3 (`../../sources/ETCSL/1.8.2.3--Enmerkar-and-the-Lord-of-Aratta/transliteration.md`; after
  Cohen 1973 and Vanstiphout) and **Mittermayer 2009** (OBO 239, open access; reconstructed text with German
  translation pp. 114–153, score pp. 154–219, commentary pp. 220 ff.; `../../sources/Mittermayer-2009--…/`).
- **Rank 2:** ORACC epsd2/literary Q000369 (the ETCSL composite with ePSD2 lemma + morph for every word;
  `../../sources/ORACC/epsd2-literary--Q000369--…/`); ePSD2 (`../../tools/sumerian_workbench.py gloss`).
- **Rank 3:** ETCSL's English (after Vanstiphout 2003); Mittermayer's German.
- Not available: Cohen 1973, Kramer 1952, Vanstiphout 2003, Jacobsen 1987, the articles on the Nudimmud spell
  (`../../sources/_records-only--Legends-of-kings/`; Mittermayer summarizes them).

## The parallels that matter for the commentary
Gen 11:1–9 (134–155; the bricks of Kulaba; the temple "brought down from heaven"; *a-da* "contest" of the lords and
the scattering); Gen 10 (the nations by their tongues; Erech, Akkad); Exod 4:10 (501–502); the messengers' formulas
(Gen 32:4–6; Num 20:14; Judg 11:12–28); writing the word (Exod 24:12; Deut 27:8; Jer 36); famine and grain (Gen 41–42;
Deut 11); the peace of the beasts (Isa 11:6–9; 65:25).
