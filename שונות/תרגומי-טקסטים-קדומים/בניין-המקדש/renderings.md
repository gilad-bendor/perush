# Shared renderings: the temple-building corpus (the Gudea Cylinders A and B)

Binding for every project of this folder (`corpus.md`). Echo tags follow guidelines §6.4: [cognate], [equivalent], [divergence], [absent]. Fixed by the corpus coordinator before the first run (2026-10-20), from the earlier Sumerian corpora and a survey of the whole text (ORACC lemma frequencies; a concordance of the key expressions in the transliteration). Translators add rows to the project's `renderings.md` and write proposals in `project.md`; the coordinator merges them here between runs (§9). **The translator of a formula's first occurrence may revise a pre-set rendering of §3, with the reason in `project.md`; after that it binds every copy.**

## 0. Inheritance
1. **The Sumerian renderings of the two earlier Sumerian corpora bind here as the default**, the most recent first: `../קינות-על-ערים/renderings.md` (all of it, including §9 merged from its five runs) and its project files; then `../סיפורי-הבריאה-המסופוטמיים/renderings.md` §5–§8 and the Sumerian project files there (2, 3, 4). Look a word up before fixing it: `python3 -I temple_tools.py grep all '<regex>'` searches the `>` lines of all of them; `../tools/sumerian_workbench.py conc` the ETCSL corpus.
2. **A Sumerian word takes the Hebrew of its Akkadian equivalent** (law corpus §6; lament corpus §0): *ensi* (dream-interpreter) = *šāʾiltu* = `שׁוֹאֶלֶת` (wisdom corpus *šāʾilu* `הַשּׁוֹאֵל`); *bur₂* (a dream) = *pašāru* = `פָּשַׁר`; *ĝiškim* = *ittu* = `אוֹת`; *ĝiš-ḫur* = *uṣurtu* = `צוּרָה`; *um-mi-a* = *ummânu* = `אָמָּן`; *erin* = *erēnu* = `אֶרֶז`; *udug sag₉* / *lamma* = *šēdu* / *lamassu* = `רוּחַ־מָגֵן` / `אֵלַת־מָגֵן`; *nu-siki* / *nu-mu-su* = *ekūtu* / *almattu* = `יָתוֹם` / `אַלְמָנָה`; *ibila* = *aplu* = `יוֹרֵשׁ`.
3. **Open cross-corpus choices followed as the most recent corpus has them (flagged; settled 2026-10-21, §9.8 – Inana `אִינַנָּה`, *me* `הַ־מֶה`, *mušen* `צִפּוֹר` as here):** Inana = `אִינַנָּה` ★ (lament and creation corpora; the law corpus writes `אִינַאנָּה`); *me* = `הַ־מֶה` ★ (lament and creation; the flood corpus has `הַכֹּחוֹת הַנִּשְׂגָּבִים`); Nisaba = `נִיסַבָּה` (EWO; the Song of the Hoe `נִיסָבָּה`); *mušen* = `צִפּוֹר`.
4. **Recorded deviations from the earlier corpora:** *šudu₃* "prayer, blessing" (Akk. *ikribu*, from *karābu* "pray, bless") = `בְּרָכָה`, *šudu₃ … ša₄* = `בֵּרֵךְ` – EWO 293 has `תפילה`, but the lament corpus fixed *sizkur₂* = `תְּפִלָּה`, the cylinders have both words side by side (A ii 9 / ii 21), and creation §6.5 licenses `ברך` by *šud₃* (2026-10-21: the other corpora follow this one, §9.8); and *lugal* of a slave's owner = `אָדוֹן` (elsewhere *lugal* = `מֶלֶךְ`).

## 2. Grammar (inherited)
Narrative marû = participle; ḫamṭu = past; *ḫe₂-* + marû = wish / jussive, *ḫe₂-* + ḫamṭu in narrative or report = `אָכֵן` (affirmative; lament §9.1); *ga-* = cohortative (`אֶבְנֶה`); *u₃-* = "when, after" (`כְּשֶׁ־`, `אַחֲרֵי שֶׁ־`); *nu-* = `לֹא`; *na-* + marû = `אַל`; non-finite *-a* = relative clause; copula *-am₃* = nominal sentence; *-gin₇* = `כְּ־`; *-da-* comitative = `עִם`; reduplication (*pad₃-pad₃*, *ĝa₂-ĝa₂*) = plurality or iteration, shown by a participle or a doubled verb (never `שוב ושוב`). Gender: Sumerian has none; a god is masculine or feminine by the god; the house (*e₂*) is masculine (`הַבַּיִת`).

## 4. Echo control for this text (read before drafting)
