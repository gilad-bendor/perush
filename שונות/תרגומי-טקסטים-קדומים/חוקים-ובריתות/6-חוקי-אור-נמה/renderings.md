# Renderings: the Laws of Ur-Namma (UN), project 6 of `חוקים-ובריתות/`

The corpus file `../renderings.md` is binding (**§5, §6.1, §6.3**: every Sumerian legal formula takes the Hebrew of its standard Akkadian equivalent, letter for letter as in the Laws of Hammurabi), together with LH's fixed formulas (`../2-חוקי-חמורבי/renderings.md` §1–§2, §4) and **the Lipit-Ištar glossary** (`../5-חוקי-ליפית-אישתר/renderings.md`), which fixed the Sumerian legal Hebrew; UN follows LI wherever the same Sumerian word occurs. This file holds the Sumerian words of UN with the Hebrew chosen for them. Started by T7 (run 6, 2026-10-18) for the laws (file 2, 181.a – f008); T8 extended it for the prologue and epilogue (§3).

Columns: Sumerian (ePSD2 `cf[gw]`) | Akkadian equivalent | Hebrew (plain plene, as in the `>` lines; vocalized form in brackets) | rejected | echo | lines (file 2).
