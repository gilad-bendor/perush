## 0. The danger specific to LH
The Covenant Code (Exod 21–23) is the natural Hebrew for much of LH, and LH is the text most often compared with it. A translator who reaches for `מוֹת יוּמָת`, `עַיִן תַּחַת עַיִן`, `נַגָּח`, `וְהוּעַד בִּבְעָלָיו`, `אֶל הָאֱלֹהִים`, `לִשְׁמֹר`, `נָקִי`, `חָפְשִׁי` would manufacture the parallels. Conversely, LH has real cognates of the Covenant Code's own words (`נָגַף` ↔ `יִגֹּף`, Exod 21:35; `הֵמִית` ↔ `וְהֵמִית`, 21:29; `בַּעַל הַשּׁוֹר`; `שָׁקַל`; `נָתַן`; `שִׁלֵּם`; `אָמָה`; `כְּשָׁפִים`; `דְּרוֹר`; `זָכָר`; `לְחִי`; `מָחַץ`), and they must stay visible. Hence the reserved list (§4) and the fixed formulas (§1).

**The class tiers.** LH grades penalties by status: *awīlum* (the full free man; also simply "a man, a person"), *muškēnum* (the "commoner", a free man of lower rank, dependent on the palace), *wardum* / *amtum* (male / female slave). The Torah has no free-man tiers (it distinguishes only free and slave). The Hebrew keeps the three terms distinct and constant (`אִישׁ`, `מִסְכֵּן`, `עֶבֶד` / `אָמָה`; with *mār* "son of", "member of the class": `בֶּן אִישׁ`, `בֶּן מִסְכֵּן`), so that a reader sees every tier.

Temples are transliterated with the meaning in a note (`אֵכּוּר`, `אֵסַגִּיל`, `אֵזִידָה`, `אֵבַּבַּר`, `אֵאַנָּה` …).
**Hammurabi** = `חַמּוּרַבִּי` (plain `חמורבי`), the familiar Hebrew form; first-occurrence note: modern editions also read *Ḫammu-rāpi* ("the (divine) kinsman heals", West Semitic *ʿammu* + *rāpiʾ*, cognate of `רָפָא`); the old identification with `אַמְרָפֶל` (בראשית יד:א) is abandoned. **Open decision** (`חַמּוּרָפִּי`).
Epithets: *rē'ûm* `רועה` (↔ `אַתָּה תִרְעֶה אֶת עַמִּי`, שמואל ב ה:ב); *nibīt X* "named by X" `קרוא X` (*nabû* = `קרא` reserved); *šar mīšarim* `מלך המישרים`; *ṣalmāt qaqqadim* `שחורי הראש` (corpus); *kibrāt erbettim* `ארבע כנפות (העולם)`? – the first translator decides (`ארבע הפינות`), and records it.

## 5. Format reminders
- The Akkadian line is the unit: one composite line = one Hebrew line, even when the line is one word. Never move a word across lines; Hebrew word order inside a line may change for grammar.
- Line numbers exactly as in CDLI (`### 304`, `### 1130'`): the prime is part of the label.
- Numbers in words; fractions as `שליש`, `חצי`, `שני שלישים`, `חמישית`, `שישית`.
