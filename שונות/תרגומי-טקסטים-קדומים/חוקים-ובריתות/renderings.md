# Shared renderings: the law and treaty corpus (`חוקים-ובריתות/`)

Binding for all projects in this folder (`corpus.md`). Echo tags follow guidelines §6.4: [cognate], [equivalent], [divergence], [absent]. Fixed 2026-10-17 by the corpus coordinator before drafting project 1 (Esarhaddon's Succession Treaty, EST); the EST-only formulas are in `1-אמנת-הירושה-של-אסרחדון/renderings.md`.

**The danger specific to this corpus.** Deuteronomy's vocabulary (`בְּרִית`, `אָלָה`, `שָׁמַר`, `שָׁמַע`, `אָהַב`, `בְּכָל לְבָבְךָ`, `נָבִיא`, `לֹא תֹסֵף … וְלֹא תִגְרַע`) is the natural Hebrew for much of what the treaties say, so a translator can make the treaty sound like Deuteronomy without noticing it. On the other hand, real cognates (`נָצַר`, `שָׁמַע`, `אָרַר`, `חָטָא`, `נֶפֶשׁ`, `שִׁנָּה`, `מֵישָׁרִים`, `נָסַח`) must stay visible. Hence: **each Deuteronomic key word is used only where the Akkadian has its cognate or its exact equivalent, and every use is tagged.**
