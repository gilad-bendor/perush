### Decisions (translator)
1. **MAL A's and LH's Hebrew for every shared formula** (looked up with `../laws_lookup.py` before drafting): `אם איש`; `בררו עליו, כוננו עליו`; `<n> במקלות ימחצוהו`; `יֶרַח ימים את מלאכת המלך יעשה`; `פי שלושה יתן` (= MAL A o iii 64); `אצבע אחת שלו יקצצו` (= MAL A o i 92); `כיכר בדיל יתן` (= MAL A o ii 92); `לבעל הבית`; `(אם) פניו (לכך)` / `יתרצה` (= MAL A §10, o i 101–103 – the same choice of the injured party); `חוצפה אמר`, `ברח`, `כְּלִבּוֹ`; *tappû* `עמית`; *annaku* `בדיל`, *biltu* `כיכר`; *zâzu* `יחלוקו` / `שלא חלקו`, *zittu* `חלק`; *ammar* `כל אשר`; *mamma šaniumma* `אדם אחר`; *zakû* `זכאי`, D `זיכה`; *šalāmu* G `ישתלם`; *hazannu* `החַזָּן`, *rabiūtu ša āle* `גדולי העיר`; *tabāku* `יצק`; *târu* D `השיב`; *ṣalāʾu* `הפיל`; *ummiānu* `נושה`; LH: *kirû* `גן`, *ugāru* `שְׁדֵמָה`, *erēšu* `חרש`, *nasāqu* `בחר`, *mērešu* `מזרע`, *eṣēdu* `קצר`, *bilat eqle* `תנובת שדה`, *warqu* `ירק`, *nāgiru* `הַכָּרוֹז`, *ragāmu* `תבע`, *libittu* `לבנה`, *nīš ilim* `חיי האל` (F13) → *nīš šarre* `חיי המלך`, *zakāru* `הזכיר`.
2. **The boundary laws (§8–§9, §20) – the key comparison with Deut 19:14; 27:17.** The Akkadian has three words where Deuteronomy has `גבול` + `הסיג`: *taḫūmu* "boundary" = **`תחום`** (the Mishnaic/Aramaic word is a loan from this Akkadian word – from memory, Kaufman; not in the Bible); *samāḫu* D "mix, confuse (field borders)" (eBL/CDA: "MA 'confuse' field borders"; ORACC "incorporate") = **`עירב`**; *nabalkutu* Š (*usbalkit*, §9) "make cross over, shift" = **`הזיז`**; *kudurru* "boundary stone" + *kadāru* D "delimit" (§20) = **`ציון` / `ציין`** (one root for one root). **`גבול`, `הסיג`, `גבל` are kept out of every `>` line** (`גבול` is also LH's *paṭṭu*): no Akkadian verb here is the exact equivalent of `הִסִּיג` "move back" – *samāḫu* is "mix in", *šubalkutu* "move across" (the closest, tagged [equivalent]). Rejected: `סיפח` (interpretive), `העתיק` (would echo `וַיַּעְתֵּק`, Gen 26:22, in the wells story), `העביר` (= *etēqu* Š, MAL A), `השיג` (Job 24:2's spelling).
3. ***qātu* "portion" = `יד`** (`שתי יָדוֹת`, `יד אחת`, `ידו השנייה`): Hebrew has the same semantic extension (`חָמֵשׁ יָדוֹת`, Gen 43:34; `עֶשֶׂר יָדוֹת`, 2 Sam 19:44); `חלק` stays for *zittu*, `פי שנים` (Deut 21:17) and `בכור` (no *bukru*: *mār rabiu* = `הבן הגדול`) are kept out. ***qāta elû*** "his hand goes up (from)" = forfeit = **`ידו תסתלק`** (the Hebrew legal idiom `סילק ידו`).
4. ***pūru* = `פּוּר`** (always vocalized; `הַפּוּרִים` for the allotted plots of §9), and *pūra ṣalāʾu* = `את הפּוּר … יפיל` – MAL A's verb for *ṣalāʾu*, which here coincides with `הִפִּיל פּוּר` (Esth 3:7). `גורל` stays for *šīmtu* (corpus).
5. **The proclamation (§6):** *nāgiru* = `הַכָּרוֹז` (LH); *šasû* G and D = `הכריז` (D with the herald as object: `(על ידי) הכרוז … יכריז`); *mušassiānu* `לַמַּכְרִיז`. `צעק` (the corpus's gated word for *šasû*) is wrong for an official proclamation, `קרא` is reserved for *nabû*: recorded as a justified deviation from the gating table (no `צעק` in the lines). *ana ṣarpe leqû* = `בכסף לקח` (F9), never `קנה` (kept free for Jer 32; Ruth 4). *qēpu* = `פקיד`; *sukkallu* = `שר` (**settled 2026-10-21, cross-corpus harmonization: `וזיר`**, o iii 30 `מן הוזירים`); *ṭupšarru* = `סופר`; *annanna mār annanna* = `פלוני בן פלוני`; *elû* Š (a tablet) = `הציג`; *udīni … lā* = `כל עוד … לא` (`טרם` kept free). The two quotations (o iii 9–27, o iii 39–47) are closed where de Ridder closes them.
6. **Water (§17–§18):** *šaqû* = `השקה` [cognate], *šīqu* = `השקיה`; *mû ša Adad* = `מי אֲדַד` (the god's name kept; a note says "rain water"; `מטר`, EST-reserved, kept out); *ištu ahāʾiš izzazzū* = `זה עם זה יעמדו`; *magru* / *lā magrūtu* = `הרוצה` / `(מי) שאינם רוצים` (MAL A *magāru* = `רצה`); *ana šīqe ana šakāne illukūni* translated literally (`להשקיה לָשׂוּם הולכים`), with a note on the idiom "to go for" = "be available for".
7. **Planting and building (§10–§15):** *būrtu* = `בְּאֵר` [cognate], *herû* = `חפר`; *dunnu* "fortified farmstead" = `מגדל` (Isa 5:2; 2 Chr 26:10 `מִגְדָּלִים … בֹּרוֹת`); *kirâ nadû* = `גן ייסד`, *nādiānu* `המייסד` (**`נטע` rejected: LH's *zaqāpu*, and a false echo of Gen 2:8 `וַיִּטַּע … גַּן`**); *urqu* = `ירק` [cognate]; *libnāte labānu* = `לבנים לָבַן` [cognate, Gen 11:3]; *mānaḫtu* "toil, invested labour and equipment" = `יְגִיעַ` (always vocalized); *eqle kī eqle* = `שדה כשדה` (corpus §5 *kīma* = `כ`).
8. **§1–§5 (brothers):** *bēl napšāte* = `בעל הנפשות` (not `גואל הדם`: kept for the comparison); *napšāte gamāru* = `נפשות כילה`; *šiluḫlu* = `*המשועבדים*` (uncertain); *esēḫu* = `הקצה`; *ina šanûtešu* = `בשנית`; *zēra zarû* = `זֶרַע זָרַע`.
9. **Other new words:** *naqāru* (a house) `הרס` (MAL A's `שרט` is the same lemma in another sense: recorded); *kalû* `כְּלָאוֹ` (cognate list); *turāšu* `עת הקציר` (beside *eṣēdu* `קצר`: two Akkadian roots, one Hebrew root – accepted, natural Hebrew); *ḫašīmu* `ערמה` (`אסם`, `מגורה`, `כרי` are taken); *tibnu* `תבן`, *kurdiššu* `מַתְבֵּן`; *nappaltu* `*יתרות*`; *tarāḫu* `*עדר*`; *galāšu* `*גירד*`; *utrâq* `*ירוקן*`; *lawû* `הקיף`; *dagālu* `ראה` (`רואה`; no *amāru* in MAL B); *ina lā eqlišu* `בשדה שאינו שלו`, *ina lā qaqqirišu* `בקרקע שאינה שלו`.
10. **Translated from the transliteration where ORACC's `norm` differs:** r v 31 *libnāte* for `SIG₄-MEŠ-šu` (`את לבניו`); r vi 30, 33 *margrūtu/-e* (typo for *magrūtu*); o ii 27 *ina eqlu*, o ii 15/22 *ina ahhū*, o iii 26 / r vi 7 / r vi 26 *aʾīle* (case smoothing ignored); o iii 4, 8, 34, 44 *-ūni* is the MA subjunctive, not a plural (the buyer is one person; o iii 23, 25 are plural by *ittablūnenni*). r v 18 "to the owner of the orchard" (*bēl kirê*) translated as written, with a note (the sense requires the owner of the field).

### Open decisions for the user
1. **`תחום`** for *taḫūmu* (Decision 2) – the loan into Mishnaic Hebrew – vs `גבול` (the Deuteronomic word; it would make §8–§9 read like Deut 19:14, which is exactly what the corpus rule forbids unless the words are equivalent).
2. **`עירב`** (*samāḫu* D, literal "mix") vs `סיפח` ("annex", the legal effect; de Ridder's "incorporates"); **`הזיז`** (*šubalkutu*) vs `הסיג` (the brief allowed `הסיג` only for an exact equivalent: we judged *šubalkutu* "move across" not exact).
3. **`ציון ציין`** for *kudurra ukaddir* vs `אבן תחום הציב` (more explicit, loses the one-root figure).
4. **`יד` / `שתי ידות`** for *qātu* "share" vs `חלק` / `מנה`; **`ידו תסתלק`** for *qāta elû* vs `יאבד את זכותו` (sense only).
5. **`הכריז`** for *šasû* (against the gating table's `צעק`); **`פקיד`** for *qēpu* vs `נציב`, `ממונה`.
6. **`מי אֲדַד`** (literal) vs `מי הגשם` (sense).
7. **`גן ייסד`** for *kirâ nadû* vs `גן נטע` (natural Hebrew, but LH's *zaqāpu* and Gen 2:8).
8. **`מגדל`** for *dunnu* vs `חווה מבוצרת`, `מצד`; **`*המשועבדים*`** for *šiluḫlu* vs transliteration.
9. **`חיי המלך`** (LH F13 pattern) vs `חֵי המלך` (the biblical oath form, Gen 42:15).

### Proposals for the renderings
- For `../renderings.md` (§6.2 or a new §6.4 "MAL B"): *taḫūmu* = `תחום` [cognate loan], **`גבול` and `הסיג` reserved for Deut 19:14; 27:17** in every law project (add `גבול`, `תסיג`, `הסיג`, `מסיג`, `גבל` to the `laws_verify.py` reserved list, or gate `גבול` to *paṭṭu* (LH 593) only); *kudurru* / *kadāru* = `ציון` / `ציין`; *samāḫu* D = `עירב`; *nabalkutu* Š = `הזיז`; *pūru* = `פּוּר` (`גורל` stays *šīmtu*); *qātu* "share" = `יד` (`ידות`), *qāta elû* = `ידו תסתלק`; *nāgiru* = `כרוז`, *šasû* (proclaim) = `הכריז` (a sense-split of the gated `צעק` row: `צעק` for "cry out", `הכריז` for "proclaim"); *qēpu* = `פקיד`; *būrtu* = `בְּאֵר`, *herû* = `חפר`; *šaqû* = `השקה`, *šīqu* = `השקיה`; *libittu*/*labānu* = `לבנה`/`לבן`; *mānaḫtu* = `יְגִיעַ`; *kirâ nadû* = `גן ייסד` (`נטע` = *zaqāpu* only); *dunnu* = `מגדל`; *ana ṣarpe leqû* = `בכסף לקח` (`קנה` kept for *šâmu*); *bēl napšāte* = `בעל הנפשות` (`גואל הדם` reserved).
- For the gating list of `laws_verify.py`: `נטע` only for *zaqāpu* (LH), `פור` only for *pūru*.

### Lessons
- **A Deuteronomic noun can hide behind three Akkadian words:** Deut 19:14 has `גבול` + `הסיג`; MAL B has *taḫūmu*, *kudurru* and three different verbs (*samāḫu*, *šubalkutu*, *kadāru*). Fixing one Hebrew word per Akkadian word (and keeping `גבול`/`הסיג` out) shows that no MAL law uses the Torah's verb – the comparison is of the offence, not of the wording.
- **A loanword can be the best "neutral" rendering:** `תחום` (from *taḫūmu*) and `פּוּר` (from *pūru*) are cognates that do not pre-empt the biblical comparison word (`גבול`, `גורל`).
- **The MA subjunctive *-ūni* looks like a plural** in ORACC's normalization (*ilaqqeūni*, *iltaknūni*): check the subject before writing a plural verb.
- **Look up the earlier projects' Hebrew for the same lemma in another sense** (*naqāru* "scratch" / "demolish"; *biltu* "talent" / "yield"; *ṣalāʾu* "miscarry" / "throw"): two senses may need two Hebrew words – or, as with *ṣalāʾu* = `הפיל`, one Hebrew verb carries both.
