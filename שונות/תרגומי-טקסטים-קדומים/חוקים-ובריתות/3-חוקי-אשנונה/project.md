## Scope
- **The whole composite**: the date-and-occasion prologue (1–7, Sumerian with Akkadian) and the laws §1–§60 with §9a, §18a, §47a (8–195): **195 composite lines**, 63 sections.
- Old Babylonian Akkadian (much of it in Sumerograms), the kingdom of Eshnunna on the Diyala, 19th c. BCE (the prologue names Daduša; the collection is earlier than Hammurabi's, which conquered Eshnunna).

### Proposals for the renderings
- For the corpus `renderings.md` §6 (shared by LE, MAL, LI, UN): **PI = `פַּרְסִיכְתּוּ`** (whatever CDLI reads); *lū* + preterite in a protasis = Hebrew future, noted; ***malû* D "make good" = `מילא`** (keeps `שילם` for *šullumu*); ***risbatum* = `קטטה`** (= LH 2560'), and `ריב`/`מריבה` out of the `>` lines (`יריבן`); ***naqābu* = `נִקֵּב`** (or the user's alternative); *ḫabātu* A N "be carried off" `נגזל`, *ḫabātu* B "flee" `ברח`; *mašāʾu* `חטף`; *šâlu* "consent" `שאלת` [cognate]; *tarû* `הסיע`; *bušû* `חפצים`; *ubāru* `אורח`; *mudû* `מוֹדָע`; *maṣṣāru* `נוֹצֵר`; *pallišu* `פולש`; *mār šipri* `שליח`; *šakkanakku* `נציב`; *kannu*/*maškanu*/*abbuttu* `כבל`/`אזיק`/`בלורית`; *ana pāni pilšim* `לפני המפלש`; ***ina X Y iṣṣabbatu* "is caught (doing) …" = `ייתפש`**; *kirru* I `כד`, *kirru* II `עצם הבריח`; *erû* `נחושת` (with the open decision on *siparru*); *ukullû* of persons `מחיה`.

### Lessons
- **A scribal slip can be the evidence of a list**: §57's "gored" for the dog shows §54–§57 were drafted (or copied) as one pattern with the verb exchanged; mark such slips `⟨ ⟩` with a footnote rather than smoothing them away, and do not let a lemma gate (here `נגף`) decide the reading.
- **Check LH's Hebrew by lemma, not only by identical lines**: `laws_verify.py` found no cross-collection REVIEW lines at all, yet a manual lemma check against LH's `>` lines changed seven LE words (`איש את איש`, `מזונות, משחה ולבוש`, `שער`, `שכמותו`, and three moved off LH words of other lemmas: `פצע`, `ממונה`, `מזונו`). A lemma-indexed lookup (Akkadian lemma → Hebrew in LH) would catch these automatically.
- **CDLI's LE normalization fails mostly where the composite is Sumerographic or unusual**: the Sumerian date formula, *lū* + preterite (*li-di-in*), numbers and measures (4 barig, 10 minas), and the *bābtum* / *bābu* confusion in the English. Read the ATF and the exemplars' own lines for every such line.
- **Running `build_parallels_index.py --help` regenerates the corpus index** (the script takes no options); the regenerated header was reverted with `git checkout`. Do not run it from a translator's session.
