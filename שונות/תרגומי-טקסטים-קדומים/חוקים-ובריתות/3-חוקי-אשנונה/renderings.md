Binding above this file: the corpus `../renderings.md` (§0, §5, **§6**) and the Laws of Hammurabi's fixed formulas and words (`../2-חוקי-חמורבי/renderings.md` §1 F1–F43, §2, §4). **An Akkadian formula that LH has gets LH's Hebrew letter for letter** (`../laws_verify.py LE` checks it). This file lists the LE words and how they were fixed (T1, run 6, 2026-10-18); "LH" = taken from the LH annotated files (`../laws_lookup.py`). Plain plene Hebrew as it must appear in the `>` lines; vocalized forms strip to it.
