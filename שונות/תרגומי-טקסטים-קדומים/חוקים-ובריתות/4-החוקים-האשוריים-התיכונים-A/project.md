Corpus: `../corpus.md` (law and treaty), project 4. Read `../../translation-guidelines.md`, `../corpus.md`, `../renderings.md` (**§5 and §6** are binding: §6.2 is this project's), the Laws of Hammurabi's fixed formulas (`../2-חוקי-חמורבי/renderings.md` §1, §2, §4) and the Laws of Eshnunna's Part T1 (`../3-חוקי-אשנונה/project.md`) first.

## Scope
- **MAL A = KAV 1 (VAT 10000)**, Aššur: the whole tablet, **828 lines** (obv. i–iv, rev. v–viii), laws **§1–§59** and the colophon (r viii 64–65: month Ša-sarrāte, day 2, eponym Saggiu; 11th c. BCE, Tiglath-pileser I; the laws themselves are older, probably 14th c.).
- Middle Assyrian Akkadian. The collection is about **women**: theft by a wife, blasphemy, assault, sexual offences (in the city, in the field, in the tavern), slander, abortion, marriage and bride-gifts, widows, the levirate-type marriages, veiling, sorcery, pledges, the husband's punishments.

## Base edition and sources
- **Rank 1:** ORACC **tcma/laws P281779** (de Ridder 2021): transliteration, the project's normalization of every word (`norm`), lemmas (`../../sources/ORACC/tcma-laws--P281779--Middle-Assyrian-Laws-A/`, README; CC BY-SA 3.0 / CC0).
- **Rank 2:** CDA (eBL API, per word), CAD (per word), ORACC lemmas (check them), the LH and LE Hebrew (`python3 -I ../laws_lookup.py`).

### Decisions (translator)

2. **Corpus §6.2 applied:** *niāku* = `בא על` (all forms), *nāʾikānu* = `הבא עליה` (`לַבָּא עליה`, vocalized), *nīku* (adj.) = `שבאו עליה`, *ana niāke* = `לביאה`; *ḫīṭu* = `חטא`, *ḫīṭa emēdu* = `חטא … הטיל`, *ḫīṭu laššu* = `חטא אין` in the line's order (`לאשה חטא אין`, o ii 24; `חטא לאיש אין`, o ii 60); *emittu* = `הטלה` (the noun of *emēdu*); *emūqamma* `בכוח`, *ṣabātu* `תפש`, *kašādu* `השיג`, *rebītu* `רחוב`, *ina libbi āle* `בתוך העיר`; *ša-libbiša* `את אשר בבטנה`, *ṣalāʾu* Š `הפיל`; *ina ḫaṭṭāte n imaḫḫuṣūš* `<n> במקלות ימחצוהו`; *šipar šarre … eppaš* `את מלאכת המלך … יעשה`; *ša-rēši* `סריס`; *Id* `הנהר`.
3. ***annaku* = `בדיל` (tin)**, not `עופרת` (= *abāru*): MA *annaku* is tin, the currency metal of Assyria (de Ridder writes "tin" once, "lead" elsewhere; Roth "lead", from memory). *biltu* = `כיכר` (`שתי כיכרות`, `שלוש כיכרות`), *manû* = `מנה` (`חמישה מנים`, `שלושים מנה`). Open decision 1.
4. ***urah ūmāte* = `יֶרַח ימים`** [cognate of the idiom `יֶרַח יָמִים`, דברים כא:יג; *warḫu* = ירח in the corpus cognate list], not `חודש ימים`.
5. ***gadāmu* = `גדם`** (`יגדמוהו`, o ii 80, 92). Corpus §6.2 assigned "shave" `גילח` to *zaqānu* D, but **MAL A has no *zaqānu***: the verb of §18–§19 is *gadāmu* "cut off (a body part, as punishment)" (CDA), "cut off hair" (ORACC); `גילח` stays free (proposal B below).
6. ***ṣāltu* = `מָדוֹן`** (always vocalized): verbal or physical strife; kept distinct from *risbatum* `קטטה` (LH/LE: another word) and from the Torah's verbs (`ריב`, `נצה`, `מצה`, Exod 21:18, 22; Deut 25:11).
7. ***tappû* = `עמית`** (`רעהו` reserved; `חבר` = LH *ibru*; `שותף` of LH renderings §4 rejected: in modern Hebrew "partner" = sexual partner, which would mislead in §20). Coincidence to note: Lev 18:20 `אֵשֶׁת עֲמִיתְךָ` and Lev 24:19 `כִּי יִתֵּן מוּם בַּעֲמִיתוֹ` – both tagged in the apparatus. Open decision 2.
8. ***puzru* = `סתר`** (`בסתר`; the root of *pazzuru* `הסתיר`, corpus §2); *ana pān ṣābē* = `לפני אנשים` (LH *ṣābu* = `צבא` in its military sense; here "in public").
9. **The slander formula** *aššatka ittinikkū* = `"על אשתך באים"`; *ittinikkūš* `"באים עליו"`, *ittinikkūka* `"באים עליך"`: the Hebrew participle carries the iterative (Gtn) and the impersonal subject. **There is no *kalūmā* (or *gabbu*) in the tablet**: "everyone" (de Ridder, Roth) is the translators' rendering of the iterative; `כולם` and `שוב ושוב` rejected.
10. ***mā*** (quotation particle) = quotation marks only (no `לאמר`); the oath *šumma* + subjunctive = `אם` "(I swear that) not" (`"אם הוריתיה"`, `"אם באתי עליה"`), corpus §3.
11. **New words** (renderings.md §2): *šillatu* `חוצפה`, *miqit pê* `נפילת פה`; *ešertu* `מקדש`; *baʾʾerūtu* `בירור`; *māhirānu* `המקבל`; *mamma* `אדם` ("anyone"; LH's `אדם` is *awīlūtu*, epilogue only); *aḫāzu* Š `הורה`; *maškattu* `הפקדה`; *išku* `אשך` [cognate]; *ubānu* `אצבע`; *rakāsu* D (bandage) `חבש`; *lapātu* N `נִנְגַּע`; *napālu* `עקר` (`נקר` = LH *ḫuppudu*); *naqāru* (face) `שרט`; *šadādu* `משך`; *erimtu* `*נדן*`; *pāšu* `גרזן`; *dāʾikānu* `ההורגים` (`רוצח` rejected: the Torah's root); *pānūšuma* `(אם) פניו (לכך)`; *zakû* `זכאי` (`נקי` reserved); *aštammu* `פונדק` (*sābītu* = `מוכרת השכר`: another word); *pû* `על פיה`; *ḫarrāna šuṣbutu* `דרך הדריכה`; *bitiqtu* `חסרונות` (`נזק` = LH *ḫiṭītu*); *mummirtu* `סרסורית`; *pīgu* `*ברמייה*`; *wadû* D `ייעד`; *Aššurāyu* `אַשּׁוּרִי`; *biādu* `לָנָה`; *ḫadû* `חָפֵץ`; *gimru* `כל הוצאות`; *qerēbu* `קרב` [cognate]; *watāru* D `הותיר` [cognate].

### Open decisions for the user
1. **`בדיל`** (tin) for *annaku* (Decision 3) vs `עופרת` (lead, the older translations' word).
2. **`עמית`** for *tappû* (Decision 7) vs `שותף` (LH renderings §4) or `חבר` (collides with *ibru*).
3. **`יגדמוהו`** for *gadāmu* (Decision 5; "they shall cut off (his hair?)") vs `יגלחוהו` (de Ridder's "shave", corpus §6.2's word for the absent *zaqānu*).
4. **`מָדוֹן`** for *ṣāltu* (Decision 6) vs `מריבה` (blocked by the reserved `יריבן`/`ריב` family).
5. **`*כעגל*`** (o i 89, *kī būre*) and **`*נדן*`** (o i 95, *erimtu*): literal, marked uncertain; alternatives "like a (rutting) bull", "blade".
6. **`לא תרצה, תנצור (את עצמה)`** (o ii 17): descriptive ("she does not consent, she guards herself") or modal (de Ridder: "she may not agree and should protect herself"); the Hebrew future carries both, the note says so – the choice changes whether the law requires resistance.
7. **`(אם) פניו (לכך)`** for *pānūšuma* (o i 101, 103): literal with additions; alternative `אם ירצה` (but `רצה` = *magāru*, in the same law).
8. **`פונדק`** for *aštammu*; **`סרסורית`** for *mummirtu* (or `שדכנית`); **`חסרונות`** for *bitiqtu*; **`הפקדה`** for *maškattu*.

### Lessons
- **A lemma can hide a sign:** ORACC lemmatizes `LU₂-MEŠ` in a list of food (o iv 47) as *awīlu* "men"; the sign is LU = UDU "sheep". Read the list's context (*ša akāle* "edible") before a lemma.
- **The verb of a key Torah comparison may sit in LH's Hebrew under another meaning:** *palāšu* "break through (a wall)" = `פלש` in LH is the very verb for piercing the ears in MAL §40; keeping LH's root avoided `רצע` (Exod 21:6) without inventing a third word. Look up the Akkadian lemma in LH even when the context is new.
- **"Who is the subject?" is the levirate finding:** in §30 and §33 the father or father-in-law *gives* (`יתננה`); Deut 25 has the brother *take*. Keeping the Akkadian verb and subject per line (rather than a smooth paraphrase "she shall be married to") is what makes the divergence visible.
- **A stative needs two Hebrew tenses by context:** *usbat* is `יושבת` in the protasis formula but `ישבה` with "two years" (o iv 73); record such splits so that `laws_verify.py`'s "identical normalization" net is read, not mechanically harmonized.

### Proposals for the renderings
**A. For the corpus `../renderings.md` §6.2 (and the later projects):** ***kīmū* "instead of" = `חֵלֶף`** (never `תחת`; *kīma* "like" stays `כְּ`); *meḫṣu* = `מַחַץ` (`מַחַץ כְּמַחַץ`); *mazāʾu* = `מיעך` (`למיעוך`; distinct from *niāku* `בא על` / `לביאה`); *kī daʾāne* = `בזרוע` (distinct from *emūqamma* `בכוח`); *šīm batulte* = `מחיר הבתולה` (*terḫatu* stays `מֹהַר`); *samāku* = `דחה`; *naṭû* = `הלקה` (LH; `הכה` reserved); *baqāmu* = `מרט`; *lapātu* D = `נִגַּע`; *zaqāpu* "impale" = `זקף` (LH's "plant" `נטע` is another sense); *wāšipu* = `אַשָּׁף` [cognate loan]; *ḫazannu* = `חַזָּן` [cognate loan]; *ḫupšu* = `חֻפְשׁוּ` (transliteration; `חפשי` reserved); *āmerānu* / *šāmeʾānu* = `הרואה` / `השומע`; *paṭāru* (redeem a person) = `פטר` (`גאל`, `פדה` kept out); *tūrtu* = `השבה`; *uššer* (prep.) = `חוץ מן`; *aranšu laššu* = `עון אין לו` (vs *ḫīṭu laššu* `חטא אין`). The *erēšu* ~ `ארשׂ` link (Klein) belongs in the corpus parallels notes: `ארש` stays reserved.

### Lessons
- **A run prompt's key word can be the wrong lemma** (as T2 found for *zaqānu*): §55's "give her to be raped" is *ana mazāʾe*, not T2's *ana niāke* (`לביאה`), and §59 ends with *arnu*, not *ḫīṭu*. Grep the `lem:` line before taking a suggested Hebrew word, and keep one Hebrew per Akkadian word even when the prompt suggests sharing.
- **The most important equation of the file was a preposition:** *kīmū* "instead of" (§50) is the Akkadian `תַּחַת` of Exod 21:23, while LH's talion uses *kīma* "like". A reserved Hebrew word (`תחת`) needs an explicit substitute (`חלף`) and a footnote, or the finding disappears.
- **Etymology can sit in a broken line:** r viii 9 (*erēšu*, the probable source of `ארשׂ`) – check Klein for the reserved Torah words; the cognate may be in the text under another meaning.
- **A verifier first meets a project's label format only when the translation is built:** the label regex worked for LE's numeric labels (`53`) but not for MAL's `o i 3`; build the clean translation as soon as the first file is done, not at the end.
