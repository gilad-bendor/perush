Binding above this file: the corpus `../renderings.md` (§0, §5, **§6.1–§6.2**), the Laws of Hammurabi's fixed formulas and words (`../2-חוקי-חמורבי/renderings.md` §1 F1–F43, §2, §4) and the Laws of Eshnunna's decisions (`../3-חוקי-אשנונה/renderings.md`; `project.md` Part T1). **An Akkadian formula that LH has gets LH's Hebrew letter for letter** (`../laws_verify.py MAL` checks it).
