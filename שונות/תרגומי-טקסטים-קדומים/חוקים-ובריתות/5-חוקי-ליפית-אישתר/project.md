## Scope
- **CDLI P464355 composite** (after Wilcke 2014): prologue 1–107′, laws 108′–429′ (§4–§38 in Roth's numbering, with Wilcke's lettered additions), epilogue 430′–500′, colophon 501′–503′: **503 lines**. Sumerian, Isin, c. 1930 BCE (Lipit-Ištar), known from OB school copies (13 exemplars).

## Parallels to watch (preliminary)
- Prologue: freedom (*ama-ar-gi₄*, `דרור`) for the sons and daughters of Nippur, Ur, Isin, Sumer and Akkad ↔ Lev 25:10; "made the father support his children, the children their father" ↔ Mal 3:24; justice for the land ↔ Ps 72; the king named by An and Enlil.
- Laws: §9–10 the orchard (theft, felling a tree ↔ Deut 20:19–20); §11 the neglected plot next to a house; §12–13 the fugitive slave sheltered a month ("a slave like a slave"; ↔ Deut 23:16–17; LH §15–20); §14 the slave who proves his status twice; §17 the false accuser bears the penalty ↔ Deut 19:16–19 `וַעֲשִׂיתֶם לוֹ כַּאֲשֶׁר זָמַם`; §18 taxes of an estate; §20 the child pulled from a well (↔ Exod 2:10?); §20b foster-child returned; §22 priestess daughters inherit (↔ Num 27:1–11); §24–27 children of the first wife, the second wife, the slave-woman, the prostitute (↔ Gen 16; 21:10; Deut 21:15–17; Judg 11:1–2); §28 the paralysed wife not evicted, supported (↔ Exod 21:10); §29 the son-in-law evicted and the bride given to his friend (↔ Judg 14:20; 15:2; 1 Sam 18:19); §30 the prostitute of the street; §31 the father's gift to a favoured son, sealed (↔ Gen 25:5–6; 37:3; 48:22; Deut 21:16); §32 the eldest son's bride-gift; **§33 slander of an unmarried daughter's virginity, 10 shekels ↔ Deut 22:13–19** (`מֵאָה כֶסֶף`); §34–37 the hired ox: hoof tendon, eye, horn, tail (↔ Exod 22:13–14; LH §247–248).
- Epilogue: "I made the father support…", the stele, blessings and curses on the one who erases the inscription ↔ Deut 27–28; Lev 26; LH epilogue.

### Decisions (translator)
1. **The Akkadian-equivalent rule (corpus §6.3) applied to every formula and recurring word**, with LH's Hebrew looked up (`laws_lookup.py`) before drafting: *tukum-bi* `אם`; *lu₂* `איש`, *lu₂ lu₂-ra* `איש לאיש`/`איש את איש` (= LH 304); *-bi* `ההוא` (F2); *i₃-la₂-e* `ישקול`; *ib₂-su-su* `יחליף`; *X X-gin₇* `עבד כעבד` (F7); sums as F6; *gin* "make firm" `כונן` (F20) throughout (171′, 182′, 197′, 218′, 245′, 407′); *di-ku₅* `דיין`; *ama-ar-gi₄ … ĝar* `את דרורם שָׂם`; *dumu-gi₇* `בן חורין`; *ibila* `יורש`; *e₂ ad-da* `בית האב`; *lugal* `בעל` (things) / `אדון` (slaves); *ĝeškiri₆*, *nu-ĝeškiri₆* `גן`, `גנן`; *ma₂* `ספינה`; *huŋ* `שכר`.
2. **Where the Akkadian equivalent is known, LH's Hebrew of that Akkadian word** (one Hebrew per Akkadian lemma across the corpus): *niĝ₂-ba* = *qīštu* `מענק` (LH 1957′), with the verb *ba* `העניק`; *ba* "divide" = *zâzu* `חלק`, *ḫa-la* = *zittu* `חלק`; ***dam gidlam* = *ḫīrtu* `האשה הבחירה`** (LH `בחירתו`, `בני הבחירה`; CDLI "equal-ranking"); *saĝ-rig₇* = *šeriktu* `השלוחים`; *niĝ₂-mussa* = *biblu* `שי`; *ušbar* = *emu* `חם`; *guli* = *ibru* `חבר`; *tab* `יכפילו` (LH *uštašannā* `יכפיל`); *il₂-il₂* = *našû* Gtn `תפרנס` (LH 1748′ `יְפַרְנְסֶנָּה`); ***nam-tag … il₂* = *arnam našû* `את עונו ישא` (LH F37)**; *inim la₂* = *ubburu* `האשים` (LH §1); *tak₄* = *ezēbu* `עזב`; *til* = *gamāru* `גמר`; *ĝeš gub* = *zaqāpu* `נטע`; *šub* = *nadû* `הזניח`; *bur₃* = *palāšu* `פלש`; *kalag* = *dunnunu* `חיזק`; *zaḫ₃* = *ḫalāqu* (persons) `נמלט`; *u₂-gu de₂* = *ḫalāqu* `אבד`; *tum₃* + *ba-* = *tabālu* `נטל`, *tum₂* + *mu-* = *wabālu* `הוביל`; *e₃* trans. = *šūṣû* `הוציא` (F31); *gur* = *târu* `שוב`; *bur₂* = *wuššuru* `ישוחרר` (LH); *gu₂-un* = *biltu* `מס` (LH `נושא מס`); *sa-sal* = *šašallu* `גיד פרסתו` (LH 2871′); *kud* = *nakāsu* `קצץ`; *sa₁₀* = *šīmu* `מחיר`; *ĝeš₃ zu* = *zikaram idû* `זכר ידעה` (LH §130, 1482′–1483′); *kislaḫ* `שטח זנוח` (LH's word for *nidītu* in the parallel LH §61, 1074′–1075′).
3. **Words that have no LH equivalent or that would echo a key verse got their own Hebrew** (each grepped against all LH/LE/MAL `>` lines: no collision): *saĝ₃* "strike down (a tree)" `גדע` (not `כרת`, Deut 20:19–20; not `קצץ` = *kud*); *ḫul* "damage" `פגם` (not `השחית`, Exod 21:26; not `נקר` = LH *ḫuppudu*); *sil₂* `חתך`; *šu … kar* `חילץ` (not `הציל`, Gen 37:21; Exod 2:19); *tul₂* `בור` (cognate of the Akk. *būrtu*; ↔ Gen 37:28); *mussa-tur* `חתן` (the cognate of the Akk. equivalent *ḫatanu*); *šeš-gal* `האח הגדול` (`בכור` kept for Deut 21:15–17); *igi-na sa₆-ga* `הנאה בעיניו` (`טוב` = *dug₃*/*ṭābu* only; *du₁₀* in 320′ `ייטיבו` is that very cognate); *gur* + *-da-* "contest" `ערער` (LH *paqāru* `יערערו`); *gi₄* "turn" `נהפך` (343′; `שוב` stays with *gur*); *miqtum* `מִקְתּוּ` (transliterated without mimation, as LH's *nadītu*); *zu₂-keš₂* `התקשרות` (`קשר` = *bārtu*); *kar-kid* `זונה` (gated, corpus); *tilla₂* `רחוב`; *ĝuruš* `בחור`; *iti* `ירח` (corpus *warḫu*).
4. ***ḫamṭu* / *marû*:** ḫamṭu in the protases = Hebrew past; marû in the apodoses = future; marû in a protasis (*ba-an-tuku-tuku* 313′, 348′) = future (as `אִם אַחֶרֶת יִקַּח לוֹ`, Exod 21:10). **Two ḫamṭu forms in an apodosis are kept as past and noted:** 304′ *ama-ar-gi₄-bi in-gar* `את דרורם שָׂם` (CDLI "shall bring about"; a resultative "the father has (thereby) freed them"), 399′ *in-ak-[…]* `עשה […]`.
5. **Brackets:** the composite marks few restorations; following the brief, a Hebrew word is in `[ ]` when its Sumerian root is broken in **every** exemplar, even where the composite prints it unbracketed (checked by script over all witness lines): 219′, 247′, 281′–282′, 284′, 309′, 312′, 314′–316′, 394′, 429′. Restorations from a damaged but legible root are unbracketed and named in **כתבי יד**.
6. **Identical Sumerian lines have identical Hebrew** (110′ = 121′; 143′ = 146′; 155′ = 408′; 251′ = 256′, 252′ = 257′, 258′ = 271′; 286′ = 298′ = 301′ = 327′ `בן ילדה לו,`; 382′ = 394′; 383′ = 395′; 411′ = 424′; 421′ = 426′), with two justified exceptions: *ibila-ni i₃-me-en* 315′ `יורשיו הם` / 334′ `יורשו הוא` (number by context; Sumerian unmarked) and *ad-da ti-la* (262′ with a comma, the absolute construction).
7. **Readings from the exemplars against the composite (sense-changing, in כתבי יד):** 345′ four copies `nu-ub-ta-e₃-e` ("he shall not expel her", followed) against ex08 (composite); 347′ ex08 `dam galam-na` "an able(?) wife" for "a second wife"; **324′ ex02 omits the negation** ("his wife bore him a son"); 196′ ex12 `2-kam` "a second time"; 235′ ex12 `lu₂ e₂-a` for *lugal e₂-a*; 306′ ex08 `-da` (comitative, the expected form); 149′ ex02 `ba-ni-še₃`; 150′ ex02 + *-bi* and the order of 149′/150′ reversed.
8. **CDLI's English corrected (rank 3, read after our reading):** 304′ tense (ḫamṭu, not "shall"); 404′ "You had sexual relations" – the form *i₃-zu* is 3rd person (`"זכר ידעה"`); 216′ passive "has been accused" – the Sumerian is active (*lu₂ lu₂-u₃ … in-da-la₂*); 163′ the man as agent – *al-tak₄* is a stative (`עזוב`); 303′ "her children" – *-anene* "their"; 255′ "gave it to the wife" – `לבעל? נתנה` (as §23 *dam-ra … šum₂*); 351′, 363′ fine. **CDLI's English imports no Torah vocabulary** in these laws; its "pay" for *la₂* (`ישקול`) and "eldest son" for *šeš-gal* would have pre-empted Exod 21:22 / Deut 21:15–17 in Hebrew (`ישלם`, `בכור`) and were not followed.

### Open decisions for the user
1. **`האשה הבחירה`** for *dam gidlam* (LH *ḫīrtu* `בחירה`): alternatives `האשה הראשית` (plain), `אשת חיקו` (Deut 13:7; echo), or CDLI's "equal-ranking" `שוות המעמד`.
2. **`שטח זנוח`** for *kislaḫ* (LH's word for *nidītu*, which makes LI §8 = LH §61 visible): alternatives `מגרש ריק` / `קרקע בור`.
3. **`גדע`** (*saĝ₃*, 158′) vs `כרת` (Deut 20:19–20's verb, which we keep free so the comparison stays visible) or LH's `קצץ`.
4. **`פגם`** for *ḫul* (416′, 425′) vs `השחית` (Exod 21:26 `וְשִׁחֲתָהּ` – the natural Hebrew, but it would make the hired-ox law sound like the slave's-eye law) or `הזיק`.
5. **197′ `לאדוניו את עבדותו פעמיים כונן`** – literal and ambiguous: "proved (his free status) twice" (CDLI) or "rendered his slavery twice over / his master was compensated twice" (Roth, from memory). Without Wilcke 2014 we do not decide.
6. **304′ `את דרורם שָׂם`** (past, as the Sumerian ḫamṭu) vs a future `ישים` (CDLI, Roth).
7. **343′ `*עיניה נהפכו*`** (literal; meaning unknown – illness? loss of looks?) and **344′ `ידיה צנחו`** (paralysis).
8. **`מענק`** for *niĝ₂-ba* (LH *qīštu*) rather than `מתנה` (Gen 25:6 `מַתָּנֹת`, the comparison verse).
9. **`חתן`** for *mussa-tur* (353′): the Hebrew word is the cognate of the Akkadian equivalent (*ḫatanu*), not of the Sumerian; it makes Judg 15:6 `חֲתַן הַתִּמְנִי` visible. Alternative: `בעל הבת`.
10. **`בור`** for *tul₂* (239′; Akk. *būrtu*, cognate) vs `באר`.
11. **`מקתו`** (*miqtum*, transliterated) vs a descriptive `הנופל (לחסות)`.
12. **`זכר ידעה`** (404′, 406′; LH §130's Hebrew): the Sumerian *ĝeš₃* is literally "penis"; a more literal `"איבר זכר ידעה"` was rejected as unidiomatic.
13. **The verifier edit** (Decision 9) – outside the project folder; the coordinator may prefer to revert it and widen the patterns differently.

2. **The names of the earlier corpora** (renderings §4.1): An `אַן` (always vocalized), Enlil `אֶנְלִיל` (plain `אנליל` as LH), Nunamnir, Ninisina `נִין־אִיסִינָה`, Utu `אוּתוּ` and Ur `אוּר` (always vocalized), **Inanna `אִינַאנָּה`** (so that it strips to the creation corpus's plain `אינאנה`; `אִינַנָּה` strips to `איננה` "is not"), Ninurta, **Ašnan `דָּגָן`** (creation corpus *ašnan* = `דָּגָן`; the goddess's name in the note), Šakkan `שַׁכַּן`, Ekur `אֵכּוּר`.
3. **The pair *niĝ₂-erim₂ niĝ₂-a₂-zi* = `רשע וחבלה`** (29 = 436′): *niĝ₂-erim₂* = Akk. *raggu* (LH 35 `רשע`), *niĝ₂-a₂-zi(g)* = *ḫabālu* / *ḫibiltu* (LH F39 `חבל` [cognate]). `חמס` (Gen 6:11) kept out. **Proposed for Ur-Namma** (its prologue has the same pair).
4. **The idioms with *gi₄*:** *gi₄* alone = *târu* D `השיב` (30, 458′); *ka-ge … gi₄* "contradict, reject" (ETCSL Šulgi B 274) and *inim … gi₄-gi₄* = **`דחה`** (437′ `דחיתי`, 484′ `ידחה`) – rendered by sense, because the literal `השבתי אל הפה` / `דבר ישיב` ("answer") misleads.
5. ***il₂* (73, 75) = `הִשֵּׂאתִי`** (*našû*, as file 2; the causative "I made (the father) carry (his children)"), vocalized at every occurrence (`השאתי` reads "married off"); ***gub* / *su₈* = `העמיד`** throughout (77, 79, 83, 88, 90; *ki-gub* `מקום עמידה` 462′), so that "standing (in service)" (88–90) and "standing with" (76–79) share one verb, as in the Sumerian. The father/son passage is **not** rendered with Malachi's words (`השיב לב`): the parallel is structural.
6. ***na₄* = `אבן`** (448′), not `מצבה`: the epilogue says "stone", not *na-ru₂-a* (LH *narû* `מצבה`, F40). This keeps the contact with the stones of Deut 27:2–8 visible. *ki … tag* = `לקרקע הִגַּעְתִּי` (*tag* = `נגע`, file 2).
7. **Justice words:** *niĝ₂-si-sa₂* `מישרים`, *niĝ₂-gi-na* `אמת`, *di* `דין` (corpus §6.3); the adjective *gi-na* = `אמת` in construct (`בדבר האמת`, `דין אמת`); *zi(d)* = `נאמן` (42 `האכר הנאמן`), *niĝ₂-zi* = `נאמנות` (440′ `נאמנות ואמת`; CDLI's "righteousness" not followed: `צדק` reserved).

### Open decisions for the user
1. **`רשע וחבלה`** for *niĝ₂-erim₂ niĝ₂-a₂-zi* (29, 436′): alternatives `רשע ועוול`, `רעה וחמס` (Gen 6:11 echo).

8. **`נאמנות ואמת`** (440′; *zi* = `נאמן`) vs CDLI's "righteousness and truth" (`צדק ואמת`, reserved).

### Proposals for the renderings
- For the corpus `../renderings.md` §6.3 (Sumerian; binding for Ur-Namma, whose prologue has the same phraseology): *u₄* `כאשר`, *u₄-ba* `בימים ההם`; *sipa* `רועה`; *mu pa₃* `קרוא בשם`; ***niĝ₂-erim₂ niĝ₂-a₂-zi* `רשע וחבלה`**; *i-Utu* `"הוֹי אוּתוּ!"`; *su … du₁₀* `את בשר … היטיב`; *niĝ₂-si-sa₂ … ĝar* `מישרים … שָׂם`; *pa-e₃ ak* `הופיע`; *bi₂-dab₅* `הִתְפִּישׂ`; *ka-ge gi₄*, *inim gi₄* `דחה`; *gi-na* (adj.) `אמת` (construct), *zi(d)* `נאמן`; *gub* `העמיד` / `עמד`; *il₂* `נשא`; *na₄* `אבן` vs *na-ru₂-a* `מצבה`; *mu-sar-ra* `כתובת`, *šu ur₃* `מחה`; *aš₂* `מארה` (and *aš₂ bal* verb = `ארר`); *lu₂-bi lugal ḫe₂-a …* `האיש ההוא, בין מלך, בין אדון, בין מושל`; *du₆* `תל`; *maškim* `הממונה`; *dumu-saĝ* `הבן הראשון`; *ensi₂* `מושל`; *šagina* `מושל הצבא`; *nu-banda₃* `המפקח`. ### Lessons
- **Read the epilogue against LH's epilogue line by line before drafting**: the curse protasis (erase the inscription, write one's name, "because of the curse" make another do it, "be he king, lord or governor") is the same in Sumerian and Akkadian, two centuries apart, and only a line-by-line comparison shows that `בין מלך, בין אדון, בין מושל` and `בגלל המארה` must be LH's Hebrew.
- **The Sumerian verb behind a "biblical" English paraphrase matters**: CDLI's "made the father support his children" invites Malachi's `השיב לב`; the Sumerian has *il₂* "carry" and *gub* "stand" – the parallel is the reciprocity, not the words. CDLI's "stela" for *na₄* "stone" would have hidden the link with Deut 27's stones.
- **A single witness at the line starts** (ex01, 1–96) puts many first words in brackets: decide the rule ("all signs broken") before drafting, and say where the composite prints a restoration unbracketed.
- **Plene spelling of a vocalized name must strip to the corpus's plain form**: `אִינַנָּה` strips to `איננה` ("is not"); `אִינַאנָּה` to `אינאנה`.
