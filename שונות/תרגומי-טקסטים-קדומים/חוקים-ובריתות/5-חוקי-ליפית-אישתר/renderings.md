# Renderings: the Laws of Lipit-Ištar (LI), project 5 of `חוקים-ובריתות/`

The corpus file `../renderings.md` is binding (**§5, §6.1, §6.3**: every Sumerian legal formula takes the Hebrew of its standard Akkadian equivalent, letter for letter as in the Laws of Hammurabi), together with LH's fixed formulas (`../2-חוקי-חמורבי/renderings.md` §1–§2, §4). This file holds the Sumerian words of LI with the Hebrew chosen for them. Started by T5 (run 6, 2026-10-18) for the laws (file 2, 108′–429′); T6 extends it for the prologue and epilogue.

Columns: Sumerian (ePSD2 `cf[gw]`) | Akkadian equivalent | Hebrew (plain plene, as in the `>` lines; vocalized first occurrence in brackets) | rejected | echo | lines (file 2).
